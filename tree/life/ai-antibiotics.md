Jonathan Stokes, James Collins and colleagues used a graph neural network to pick the antibiotic halicin out of a chemical library (Cell, 2020). In 2025 Aarti Krishnan and colleagues used generative models to design new compounds from scratch, NG1 against gonorrhoea and DN1 against MRSA, which reduced bacterial load in mouse infection models (Cell, 2025).
