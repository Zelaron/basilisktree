John O'Keefe and Jonathan Dostrovsky found in 1971 that some neurons in the rat hippocampus fire only when the animal is in one particular place. In 2005 May-Britt and Edvard Moser found grid cells in the neighbouring entorhinal cortex, firing in a hexagonal lattice. Together these cells form the brain's map of space, and O'Keefe and the Mosers shared the 2014 Nobel Prize.
