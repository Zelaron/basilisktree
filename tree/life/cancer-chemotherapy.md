Sidney Farber gave the folate antagonist aminopterin to children with acute leukaemia in 1948 and produced the first temporary remissions. His work followed wartime studies of nitrogen mustard by Louis Goodman and Alfred Gilman, who treated a lymphoma patient in 1942. Drug treatment of cancer began with these results.
