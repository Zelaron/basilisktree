Luigi Galvani found that frog legs twitched when touched with two metals or sparked by an electrical machine, and in 1791 published his view that nerve and muscle carry an intrinsic electricity. His dispute with Volta led to the battery, and electrical signalling was later shown to underlie nerve conduction.
