Ian Frazer and Jian Zhou made virus-like particles from the HPV capsid protein in 1991, and Douglas Lowy and John Schiller developed them as a vaccine antigen at the US National Cancer Institute, building on Harald zur Hausen's finding that HPV causes cervical cancer. Gardasil was licensed in 2006 and prevents the infections behind most cervical cancers.
