Stephen Hubbell's The Unified Neutral Theory of Biodiversity and Biogeography (2001) assumed that all individuals in a community are ecologically equivalent, so that species abundances arise from random birth, death, dispersal and speciation. It reproduces the abundance patterns of many tropical forests closely and serves as a null model for community ecology.
