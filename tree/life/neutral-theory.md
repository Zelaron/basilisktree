Motoo Kimura argued in 1968, and Jack King and Thomas Jukes in 1969, that most molecular changes between species are neither helped nor hindered by selection and spread by chance drift. The neutral theory gave molecular evolution a null model and an explanation for the steady rate of the molecular clock.
