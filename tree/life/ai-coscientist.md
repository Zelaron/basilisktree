Google's Co-Scientist (Juraj Gottweis and colleagues) and FutureHouse's Robin (Ali Ghareeb and colleagues) are multi-agent systems that generate hypotheses and plan experiments, and both were published in Nature on 19 May 2026. Co-Scientist proposed leukaemia drug-repurposing candidates validated in vitro, and Robin identified ripasudil as a candidate for dry macular degeneration.
