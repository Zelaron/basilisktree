Alan Turing showed in 1952 that two chemicals that react and diffuse at different rates can turn a uniform mixture into stable stripes or spots. The paper The Chemical Basis of Morphogenesis proposed that such reaction-diffusion patterns might shape animal markings and tissues, and experiments have since found Turing patterns in several living systems.
