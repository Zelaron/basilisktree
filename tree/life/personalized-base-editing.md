Kiran Musunuru and Rebecca Ahrens-Nicklas's teams designed a base-editing therapy, delivered in lipid nanoparticles, to correct the CPS1 mutation in KJ Muldoon, a newborn with a severe urea cycle disorder. Dosing began about six months after diagnosis in 2025, and the case appeared in the New England Journal of Medicine. It showed that a gene-editing therapy can be built for one patient.
