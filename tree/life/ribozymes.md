Thomas Cech found in 1982 that a ribosomal RNA from the ciliate Tetrahymena cuts itself out of a longer transcript with no protein help, and Sidney Altman showed in 1983 that the RNA part of RNase P is the catalyst. RNA can therefore act as an enzyme, which supports the idea that early life ran on RNA. They shared the 1989 Nobel Prize.
