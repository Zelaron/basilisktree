AlphaMissense (Jun Cheng, Žiga Avsec and colleagues at Google DeepMind, Science, September 2023) adapts AlphaFold, fine-tuned on variant frequencies in humans and primates, to predict the pathogenicity of single amino acid changes. It published predictions for all 71 million possible human missense variants and classified 89% as likely benign or likely pathogenic.
