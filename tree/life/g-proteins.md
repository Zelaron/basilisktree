Earl Sutherland found in 1957 that cyclic AMP carries the message of hormones such as adrenaline inside a cell, the first second messenger. In 1971 Martin Rodbell showed that a GTP-binding step couples the hormone receptor to the enzyme making it, and Alfred Gilman purified the G protein involved. G proteins mediate signalling for many receptors and are targeted by about a third of drugs.
