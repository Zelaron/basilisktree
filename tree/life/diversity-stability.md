Robert May proved in 1972 that a large community with random interactions is almost surely unstable once its richness, connectance and interaction strength pass a threshold, against the older view that diversity begets stability. Field experiments find diverse communities more stable. Solving it means identifying the features of real interaction networks that reconcile the two and confirming them on measured communities.
