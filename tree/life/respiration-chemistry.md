Antoine Lavoisier and Pierre-Simon Laplace placed a guinea pig in an ice calorimeter in 1783 and measured the heat it gave out and the carbon dioxide it breathed out. The ratio resembled that of burning carbon, so they concluded that respiration is a slow combustion that powers the body.
