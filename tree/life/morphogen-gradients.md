Lewis Wolpert proposed in 1969 that cells in a developing tissue learn their position from the concentration of a signalling molecule that spreads from a source, and choose a fate according to thresholds, the French flag model. Gradients of Bicoid and other morphogens later confirmed it in fly embryos.
