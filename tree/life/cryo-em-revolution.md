Direct electron detectors and better image-processing software, notably Sjors Scheres's RELION, let cryo-electron microscopy of frozen, unstained molecules reach near-atomic resolution without crystals from about 2013. The methods came from Richard Henderson, Joachim Frank and Jacques Dubochet, who shared the 2017 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. Large complexes are now routinely solved this way.
