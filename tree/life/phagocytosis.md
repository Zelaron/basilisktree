Élie Metchnikoff saw mobile cells in starfish larvae gather around a thorn pushed into the body, and in 1884 watched similar cells engulf yeast spores in water fleas. He proposed that white blood cells ingest invaders as a defence, founding cellular immunity, and shared the 1908 Nobel Prize with Paul Ehrlich.
