Claude Bernard argued, most fully in his 1865 Introduction to the Study of Experimental Medicine, that the body keeps its internal environment (milieu intérieur) constant and that this constancy makes independent life possible. Walter Cannon named the principle homeostasis in 1926 and showed how nerves and hormones maintain it.
