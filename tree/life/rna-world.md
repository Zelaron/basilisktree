Walter Gilbert coined the term RNA world in 1986 for a stage of early life in which RNA both stored information and catalysed reactions. Carl Woese, Francis Crick and Leslie Orgel had proposed the idea in the 1960s, and the discovery of ribozymes made it plausible. The hypothesis frames much research on the origin of life.
