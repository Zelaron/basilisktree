David Liu's group, with Alexis Komor and Nicole Gaudelli, fused a disabled Cas9 to a deaminase enzyme in 2016 to convert one DNA letter to another at a chosen site without cutting both strands, first C to T and in 2017 A to G. Base editors can correct many disease-causing point mutations, and the related prime editing (2019) makes small insertions and deletions.
