Evo 2, from the Arc Institute and collaborators (Garyk Brixi, Brian Hie, Patrick Hsu and colleagues; Nature, March 2026), is trained on 9 trillion DNA bases from all domains of life. It predicts variant effects, such as in BRCA1, without task-specific training and generates genome-scale mitochondrial, bacterial and eukaryotic sequences. Its code and data are open.
