Wolves and hunter-gatherers began living together, and the wolves' descendants became dogs, the first domesticated animal. The oldest undisputed dog burial, beside a human grave at Bonn-Oberkassel in Germany, is about 14,200 years old, and DNA suggests the split from wolves is older still. Dogs helped with hunting and guarding.
