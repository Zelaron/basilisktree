Randy Schekman found the yeast genes needed for secretion, James Rothman and colleagues described in 1993 the SNARE proteins that make a vesicle fuse with its target membrane, and Thomas Südhof found how calcium triggers that fusion at synapses. The machinery of vesicle traffic explains hormone release and neurotransmission. The three shared the 2013 Nobel Prize.
