Sheep, goats, pigs and cattle were domesticated in south-west Asia between about 8500 and 8000 BCE, in the same farming villages that grew wheat and barley. Herds gave meat, milk, wool, hides and manure, and later pulled ploughs and carts, and herding societies spread across Eurasia and Africa.
