W. D. Hamilton showed in 1964 that a gene for helping can spread if the cost to the helper is less than the benefit to the recipient weighted by relatedness (rb > c). The rule explains sterile worker insects, alarm calls and other altruism toward relatives, and gave evolutionary biology the idea of inclusive fitness.
