Samuel King, Brian Hie and colleagues at the Arc Institute fine-tuned the Evo genome language models to generate complete genomes of phages resembling phiX174 (Science, August 2026). Sixteen of the generated genomes gave viable viruses, and a cocktail of them overcame E. coli strains resistant to phiX174. The authors describe it as the first generative design of complete phage genomes.
