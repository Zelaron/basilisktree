The WHO launched a global malaria eradication programme in 1955 and gave it up in 1969. Malaria still caused an estimated 282 million cases and 610,000 deaths in 2024, most of them children, and the parasite is becoming partly resistant to artemisinin in Africa. Eradication means certified zero indigenous transmission worldwide, as was achieved for smallpox in 1980.
