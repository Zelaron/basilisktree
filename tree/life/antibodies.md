Emil von Behring and Shibasaburo Kitasato showed in 1890 that serum from animals immunised against diphtheria or tetanus toxin protected other animals, and Paul Ehrlich standardised the antitoxin. Serum therapy was the first effective treatment for diphtheria, and Behring received the first Nobel Prize in Medicine in 1901.
