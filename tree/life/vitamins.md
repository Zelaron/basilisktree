Christiaan Eijkman showed in the 1890s that beriberi comes from a diet of polished rice, and Frederick Gowland Hopkins found that animals need small amounts of accessory food factors. Casimir Funk coined 'vitamine' in 1912. Scurvy, beriberi, rickets and pellagra became preventable by diet.
