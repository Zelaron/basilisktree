George Smith showed in 1985 that a foreign peptide can be fused to a coat protein of filamentous phage and displayed on its surface, and Greg Winter later displayed antibodies the same way. Libraries of billions of variants can be searched for those that bind a target, a method behind drugs such as adalimumab. Smith and Winter shared the 2018 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
