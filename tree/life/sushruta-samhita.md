This Sanskrit compendium of surgery, attributed to Sushruta and in its oldest layers from the last centuries BCE, describes about 120 instruments and some 300 operations, including cataract couching and reconstruction of the nose with a skin flap. It was the central surgical text of Indian medicine for centuries.
