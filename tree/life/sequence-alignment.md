Saul Needleman and Christian Wunsch published in 1970 a dynamic programming method for finding the best global alignment of two protein sequences, and Temple Smith and Michael Waterman added local alignment in 1981. Comparing sequences is the basis of bioinformatics, used to find related genes and infer function and ancestry.
