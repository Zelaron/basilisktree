On 16 October 1846 at Massachusetts General Hospital, William Morton gave ether to a patient while John Collins Warren removed a neck tumour without pain. Crawford Long had used ether in 1842 and John Snow later worked out safe dosing of ether and chloroform. Painless surgery allowed longer and more careful operations.
