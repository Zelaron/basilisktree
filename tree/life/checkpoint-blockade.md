James Allison showed in 1996 that an antibody blocking CTLA-4, a brake on T cells, made mice reject tumours, and Tasuku Honjo's work on PD-1 led to a second class of checkpoint inhibitors. Ipilimumab, approved in 2011, was the first drug shown to extend survival in metastatic melanoma. Allison and Honjo shared the 2018 Nobel Prize.
