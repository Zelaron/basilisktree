Basal metabolic rate rises roughly as body mass to the power 3/4, as Max Kleiber reported in 1932, but whether the exponent is really 3/4, 2/3 or variable, and why, is unresolved. West, Brown and Enquist's fractal-network derivation (1997) is disputed, and mammal data curve on log-log axes. Solving it means a mechanistic theory that predicts measured exponents, their curvature and their variation across taxa without fitted constants.
