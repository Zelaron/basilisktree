AlphaGenome, from Google DeepMind (Žiga Avsec and colleagues, Nature, January 2026), takes up to one million DNA bases and predicts thousands of functional genomic tracks at single-base resolution, including gene expression, splicing, chromatin accessibility and contacts. It matched or exceeded the best external models in 25 of 26 variant effect evaluations.
