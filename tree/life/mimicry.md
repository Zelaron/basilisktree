Henry Walter Bates, after eleven years collecting in the Amazon, explained in 1862 why harmless butterflies resemble distasteful ones: predators learn to avoid the model, so lookalikes gain protection. Published soon after the Origin of Species, it was an early and persuasive example of natural selection at work.
