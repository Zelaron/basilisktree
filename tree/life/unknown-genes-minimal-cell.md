JCVI-syn3.0, the smallest genome that supports an autonomously replicating cell, has 473 genes, and 149 of them had no known biological function when it was built in 2016. Solving it means an experimentally supported function for each one. Computational annotation has closed much of the gap, with 66 assigned in 2019 and homologs found for about 93% of the genome by 2025, but those are predictions.
