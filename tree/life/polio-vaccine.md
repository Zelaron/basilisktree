Jonas Salk's inactivated poliovirus vaccine was declared safe and effective on 12 April 1955 after a field trial of about 1.8 million American children. Oral live vaccines, tested first by Hilary Koprowski and licensed in Albert Sabin's form in 1961, followed. Together they drove polio from nearly the whole world.
