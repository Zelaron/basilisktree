Alec Jeffreys at the University of Leicester saw on an X-ray film in September 1984 that variable repeated DNA sequences give each person a distinctive banding pattern. DNA profiling was used first in an immigration dispute and then to catch the murderer Colin Pitchfork in 1987, and it transformed forensic science and paternity testing.
