Karl Landsteiner found in 1901 that mixing the blood of one person with the serum of another sometimes makes it clump, and sorted human blood into the groups A, B and O (AB followed in 1902). The groups explained why transfusions had so often failed and made safe transfusion possible. He won the 1930 Nobel Prize.
