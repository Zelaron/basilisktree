In 1921 Frederick Banting and Charles Best, in J. J. R. Macleod's laboratory at Toronto, extracted insulin from dog pancreas, and James Collip purified it. In January 1922 it was given to Leonard Thompson, a 14-year-old dying of diabetes. Type 1 diabetes changed from a fatal illness to a manageable one.
