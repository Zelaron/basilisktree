The MICrONS consortium, including Andreas Tolias, Sebastian Seung and R. Clay Reid, published in Nature in April 2025 a cubic millimetre of mouse visual cortex reconstructed from electron microscopy, with more than 200,000 cells and 0.5 billion synapses, matched to calcium imaging of about 75,000 neurons. It links the wiring of a cortical circuit to what its neurons did.
