David Deamer, Daniel Branton and colleagues showed in 1996 that single DNA strands passing through a protein pore change the ionic current. Hagan Bayley engineered pores for sequencing, and Oxford Nanopore Technologies released the pocket-sized MinION to early users in 2014. Nanopores read very long DNA molecules and allow sequencing in the field.
