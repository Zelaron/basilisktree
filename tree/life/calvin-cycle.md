Melvin Calvin, Andrew Benson and James Bassham fed plants radioactive carbon dioxide and used paper chromatography to identify the compounds that formed within seconds, working out from 1946 how plants build sugar from carbon dioxide. The cycle was published in the early 1950s and won Calvin the 1961 Nobel Prize.
