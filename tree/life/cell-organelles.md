Albert Claude, Christian de Duve and George Palade spun broken cells in the ultracentrifuge and examined the fractions by electron microscopy between about 1945 and 1955. They characterised mitochondria, lysosomes, ribosomes and the endoplasmic reticulum and traced how proteins move through the secretory pathway. The three shared the 1974 Nobel Prize.
