Genentech scientists, including David Goeddel, made human insulin in E. coli from a synthetic gene in 1978, and Herbert Boyer had cofounded the company. Eli Lilly's Humulin, approved by the US FDA in October 1982, was the first licensed drug made by recombinant DNA technology and marked the start of the biotechnology drug industry.
