Georges Cuvier compared living elephants with fossil mammoth and mastodon bones in 1796 and showed they were distinct species with no living counterparts. He argued that whole species can vanish, and later that catastrophes had repeatedly changed the fauna. The idea made a deep history of life thinkable.
