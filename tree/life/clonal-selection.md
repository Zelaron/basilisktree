Frank Macfarlane Burnet, building on ideas of Niels Jerne and David Talmage, proposed in 1957 that each lymphocyte carries receptors for one antigen and that exposure selects and multiplies the cells that fit. The theory explained antibody diversity, immune memory and tolerance, and organised modern immunology.
