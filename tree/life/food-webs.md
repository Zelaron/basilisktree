Charles Elton's Animal Ecology (1927) set out food chains, food webs and the pyramid of numbers, and the idea that each species has a niche, a role in the community. Raymond Lindeman added energy flow between trophic levels in 1942, which made ecology the study of how matter and energy pass through living systems.
