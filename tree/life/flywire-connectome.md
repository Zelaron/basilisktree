The FlyWire consortium, led by Sebastian Seung and Mala Murthy at Princeton, reconstructed and proofread the wiring of a whole adult female fruit fly brain from electron microscope images: 139,255 neurons and about 50 million synapses (Nature, October 2024). It is a complete connectome of a brain with more than 100,000 neurons and a resource for linking circuit structure to behaviour.
