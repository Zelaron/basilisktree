After William Scoville removed the medial temporal lobes of Henry Molaison in 1953 to treat his epilepsy, Brenda Milner showed in 1957 that he could no longer form new memories of events, though he could still learn new motor skills. The case showed that the brain has separate memory systems and that the hippocampus is central to making lasting memories.
