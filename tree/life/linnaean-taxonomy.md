Carl Linnaeus's Systema Naturae (1735) arranged plants, animals and minerals in nested ranks, and his Species Plantarum (1753) gave each species a two-part Latin name. Binomial names gave naturalists everywhere an unambiguous label for every species, and the system is still in use.
