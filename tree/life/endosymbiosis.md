Lynn Margulis (then Lynn Sagan) argued in 1967 that mitochondria and chloroplasts descend from free-living bacteria that were engulfed by an ancestral cell and stayed. The idea was rejected for about a decade, then confirmed when the organelles' own DNA and ribosomes were found to resemble those of bacteria.
