Imatinib (Gleevec) blocks BCR-ABL, the overactive fusion kinase produced by the Philadelphia chromosome in chronic myeloid leukaemia. Brian Druker, Nicholas Lydon and Charles Sawyers led the work that showed its effect in patients, and it was approved in 2001. Most patients now live for many years on a daily pill, and it became the model for targeted cancer therapy.
