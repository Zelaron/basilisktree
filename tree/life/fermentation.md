People brewed and baked with microbes long before anyone knew they existed. Chemical residues in pottery from Jiahu in China show a fermented drink of rice, honey and fruit by about 7000 BCE, and wine, beer and leavened bread followed across the Near East. Fermentation also preserved milk and vegetables.
