Patrik Ståhl, Joakim Lundeberg and colleagues published in 2016 a method that captures messenger RNA from a tissue section on a slide of barcoded spots, so that gene expression is measured together with its position in the tissue. Spatially resolved transcriptomics was named Nature Methods' Method of the Year for 2020 and is used to map cell types in tissues, tumours and brains.
