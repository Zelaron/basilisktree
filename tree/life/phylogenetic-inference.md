Joseph Felsenstein published in 1981 an algorithm that computes the likelihood of an evolutionary tree given DNA sequences, making maximum-likelihood tree building practical, and in 1985 introduced the bootstrap to put confidence values on branches. Walter Fitch had earlier built trees by parsimony and distance methods. Statistical methods became standard in phylogenetics.
