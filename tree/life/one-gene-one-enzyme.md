George Beadle and Edward Tatum exposed the bread mould Neurospora to X-rays in 1941 and found mutants that each lacked one enzyme of a metabolic pathway. They concluded that each gene directs the making of one enzyme, which linked genetics to biochemistry and earned them the 1958 Nobel Prize.
