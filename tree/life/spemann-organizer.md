Hans Spemann and Hilde Mangold grafted the dorsal lip of one newt gastrula onto the side of another and found that the host grew a second body axis (1924). Some embryonic cells, the organizer, instruct neighbouring cells what to become. Spemann received the 1935 Nobel Prize.
