Alfred Sturtevant, then an undergraduate in Thomas Hunt Morgan's laboratory, used how often genes are separated by crossing over to place six fly genes in a line on the X chromosome (published 1913). It was the first genetic map and showed that genes are arranged linearly on chromosomes.
