Gentile Francesco Ficetola, Pierre Taberlet and colleagues showed in 2008 that DNA shed by animals into pond water can reveal which species live there, detecting the invasive American bullfrog in French ponds. Environmental DNA allows biodiversity to be surveyed from a water or soil sample without capturing any animal.
