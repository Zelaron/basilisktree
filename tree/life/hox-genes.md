Edward Lewis, Christiane Nüsslein-Volhard and Eric Wieschaus identified genes that set the body plan of the fruit fly, and in 1983-84 Walter Gehring's group found a shared DNA motif, the homeobox, in them and in vertebrate genes. The same clusters of Hox genes lay out the head-to-tail axis in flies and in humans, joining developmental and evolutionary biology.
