RFdiffusion, from David Baker's laboratory (Joseph Watson and colleagues, Nature 2023), generates new protein backbones around a chosen target site, and BindCraft (Martin Pacesa and colleagues, Nature 2025) designs binders using AlphaFold2. Both produce proteins that bind a given target, and BindCraft reports experimental success rates of 10 to 100% without high-throughput screening.
