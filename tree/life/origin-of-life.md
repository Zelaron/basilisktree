Build an unbroken chemical route from plausible prebiotic conditions to a self-replicating, evolving system, the problem Oparin posed in 1924. Every step demonstrated so far has been staged by a chemist. A solution is one set of conditions under which the whole sequence runs by itself and ends in a replicator that copies itself and evolves.
