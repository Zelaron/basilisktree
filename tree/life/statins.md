Akira Endo isolated mevastatin, a fungal inhibitor of cholesterol synthesis, from the mould Penicillium citrinum in 1976. Merck's lovastatin, approved in 1987, was the first marketed statin. Trials showed that statins cut heart attacks and strokes, and they became one of the most prescribed classes of medicine in the world.
