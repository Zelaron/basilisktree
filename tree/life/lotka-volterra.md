Alfred Lotka (1925) and Vito Volterra (1926) wrote down coupled equations for a predator and its prey that give regular oscillations in both populations. Volterra was prompted by Umberto D'Ancona's Adriatic fish catches, which showed more predators when fishing stopped in the First World War. The equations opened mathematical ecology.
