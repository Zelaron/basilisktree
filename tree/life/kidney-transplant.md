On 23 December 1954 at the Peter Bent Brigham Hospital in Boston, Joseph Murray transplanted a kidney from Ronald Herrick into his identical twin Richard, who lived for eight more years. Because the donor was a twin, no rejection occurred. The operation showed that organ transplantation could work, and Murray shared the 1990 Nobel Prize.
