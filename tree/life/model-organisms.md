In 1910 Thomas Hunt Morgan found a white-eyed fruit fly at Columbia University, and his laboratory used Drosophila to show that genes sit on chromosomes. Its quick breeding and cheap upkeep made the fly the first widely shared genetic model organism, followed by E. coli, yeast, the worm, zebrafish and the mouse.
