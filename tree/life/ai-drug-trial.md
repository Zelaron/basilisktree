Insilico Medicine's rentosertib (ISM001-055), a TNIK inhibitor whose target and molecule were both found with generative AI, was tested in a randomised, placebo-controlled phase 2a trial of 71 patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (Nature Medicine, June 2025). Forced vital capacity rose by 98 mL at the highest dose and fell by 20 mL on placebo over 12 weeks, and larger trials are needed.
