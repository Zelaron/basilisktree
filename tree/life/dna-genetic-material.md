Oswald Avery, Colin MacLeod and Maclyn McCarty showed in 1944 that the substance that transformed harmless pneumococci into a virulent type is DNA. Alfred Hershey and Martha Chase confirmed in 1952, with radioactively labelled phage, that DNA carries heredity. The result pointed biology toward the nucleic acids and led to the double helix.
