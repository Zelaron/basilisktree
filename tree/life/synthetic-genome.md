In 2010 Craig Venter, Clyde Hutchison, Daniel Gibson and colleagues chemically synthesised the 1.08 million base genome of Mycoplasma mycoides, put it into a related cell and showed that the cell ran on it. In 2016 the team cut the design down to JCVI-syn3.0, with 473 genes, the smallest genome of any self-replicating cell known.
