In the early 1880s Robert Koch's laboratory introduced solid culture media, with agar suggested by Fanny Hesse, and staining methods that let single bacterial colonies be grown and identified. Hans Christian Gram's stain followed in 1884. With these tools Koch identified the causes of tuberculosis (1882) and cholera (1883).
