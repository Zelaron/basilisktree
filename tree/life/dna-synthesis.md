Marvin Caruthers and Serge Beaucage developed phosphoramidite chemistry in 1981, which lets machines build short DNA strands one base at a time, quickly and reliably. Har Gobind Khorana had made the first synthetic gene in 1970. Automated oligonucleotide synthesis supplies primers for PCR and sequencing and underlies gene synthesis.
