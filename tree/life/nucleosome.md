Roger Kornberg proposed in 1974, from X-ray and biochemical data, that chromatin is built from repeating units of about 200 base pairs of DNA wound around a core of eight histones. Ada and Donald Olins saw such beads on a string with the electron microscope the same year. How tightly DNA is wrapped, and its chemical marks, controls which genes can be read.
