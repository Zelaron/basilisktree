Seiji Ogawa showed in 1990 that blood oxygen level changes the MRI signal, and in 1992 Ogawa's group, Kenneth Kwong's group and others used it to map activity in the human visual cortex, as John Belliveau had done with a contrast agent in 1991. Functional MRI turned human cognitive neuroscience into an imaging science.
