Amit Varma and Bernhard Palsson showed in 1994 that a cell's metabolic network, written as a matrix of reactions with a growth objective, can be solved by linear programming to predict growth rates and by-products in E. coli. Flux balance analysis predicts the effect of gene deletions without kinetic parameters and became the basis of genome-scale metabolic models.
