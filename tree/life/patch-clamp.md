Erwin Neher and Bert Sakmann pressed a fine glass pipette against a cell membrane and recorded the tiny current through a single ion channel in 1976. Improved sealing in 1980-81 gave the gigaohm seal and whole-cell recording. Patch clamping made individual channels measurable and won them the 1991 Nobel Prize.
