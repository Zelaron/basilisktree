In 2014 Ophir Shalem, Neville Sanjana and Feng Zhang, and independently Tim Wang and colleagues in David Sabatini's and Eric Lander's laboratories, knocked out essentially every human gene with pooled libraries of guide RNAs and sequenced the surviving cells to see which genes matter for a trait. Genome-wide CRISPR screens became the main route from phenotype to gene.
