Svante Pääbo, Richard Green and colleagues published a draft Neanderthal genome in 2010, from bones about 40,000 years old, and found that people outside Africa carry 1 to 2% Neanderthal DNA, evidence of interbreeding. The same year Pääbo's group reported the Denisovans, from DNA in a finger bone found in a Siberian cave. Pääbo received the 2022 Nobel Prize.
