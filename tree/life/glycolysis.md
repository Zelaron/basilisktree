Gustav Embden, Otto Meyerhof and Jakub Parnas and their co-workers pieced together the ten steps by which a cell converts glucose to pyruvate and gains ATP, a pathway complete by about 1940. It is shared by almost all living cells and is a central route of energy metabolism.
