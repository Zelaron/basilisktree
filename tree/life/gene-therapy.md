On 14 September 1990 at the US National Institutes of Health, W. French Anderson, Michael Blaese and Kenneth Culver gave four-year-old Ashanthi DeSilva her own T cells carrying a working ADA gene, as a treatment for a severe immune deficiency. Setbacks followed in 1999 and 2002, and gene therapies were approved in Europe from 2012 and in the United States from 2017.
