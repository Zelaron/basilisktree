Alfred Russel Wallace's The Geographical Distribution of Animals (1876) divided the world into six zoogeographic regions and explained their faunas by evolution and past land connections. It built on Humboldt's maps of plant zones and on Wallace's own line through Indonesia between Asian and Australian animals.
