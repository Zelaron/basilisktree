Toshiro Sato and Hans Clevers grew single Lgr5-positive intestinal stem cells into self-organising mini-guts in a dish in 2009, and Yoshiki Sasai's group had grown layered cortical tissue from embryonic stem cells in 2008. Organoids of brain, kidney, liver and tumour tissue followed and are used to model development and disease and to test drugs.
