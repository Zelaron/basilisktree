George Shull and Edward East showed around 1908 that crossing two inbred maize lines produces plants that outyield either parent, an effect called hybrid vigour. Hybrid seed spread through the US Corn Belt from the 1930s and, with fertiliser, helped more than double American maize yields by 1960. It also created the commercial seed industry.
