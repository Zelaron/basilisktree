William Kermack and Anderson McKendrick published equations in 1927 for how a population moves from susceptible to infected to recovered. They showed that an epidemic takes off only when susceptibles exceed a threshold, and that it ends before everyone is infected. Later work on the reproduction number and herd immunity builds on it.
