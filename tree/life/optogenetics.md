Georg Nagel and Peter Hegemann identified channelrhodopsin, a light-gated ion channel from algae, in 2002-03. In 2005 Karl Deisseroth and Edward Boyden expressed it in neurons, so that pulses of light switch them on within milliseconds, and the method was soon used in living animals. Deisseroth, Hegemann and Nagel received the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.
