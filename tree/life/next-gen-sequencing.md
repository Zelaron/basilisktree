Machines that read millions of short DNA fragments in parallel began with the 454 sequencer of Jonathan Rothberg's group (2005) and the Solexa method of Shankar Balasubramanian and David Klenerman, later Illumina's. The cost of sequencing a human genome fell from about $10 million in 2007 to about $1,000 by 2016, and genomics became a routine tool of biology and medicine.
