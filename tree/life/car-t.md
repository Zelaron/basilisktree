Zelig Eshhar designed the first chimeric antigen receptors around 1989, and Michel Sadelain and Carl June developed them into engineered T cells that attack cancer cells carrying a chosen antigen. In 2011 June's team reported remissions in patients with chronic lymphocytic leukaemia. The first CAR-T therapies were approved in 2017 for leukaemias that had resisted other treatments.
