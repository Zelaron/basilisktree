Howard Temin with Satoshi Mizutani, and David Baltimore independently, found in 1970 that RNA tumour viruses carry an enzyme that copies RNA into DNA. The discovery showed that genetic information can flow from RNA back to DNA, gave molecular biology the cDNA technique and made possible drugs against HIV. Temin and Baltimore shared the 1975 Nobel Prize.
