Mark Schena, Patrick Brown and colleagues printed cDNAs in a grid on a glass slide and measured the activity of many genes at once (1995). By 1997 Brown's group with David Botstein followed the expression of nearly every yeast gene on one array. Microarrays made expression profiles a basic way to compare tissues, diseases and conditions.
