No vaccine protects against HIV, more than forty years after one was promised within two years in 1984. Only the Thai RV144 trial has ever shown efficacy, 31.2% in 2009. Solving it means a licensed vaccine with durable efficacy in people; the leading route primes the rare B cells that can mature into broadly neutralizing antibodies, which now works in macaques but has not been tested for efficacy.
