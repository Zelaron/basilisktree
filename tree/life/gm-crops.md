Crops carrying inserted genes reached the market with the Flavr Savr tomato in 1994, and large-scale planting began in 1996 with herbicide-tolerant soybean and insect-resistant Bt maize and cotton. Plant transformation with Agrobacterium was first achieved in 1983. GM crops now cover nearly 200 million hectares, and their use remains contested.
