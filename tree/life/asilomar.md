In July 1974 Paul Berg and other leading molecular biologists called for a pause in certain recombinant DNA experiments, and in February 1975 about 140 scientists, lawyers and doctors met at Asilomar, California, to agree safeguards. The meeting led to the NIH biosafety guidelines and is often cited as a model of scientists regulating their own research.
