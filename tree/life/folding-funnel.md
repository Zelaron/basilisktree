Joseph Bryngelson and Peter Wolynes proposed in 1987, with later work by José Onuchic and others, that a protein folds by sliding down a funnel-shaped energy landscape in which many routes lead to the native state. This resolved Levinthal's paradox, the puzzle that a random search of all conformations would take far too long.
