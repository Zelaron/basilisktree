Rachel Carson's Silent Spring (1962) documented how DDT and other pesticides moved up food chains, thinned the eggshells of raptors and poisoned wildlife. The book drew public attention to the ecological side effects of chemicals, helped lead to the US ban on DDT in 1972, and is widely credited with launching the environmental movement.
