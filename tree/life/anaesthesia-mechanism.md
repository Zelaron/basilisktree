How do chemically unrelated drugs, from ether and xenon to propofol and ketamine, all switch off consciousness? The first theory came in 1847, a year after ether was demonstrated in public, and many molecular targets are now known. Solving it means a chain from targets through brain circuits that predicts each agent's potency and effects; in 2026 three drug classes were found to destabilize cortical dynamics in the same way in monkeys.
