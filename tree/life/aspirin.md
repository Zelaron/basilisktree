Felix Hoffmann and Arthur Eichengrün at Bayer made pure, well-tolerated acetylsalicylic acid in 1897 from salicylic acid, the pain-relieving compound derived from willow bark. Marketed from 1899, aspirin became the first mass-market synthetic drug. John Vane showed in 1971 that it works by blocking prostaglandin synthesis.
