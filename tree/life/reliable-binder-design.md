Design a protein that binds a named target at nanomolar affinity on the first try, most of the time. Adaptyv's open competitions test every entry in one laboratory and publish the misses: the first EGFR round (2024) had 2.5% of 201 designs bind, best 491 nM, and the Nipah round (January 2026) 9.3% of about 1,200, best 0.37 nM. Rates per round are posted at proteinbase.com/competitions.
