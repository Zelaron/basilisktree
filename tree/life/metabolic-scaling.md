Geoffrey West, James Brown and Brian Enquist proposed in 1997 that the branching, space-filling networks that supply blood or sap to tissues explain why metabolic rate scales with body mass to the power three-quarters, as Max Kleiber had found in 1932. The model is influential and its assumptions and exact exponent are still debated.
