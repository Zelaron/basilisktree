Oscar Hertwig first saw meiosis in sea urchin eggs in 1876, and in 1883 Edouard Van Beneden showed in roundworm eggs that gametes carry half the usual number of chromosomes. By 1890 August Weismann had explained why two divisions are needed and Theodor Boveri had traced its course. Meiosis keeps chromosome number constant across generations and mixes parental chromosomes.
