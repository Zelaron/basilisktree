The United States set itself a national goal in 2012 of preventing and effectively treating Alzheimer's disease by 2025. Two antibodies now clear amyloid and slow decline slightly, and a 2026 Cochrane review of 17 trials called the cognitive benefit trivial against a tenfold rise in brain swelling. A solution is a treatment that halts or reverses decline rather than delaying it by months.
