David Hubel and Torsten Wiesel recorded from single neurons in cat visual cortex from 1959 and found that cells respond to bars of light at a particular orientation, with simple cells feeding complex ones in stages. Their hierarchy of feature detectors inspired convolutional neural networks, and they shared the 1981 Nobel Prize.
