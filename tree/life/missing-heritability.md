Twin and family studies long gave higher heritability estimates than genome scans could explain. Pierrick Wainschtein, Loic Yengo, Peter Visscher and colleagues analysed whole genomes of 347,630 UK Biobank participants and found that sequencing captured about 88% of pedigree-based heritability across 34 traits (Nature, November 2025). For 15 traits no significant gap remained.
