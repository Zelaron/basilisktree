Jan Ingenhousz showed in 1779 that plants give off oxygen only in sunlight and only from their green parts, building on Joseph Priestley's finding that plants restore air spoiled by burning. It revealed that life on land runs on sunlight, and later work identified carbon dioxide as the raw material.
