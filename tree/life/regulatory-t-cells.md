Shimon Sakaguchi showed in 1995 that a subset of T cells carrying the surface protein CD25 prevents autoimmune disease in mice by suppressing other T cells. Mary Brunkow and Fred Ramsdell found in 2001 that mutations in the gene Foxp3 cause fatal autoimmunity, and Foxp3 was later shown to define regulatory T cells. The three shared the 2025 Nobel Prize.
