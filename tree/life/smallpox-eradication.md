The World Health Organization's intensified campaign from 1967, led by Donald Henderson and built on a proposal by Viktor Zhdanov, used surveillance and ring vaccination of contacts to end transmission. The last natural case occurred in Somalia in 1977, and the WHO declared smallpox eradicated on 8 May 1980, the first human disease to be eradicated.
