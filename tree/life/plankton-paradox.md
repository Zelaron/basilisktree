G. Evelyn Hutchinson asked in 1961 how dozens of phytoplankton species coexist in well-mixed water while competing for a few nutrients and light, when competition theory allows no more species at equilibrium than limiting resources. Chaos, fluctuating environments, grazing, viruses and niche partitioning each suffice in models. Solving it means measuring which of these actually maintains the diversity of a real community, and by how much.
