Ronald Vale, Thomas Reese and Michael Sheetz identified kinesin in 1985, a motor protein that walks along microtubules carrying cargo through the cell. Together with myosin and dynein, motors showed that cells contain molecular machines that move, and that the cytoskeleton is a set of tracks for transport.
