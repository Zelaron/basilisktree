The Human Cell Atlas, launched in 2016 by Aviv Regev and Sarah Teichmann, is an international effort to define every human cell type by its molecular profile using single-cell methods. By 2024 it had mapped about 62 million cells, and a November 2024 collection of Nature papers presented a step toward a first draft atlas.
