Hans Krebs proposed in 1937 that cells oxidise the fragments of food molecules through a closed cycle of reactions that starts and ends with the same compound and releases carbon dioxide and energy-rich electrons. This citric acid cycle is the central pathway of energy metabolism in aerobic life. Krebs shared the 1953 Nobel Prize with Fritz Lipmann.
