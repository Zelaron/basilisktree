Every eukaryote descends from one merger of an archaeal host with the bacterium that became the mitochondrion. Since Lake's eocyte proposal of 1984 the host has been traced to the Asgard archaea, but its exact sister lineage is disputed and the order in which the nucleus, mitochondrion and endomembranes arrived is unknown. A solution is a stable placement plus a step-by-step account.
