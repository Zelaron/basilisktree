On 7 January 2022 surgeons at the University of Maryland, led by Bartley Griffith and Muhammad Mohiuddin, transplanted a pig heart with ten gene edits into David Bennett, who survived for two months. Gene-edited pig kidneys were transplanted into living patients from 2024, with eGenesis and United Therapeutics supplying the animals. Pig organs may relieve the shortage of donor organs.
