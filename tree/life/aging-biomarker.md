No measure of biological ageing is accepted as a surrogate endpoint, so a trial of an anti-ageing treatment has to wait for deaths or disease. Solving it means a marker whose change under an intervention predicts the change in clinical outcomes. The Biomarkers of Aging Challenge, reported in June 2026, scores age error in years and mortality C-index; the best were 2.45 years and 0.844.
