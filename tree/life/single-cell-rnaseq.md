Fuchou Tang, Azim Surani and colleagues sequenced the full set of RNA transcripts from a single mouse cell in 2009. Droplet methods such as Drop-seq, developed by Evan Macosko, Steven McCarroll and colleagues, profiled tens of thousands of cells in a single run in 2015. Cell types are now defined by their patterns of gene expression.
