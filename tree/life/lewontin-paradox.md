Neutral theory says genetic diversity should grow in proportion to population size, yet across animals diversity spans only two to three orders of magnitude while census sizes span many more. Richard Lewontin pointed out the mismatch in 1974. Solving it means a quantitative model of demography, linked selection and measurement bias that predicts each species' diversity from its census size on taxa it was not fitted to.
