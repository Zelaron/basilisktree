In 1983 Françoise Barré-Sinoussi and Luc Montagnier at the Pasteur Institute isolated a retrovirus from a patient with swollen lymph nodes, and Robert Gallo's laboratory in 1984 showed that such a virus causes AIDS. A blood test followed in 1985. Barré-Sinoussi and Montagnier shared the 2008 Nobel Prize.
