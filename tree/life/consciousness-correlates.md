Which brain activity is minimally sufficient for a specific conscious experience, as distinct from the processing before it or the report after it? Francis Crick and Christof Koch launched the modern search in 1990. Solving it means a theory whose preregistered predictions survive adversarial tests; the first such test (Cogitate, Nature 2025, 256 people) contradicted key claims of both integrated information and global workspace theory.
