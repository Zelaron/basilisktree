Stephen Altschul, Warren Gish, Webb Miller, Eugene Myers and David Lipman published BLAST in 1990, a fast heuristic that finds similar stretches in a sequence database and scores them statistically. It made it routine to ask what a newly sequenced gene resembles and became one of the most cited papers in biology.
