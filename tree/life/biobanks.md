UK Biobank, led by Rory Collins, recruited about 500,000 volunteers aged 40 to 69 between 2006 and 2010 and has linked their genomes, scans, blood measurements and health records. Its data are open to approved researchers worldwide, and it set the standard for population biobanks used to study how genes and environment cause disease.
