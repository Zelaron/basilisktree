RFantibody, from David Baker's laboratory (Nathaniel Bennett, Joseph Watson and colleagues, Nature, November 2025), uses a fine-tuned RFdiffusion network and yeast display to generate single-domain antibodies and scFvs against chosen epitopes. Cryo-EM confirmed designed binding poses for influenza haemagglutinin and C. difficile toxin B, and affinity maturation reached nanomolar binding.
