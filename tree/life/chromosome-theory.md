Walter Sutton and Theodor Boveri noted independently in 1902 that chromosomes pair and separate in meiosis exactly as Mendel's hereditary factors do. They concluded that the factors are carried on chromosomes, which gave heredity a physical location and led to the fly work of Thomas Hunt Morgan.
