Ian Wilmut, Keith Campbell and colleagues at the Roslin Institute created Dolly, born on 5 July 1996, by transferring the nucleus of a cell from an adult sheep's udder into an egg whose own nucleus had been removed (announced 1997). It showed that the nucleus of a specialised adult cell can be reprogrammed to produce a whole animal.
