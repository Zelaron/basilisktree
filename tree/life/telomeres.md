Elizabeth Blackburn and Carol Greider discovered telomerase in 1984-85, an enzyme containing an RNA template that rebuilds the repeated DNA sequences capping chromosome ends, and Jack Szostak showed that the caps protect chromosomes. Telomere shortening limits how often normal cells can divide, and telomerase is active in most cancers. The three shared the 2009 Nobel Prize.
