Alexander Rives and colleagues at Meta AI trained a transformer on 250 million protein sequences (preprint 2019, PNAS 2021) and found that information about structure and function emerges in its internal representations. Such models predict the effects of mutations, and ESMFold (2022) predicts structure from a single sequence. They became a basis for protein design.
