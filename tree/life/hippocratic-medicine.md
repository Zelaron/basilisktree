The Hippocratic Corpus, about sixty texts written around 400 BCE by Hippocrates and his followers, attributed disease to natural causes such as diet, climate and the balance of the humours. It set out methods of careful bedside observation and prognosis and an ethic for physicians that lives on in the Hippocratic Oath.
