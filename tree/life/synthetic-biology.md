In 2000 Michael Elowitz and Stanislas Leibler built the repressilator, a ring of three repressor genes that makes E. coli glow in oscillations, and Timothy Gardner, Charles Cantor and James Collins built a genetic toggle switch, a memory element. Designing gene circuits from standard parts started synthetic biology as an engineering discipline.
