Louise Brown, the first baby conceived by in vitro fertilisation, was born in Oldham on 25 July 1978 after years of work by the physiologist Robert Edwards, the gynaecologist Patrick Steptoe and the nurse and embryologist Jean Purdy. More than twelve million people have since been born through IVF. Edwards received the 2010 Nobel Prize.
