Rinderpest, a viral disease of cattle that caused famines for thousands of years, was declared eradicated in June 2011, the second disease ever eradicated and the first in animals. Walter Plowright's tissue-culture vaccine, mass vaccination and surveillance coordinated by the FAO and partners made it possible.
