A CRISPR gene drive cuts the matching site on the other chromosome and copies itself there, so nearly all offspring inherit it and it can spread through a wild population against selection. Kevin Esvelt and George Church proposed the idea in 2014, and Valentino Gantz and Ethan Bier demonstrated it in fruit flies in 2015. No gene drive had been released into the wild by 2025.
