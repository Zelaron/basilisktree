Japanese researchers including Tomoichiro Akiba and Kunitaro Ochiai reported in 1959 and 1960 that resistance to several antibiotics passed together from one species of gut bacteria to another, carried on transferable plasmids. Bacteria exchange genes sideways as well as by descent, which explains how antibiotic resistance spreads so quickly.
