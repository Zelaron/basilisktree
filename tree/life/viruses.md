Dmitri Ivanovsky showed in 1892 that sap from diseased tobacco plants stayed infectious after passing through a filter fine enough to hold back all bacteria. In 1898 Martinus Beijerinck concluded that the agent was a contagious living fluid, and the field of virology grew from these experiments.
