Rolf Zinkernagel and Peter Doherty showed in 1974 that killer T cells recognise virus-infected cells only when the viral fragment is presented on the body's own MHC molecules. The same molecules provoke rejection of transplants. The discovery explained how T cells distinguish self from non-self, and won the 1996 Nobel Prize.
