Inspired by Pasteur's work on microbes, Joseph Lister used carbolic acid on wounds, dressings and instruments in Glasgow from 1865 and published his method in 1867. Deaths after amputation in his wards fell from 46% to 15%. Antisepsis led on to aseptic surgery, in which everything that touches the wound is sterile.
