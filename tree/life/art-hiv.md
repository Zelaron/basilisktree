Trials reported at the 1996 International AIDS Conference in Vancouver showed that three drugs taken together, including a protease inhibitor, could suppress HIV to undetectable levels, as David Ho's work on viral dynamics had suggested. Combination antiretroviral therapy then cut US AIDS deaths by about two thirds between 1995 and 1998 and made HIV a chronic, treatable condition.
