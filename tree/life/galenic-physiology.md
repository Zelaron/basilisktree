Galen of Pergamon, physician to gladiators and emperors, combined animal dissection and experiments on nerves with the humoral theory to build a complete system of anatomy and physiology (about 170 CE). It dominated European and Islamic medicine for some 1,400 years, until Vesalius and Harvey overturned much of it.
