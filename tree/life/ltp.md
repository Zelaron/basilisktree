Timothy Bliss and Terje Lømo reported in 1973 that a brief high-frequency burst of stimulation strengthens synapses in the rabbit hippocampus for hours or weeks. Long-term potentiation is the best-studied cellular model of learning and memory and a physical realisation of Hebb's rule.
