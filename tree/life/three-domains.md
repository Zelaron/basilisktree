Carl Woese and George Fox compared ribosomal RNA sequences and announced in 1977 that the prokaryotes include two groups as different from each other as either is from eukaryotes. The archaea were recognised as a third domain of life alongside bacteria and eukaryotes, and Woese formalised the three domains in 1990.
