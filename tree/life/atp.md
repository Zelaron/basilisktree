Fritz Lipmann proposed in 1941 that cells carry energy in the phosphate bonds of ATP, which is made in energy-yielding reactions and spent in energy-demanding ones. Karl Lohmann had isolated ATP in 1929. ATP came to be recognised as the universal energy currency of life.
