Russell Higuchi, Allan Wilson and colleagues recovered DNA from a 140-year-old museum skin of the extinct quagga in 1984, and Svante Pääbo extracted DNA from an Egyptian mummy in 1985. PCR then made the study of old DNA practical, and the field grew to whole genomes from fossils tens of thousands of years old.
