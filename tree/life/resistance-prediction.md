Sequence a pathogen and say which drugs will still work, accurately enough to replace culturing it. For tuberculosis, sequencing predicted isoniazid and rifampicin resistance with 97.1% and 97.5% sensitivity in 2018, but across ten drugs the best genotypic tool reached AUC 92.1% on 36,385 isolates in 2025, and bedaquiline has too few known resistance mutations to call.
