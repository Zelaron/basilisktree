Mario Capecchi, Oliver Smithies and Martin Evans developed methods in the 1980s to replace a chosen gene in mouse embryonic stem cells by homologous recombination, and the first knockout mice were reported in 1989. Deleting a gene on purpose became the standard way to test its function, and they shared the 2007 Nobel Prize.
