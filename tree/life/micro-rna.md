Victor Ambros and Rosalind Lee, with Gary Ruvkun's group, showed in 1993 that lin-4, a gene of the nematode worm, makes a tiny RNA about 22 nucleotides long that silences another gene, lin-14, by pairing with its messenger RNA. MicroRNAs turned out to be a large class that tunes much of the genome in animals and plants. Ambros and Ruvkun won the 2024 Nobel Prize.
