The standard genetic code assigns 64 codons to 20 amino acids, and it limits the damage from mutation and mistranslation better than all but about one random alternative in a million (Freeland and Hurst, 1998). Crick called it a frozen accident in 1968. Solving it means showing which of selection, coevolution with amino acid biosynthesis and direct chemical affinity shaped it, and in what order it was built.
