Rebecca Cann, Mark Stoneking and Allan Wilson compared mitochondrial DNA from 147 people worldwide and reported in 1987 that all lineages trace back to one maternal ancestor who lived in Africa roughly 200,000 years ago. She was one of many women alive then, and hers is the only maternal line that survives in everyone. The result supported a recent African origin of modern humans.
