Paul Ehrlich and Sahachiro Hata found in 1909 that compound 606, an arsenic compound later named arsphenamine, cleared syphilis spirochaetes from infected rabbits, and it was sold as Salvarsan from 1910. It was the first synthetic drug to treat an infection effectively, and Ehrlich coined the word chemotherapy.
