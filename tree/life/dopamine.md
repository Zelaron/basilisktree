Arvid Carlsson showed in 1957-58 that dopamine is a neurotransmitter in its own right, found mainly in the basal ganglia. Oleh Hornykiewicz found in 1960 that it is depleted in the brains of people with Parkinson's disease, and L-DOPA, which the brain converts to dopamine, then relieved their rigidity and slowness. Carlsson shared the 2000 Nobel Prize.
