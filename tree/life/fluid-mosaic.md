S. J. Singer and Garth Nicolson proposed in 1972 that a cell membrane is a fluid sheet of lipid in which proteins float and move sideways. The model displaced the older view of a fixed protein-lipid sandwich and explained how membrane proteins can cluster, signal and be recycled.
