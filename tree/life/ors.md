Glucose in a salt solution makes the gut absorb sodium and water even during cholera. Robert Cash, David Nalin and colleagues showed this in Dhaka in 1968, and Dilip Mahalanabis used it widely among refugees in 1971. Oral rehydration therapy has saved tens of millions of lives, and The Lancet called it potentially the most important medical advance of the 20th century.
