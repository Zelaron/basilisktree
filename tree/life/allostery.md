Jacques Monod, Jeffries Wyman and Jean-Pierre Changeux proposed in 1965 that a protein built of subunits can flip between two shapes and that binding of a molecule at one site shifts the balance, changing activity at another site. The model explains haemoglobin and the feedback control of many enzymes and is a basis of drug design.
