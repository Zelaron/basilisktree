In 2025 Sarel Fleishman's group (Listov, Lipsh-Sokolik and colleagues) designed Kemp eliminases by a fully computational workflow, without mutant-library screening, reaching efficiencies above 100,000 per molar per second. David Baker's group (Anna Lauko and colleagues) used RFdiffusion to design serine hydrolases reaching 220,000. Earlier designs needed laboratory optimisation.
