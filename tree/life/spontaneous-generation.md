Louis Pasteur boiled nutrient broth in swan-necked flasks (1859-61) and showed that it stayed clear until dust carrying microbes reached it. The experiments ended the long belief that living things arise from non-living matter, and gave bacteriology and surgery the principle of keeping microbes out.
