Did sponges or comb jellies split off first from the rest of the animals? The answer decides whether nerves and muscles were invented once, twice or lost, and the two camps have traded the lead since 2008. Settling it needs a root that survives changes of model, gene sampling and outgroup; a sponge-first paper in Science was retracted in February 2026.
