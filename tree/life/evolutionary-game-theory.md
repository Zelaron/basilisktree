John Maynard Smith and George Price defined in 1973 an evolutionarily stable strategy as one that, once common in a population, cannot be displaced by any rare alternative. Applying game theory to animal conflict, they showed that ritualised fighting can be stable, and the method became a standard tool for analysing behaviour.
