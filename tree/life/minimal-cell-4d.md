Zane Thornburg, Zaida Luthey-Schulten and colleagues simulated a complete cell cycle of about 100 minutes of the genetically minimal bacterium JCVI-syn3A in space and time (Cell, March 2026), covering metabolism, gene expression, growth, chromosome replication and segregation, and division. The model recovers measured doubling time, mRNA half-lives and ribosome counts.
