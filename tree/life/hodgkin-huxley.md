Alan Hodgkin and Andrew Huxley recorded currents in the squid giant axon with a voltage clamp and in 1952 fitted four differential equations that reproduce the action potential from the opening and closing of sodium and potassium conductances. It was the first quantitative model of a neuron and won them the 1963 Nobel Prize.
