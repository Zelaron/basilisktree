Dioscorides, a Greek physician serving with Roman armies, described about 600 plants and some 1,000 drugs and their preparation in this five-volume work, written around 50 to 70 CE. It stayed the standard pharmacopoeia in Europe and the Islamic world for some 1,500 years and survives in illustrated Byzantine manuscripts.
