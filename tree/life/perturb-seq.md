Perturb-seq, published in 2016 by Atray Dixit, Britt Adamson and colleagues in the laboratories of Aviv Regev and Jonathan Weissman, pairs a CRISPR perturbation in each cell with sequencing of that cell's transcriptome. Thousands of genetic perturbations can be tested in one pooled experiment, with the effect of each on the whole expression program read out.
