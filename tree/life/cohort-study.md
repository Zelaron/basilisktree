The Framingham Heart Study, begun in 1948 under Thomas Dawber, enrolled 5,209 healthy adults in a Massachusetts town and followed them for decades. It identified high blood pressure, high cholesterol and smoking as risk factors for heart disease, and gave the term risk factor to medicine.
