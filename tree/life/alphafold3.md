AlphaFold 3, published in Nature in May 2024 by Josh Abramson, John Jumper and colleagues at Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs, uses a diffusion model to predict the joint structure of proteins with DNA, RNA, small-molecule ligands and ions. Open models such as Chai-1 and Boltz-1 reproduced much of its accuracy within months.
