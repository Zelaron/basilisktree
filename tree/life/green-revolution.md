Plant breeders led by Norman Borlaug in Mexico, with M. S. Swaminathan and others in India, developed short-strawed wheat and rice that gave high yields with fertiliser and irrigation. Adopted across Mexico, India, Pakistan and the Philippines in the 1960s, they roughly doubled cereal yields in much of Asia and Latin America. Borlaug won the 1970 Nobel Peace Prize.
