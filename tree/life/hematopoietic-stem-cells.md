James Till and Ernest McCulloch injected bone marrow cells into irradiated mice in 1961 and counted colonies of blood cells that grew in the spleen. Each colony came from a single cell that could both renew itself and give rise to several kinds of blood cell, which demonstrated the existence of stem cells.
