Peter Mitchell proposed in 1961 that respiration pumps protons across a membrane and that the resulting gradient drives the making of ATP. Biochemists had been searching for a high-energy chemical intermediate, and his theory met strong resistance before experiments confirmed it. He won the 1978 Nobel Prize.
