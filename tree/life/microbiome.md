The Human Microbiome Project, launched by the US National Institutes of Health in 2007, sequenced the microbes of the gut, mouth, skin and other body sites and published reference catalogues in 2012. Work by Jeffrey Gordon and others tied gut microbial communities to nutrition, obesity, immunity and the metabolism of drugs.
