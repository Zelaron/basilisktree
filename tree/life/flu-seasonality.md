Influenza epidemics arrive each winter in temperate countries and behave quite differently in the tropics, a pattern Hope-Simpson set out in 1981. Humidity, cold-impaired airway defences, aerosol chemistry and indoor crowding each explain part of it. A solution is a mechanistic model that predicts epidemic timing and size at temperate and tropical sites from environment and behaviour alone.
