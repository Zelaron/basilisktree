Jo Handelsman and colleagues named the approach in 1998: sequence the DNA of a whole microbial community without culturing any member. In 2004 Craig Venter's team shotgun-sequenced Sargasso Sea water and found over a million new genes, and Edward DeLong's group studied ocean microbes the same way. Metagenomics showed that most microbes have never been grown in a laboratory.
