Ancient DNA shows that every modern domestic horse descends from a population on the lower Volga-Don steppe, which spread across Eurasia from about 2200 BCE (Librado and colleagues, 2021). Horses brought fast transport, mounted warfare and chariots, and changed trade, migration and war.
