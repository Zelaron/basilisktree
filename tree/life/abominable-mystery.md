---
Flowering plants appear suddenly in the Cretaceous and diversify explosively, which Darwin called an abominable mystery in a letter of 22 July 1879. Molecular clocks place their origin well before any accepted fossil. Settling it takes a dated tree that reconciles clocks with the fossil record and identifies what drove the burst.
