On 6 May 1953 John Gibbon closed a hole in the heart of 18-year-old Cecelia Bavolek in Philadelphia while a heart-lung machine he had developed over twenty years carried her circulation. C. Walton Lillehei in Minneapolis used cross-circulation between patients and parent donors from 1954. Surgery inside the stopped heart became possible.
