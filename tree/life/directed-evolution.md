Frances Arnold applied random mutation and screening to the enzyme subtilisin to make it work in organic solvent (1993), and Willem Stemmer added DNA shuffling in 1994. Repeated rounds of mutation and screening can improve an enzyme without knowing how it works, and the approach now supplies enzymes for industry and medicine. Arnold shared the 2018 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
