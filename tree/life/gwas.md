A genome-wide association study tests hundreds of thousands of common genetic variants for association with a disease or trait across thousands of people. The Wellcome Trust Case Control Consortium's 2007 study of 14,000 cases of seven diseases and 3,000 shared controls set the template, and the method has since linked thousands of genome regions to diseases and traits.
