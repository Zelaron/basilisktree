Richard Roberts, Phillip Sharp and their colleagues found in 1977 that adenovirus genes are interrupted by stretches of DNA that are transcribed but then cut out of the messenger RNA. Genes in complex organisms are mosaics of exons and introns, and alternative splicing lets one gene make many proteins. They shared the 1993 Nobel Prize.
