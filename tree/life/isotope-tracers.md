Rudolf Schoenheimer and David Rittenberg fed animals compounds labelled with heavy hydrogen from 1935, and later heavy nitrogen, and followed the atoms through the body. They found that fats and proteins are constantly broken down and rebuilt, so that body constituents are in a dynamic state. Isotope labelling became a basic tool of biochemistry.
