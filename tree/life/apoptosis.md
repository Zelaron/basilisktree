John Kerr, Andrew Wyllie and Alastair Currie described in 1972 a distinct, orderly form of cell death that they named apoptosis. Robert Horvitz later identified the genes that carry it out in the nematode worm. Cells are built to die on cue during development and in defence against disease, and failure of apoptosis contributes to cancer.
