Karl Ziegler found in 1953 that titanium and aluminium compounds polymerise ethylene at low pressure into linear polyethylene, and Giulio Natta used related catalysts in 1954 to make stereoregular polypropylene. Ziegler-Natta catalysts gave the polyolefin industry its main plastics and won the two chemists the 1963 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
