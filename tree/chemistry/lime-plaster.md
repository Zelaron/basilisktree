Burning limestone to quicklime, slaking it with water and letting it set again makes plaster, mortar and whitewash. Lime plaster appears in Late Natufian sites of the Levant by about 10,000 BCE, before pottery, and the technology later led to Roman concrete and to the cements that followed.
