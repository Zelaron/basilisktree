William Chandler Roberts-Austen drew an iron-carbon diagram in 1897, and Hendrik Roozeboom interpreted it with Gibbs's phase rule in 1900. It maps where ferrite, austenite and cementite form at each temperature and carbon content, and turned the heat treatment of steel from craft into science.
