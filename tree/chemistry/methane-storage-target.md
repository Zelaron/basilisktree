Find a porous adsorbent that, at 25 °C, delivers 315 volumes of methane (at standard conditions) per volume of material between 65 and 5.8 bar, the target ARPA-E set in 2012 for natural-gas vehicles, or prove that none can. Screens of over 100,000 hypothetical frameworks (2014–15) found ceilings near 200. A solution is a material whose measured deliverable capacity meets the target, or a rigorous bound that rules it out.
