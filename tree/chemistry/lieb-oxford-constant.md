What is the smallest constant C for which the indirect Coulomb energy of every N-electron state is at least −C times the integral of the density to the power 4/3? Lieb and Oxford proved C ≤ 1.68 in 1981, and the bound is used to constrain density functionals such as PBE. Since 2022 it is known that 1.4442 ≤ C ≤ 1.58; a solution is the exact value, for instance a proof that the Wigner-crystal value 1.4442 is optimal.
