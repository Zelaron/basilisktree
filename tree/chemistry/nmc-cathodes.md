Tsutomu Ohzuku and Yoshinari Makimura at Osaka City University, and Jeff Dahn's group at Dalhousie University, reported layered lithium nickel-manganese-cobalt oxides in 2001 that store more charge than lithium cobalt oxide while using less cobalt. NMC cathodes, later made richer in nickel, power many long-range electric vehicles.
