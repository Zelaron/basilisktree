Does supercooled water have a second critical point, ending a line of transitions between a high-density and a low-density liquid, as Poole, Sciortino, Essmann and Stanley proposed in 1992 to explain water's anomalies? Ice forms too fast for direct tests. In March 2026 Nilsson, Kim and co-workers reported X-ray laser evidence of a critical point near 210 K and 1,000 bar; independent confirmation would settle the question.
