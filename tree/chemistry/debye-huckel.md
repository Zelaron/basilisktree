Peter Debye and Erich Hückel showed in 1923 that each ion in a dilute electrolyte is surrounded by a cloud of opposite charge that screens it. Their theory gives the activity coefficients and conductivities of dilute salt solutions, and its screening length, the Debye length, is used throughout colloid science and plasma physics.
