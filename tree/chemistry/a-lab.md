Berkeley Lab's A-Lab, built by Gerbrand Ceder's group with Nathan Szymanski, combined computed targets, literature-trained synthesis planning and robots to make 41 of 58 target compounds in 17 days (Nature, 2023). A 2024 re-analysis argued that most of the claimed products were misidentified or already known, so automated phase identification remains hard.
