When ozone forms from oxygen atoms and molecules, it becomes enriched in oxygen-17 and oxygen-18 by nearly equal amounts, although ordinary isotope effects scale with mass, as Thiemens and Heidenreich found in 1983; the signature is used to trace atmospheric and early Solar System chemistry. A solution is a quantum-dynamics calculation without fitted parameters that reproduces the measured enrichments and isotope-specific rate constants.
