Palladium catalysts join two carbon fragments into a new carbon-carbon bond: Richard Heck's alkene coupling (1968 to 1972), Ei-ichi Negishi's organozinc coupling (1977) and Akira Suzuki's organoboron coupling (1979). The reactions are among the most used in pharmaceutical and materials synthesis, and the three won the 2010 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
