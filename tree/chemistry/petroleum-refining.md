Abraham Gesner distilled kerosene from coal in 1846, and Ignacy Łukasiewicz refined it from crude oil and used it to light a hospital operating room in Lviv in 1853. Kerosene lamps replaced whale oil, and after Drake's well of 1859 refining grew into the industry that supplies fuels and chemical feedstocks.
