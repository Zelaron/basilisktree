Thomas Graham separated dissolved substances by how fast they diffuse through parchment and named colloids and dialysis in 1861. Faraday's ruby-red gold sols (1857) were an early scientific study of nanoparticles, and colloid science grew into the study of gels, emulsions and nanomaterials.
