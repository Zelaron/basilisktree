At the first international chemistry congress, held in Karlsruhe in 1860, Stanislao Cannizzaro revived Avogadro's hypothesis and showed that it gives one consistent set of atomic and molecular weights. Mendeleev and Lothar Meyer attended, and the agreed weights made the periodic table of 1869 possible.
