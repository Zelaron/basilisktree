Don Eigler and Erhard Schweizer at IBM used the tip of a scanning tunnelling microscope to drag 35 xenon atoms on a nickel surface into the letters IBM (Nature, 1990; the experiment was done in late 1989). It was the first deliberate placement of individual atoms and became an icon of nanotechnology.
