Noriaki Kamaya, Ryoji Kanno and colleagues reported in 2011 that Li10GeP2S12 conducts lithium ions as well as liquid electrolytes do, about 12 millisiemens per centimetre at room temperature. It revived research on all-solid-state batteries, which promise higher energy density and better safety than liquid-electrolyte cells.
