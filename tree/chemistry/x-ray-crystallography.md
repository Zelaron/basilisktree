---
title: X-ray Crystallography
year: 1912
requires: [x-rays, atomic-theory]
by: [Max von Laue, William Henry Bragg, William Lawrence Bragg]
links:
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-ray_crystallography
---
Crystals diffract X-rays (von Laue, 1912), and the pattern reveals where every atom sits (the Braggs, 1913). It later solved salt, penicillin, DNA and proteins.
