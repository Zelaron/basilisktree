Charles Martin Hall in Ohio and Paul Héroult in France discovered in 1886, independently, that alumina dissolved in molten cryolite can be electrolysed to aluminium. With Bayer's process for refining bauxite (1888) it still produces essentially all primary aluminium, which went from a precious metal to a commodity.
