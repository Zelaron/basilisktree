Latin treatises written under the name of Geber, now attributed to Pseudo-Geber (about 1310), give the first clear recipes for nitric acid and aqua regia, and sulfuric acid followed. Strong mineral acids could dissolve metals and minerals that nothing else could, and became chemistry's main solvents and reagents.
