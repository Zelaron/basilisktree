Marwin Segler, Mike Preuss and Mark Waller combined deep neural networks with Monte Carlo tree search to plan multistep syntheses, and chemists rated the routes as good as those from the literature (Nature, 2018). In the same year Bartosz Grzybowski's group ran in the laboratory syntheses planned by the rule-based Chematica program.
