Henry Louis Le Chatelier stated in 1884 that a system at equilibrium shifts in the direction that counteracts an imposed change in temperature, pressure or concentration. Chemists use it to predict how to push a reaction toward more product; Haber applied it to raise ammonia yields at high pressure.
