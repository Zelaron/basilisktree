Chinese ironworkers were casting iron in quantity by the 5th century BCE, about 1,500 years before Europe, using furnaces hot enough to melt it and moulds for tools, weapons and later pots. Blast furnaces are attested by the Han dynasty, and cast iron became the cheap material of ploughshares and, much later, of bridges and machines.
