Metal carbene catalysts swap the partners of carbon-carbon double bonds. Yves Chauvin proposed the mechanism in 1971, Richard Schrock made well-defined molybdenum catalysts in 1990 and Robert Grubbs made air-tolerant ruthenium catalysts in 1992 to 1995; the three shared the 2005 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. Metathesis is used for drugs, polymers and specialty chemicals.
