An engineered leaf-compost cutinase depolymerised 90 percent of PET bottle plastic into monomers in 10 hours at 72 degrees Celsius (Vincent Tournier, Alain Marty and colleagues, Nature, 2020). Hal Alper's FAST-PETase (2022), designed with machine learning, works at milder temperatures.
