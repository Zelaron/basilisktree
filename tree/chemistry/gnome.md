Google DeepMind's GNoME, a graph network trained on density functional theory data, predicted 2.2 million new crystal structures, about 380,000 of them stable (Amil Merchant, Ekin Dogus Cubuk and colleagues, Nature, 2023). Critics argued that few of the predictions are both new and useful, and the scale of the claim remains debated.
