Oxygen-evolution catalysts bind the *OH and *OOH intermediates through the same atom, so their energies rise and fall together and set a minimum overpotential of about 0.37 V (Man and colleagues; Koper, 2011), a major energy loss in water electrolysis. A solution is a catalytic site, designed with DFT and confirmed experimentally, that breaks this scaling relation and evolves oxygen stably at a clearly lower overpotential.
