Akshaya Padhi and John Goodenough showed in 1997 that olivine-structured lithium iron phosphate can reversibly store lithium and works as a battery cathode. It trades some energy density for low cost, safety and long cycle life, and with a carbon coating it now powers many electric cars, buses and grid storage systems.
