Hans Bethe showed in 1929 that the electric field of the surrounding ions splits the energy levels of a metal ion's d electrons, and John Van Vleck developed the idea for chemistry in the 1930s. Later refined as ligand field theory, it explains the colours, magnetism and spectra of transition-metal compounds.
