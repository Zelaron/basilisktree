Cotton textiles dyed with indigo about 6,200 years ago were found at Huaca Prieta in Peru. Natural dyeing depended on chemistry such as the fermentation vat for indigo and mordants like alum that fix madder and other colours, and Tyrian purple from sea snails was a luxury product of the ancient Mediterranean.
