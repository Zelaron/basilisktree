Jiankun Lyu, John Irwin, Brian Shoichet, Bryan Roth and colleagues docked 170 million make-on-demand molecules against two targets and found new potent ligands (Nature, 2019). Docking libraries of billions of molecules followed, showing that larger virtual screens can find better hits.
