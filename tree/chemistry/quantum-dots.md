Alexei Ekimov saw in 1981 that tiny semiconductor crystals in glass change colour with their size, and Louis Brus found the same in solution in 1983. Moungi Bawendi's hot-injection synthesis (1993) gave uniform nanocrystals, now used in displays and bioimaging, and the three received the 2023 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
