Sodium-ion cells pair hard-carbon anodes, developed by Stevens and Dahn in 2000, with layered-oxide or Prussian-white cathodes, and need no lithium or cobalt. CATL unveiled a commercial cell in 2021, and sodium-ion batteries have since entered Chinese cars and grid storage.
