Roy Plunkett at DuPont found in 1938 that a cylinder of tetrafluoroethylene gas had polymerised by itself into a slippery white solid. Polytetrafluoroethylene, sold as Teflon, resists heat and nearly all chemicals; it sealed uranium hexafluoride equipment in the Manhattan Project before it coated cookware and lined chemical plant.
