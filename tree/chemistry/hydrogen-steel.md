Making steel by reducing iron ore with hydrogen from renewable electricity avoids the carbon dioxide released by coke. The HYBRIT consortium of SSAB, LKAB and Vattenfall delivered the first fossil-free steel to Volvo in 2021, and larger plants are planned.
