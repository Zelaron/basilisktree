Alexandrian alchemists, above all Maria the Jewess (1st to 3rd century CE, known through Zosimos), described stills such as the three-armed tribikos and the kerotakis. Their apparatus led to the alembic and later the distillation column, the main way to separate and purify liquids, from perfumes and spirits to petroleum fractions.
