Henry Eyring, and independently Meredith Evans with Michael Polanyi, derived reaction rates in 1935 from the properties of a short-lived activated complex at the top of the energy barrier. The Eyring equation links the rate to the free energy of activation, and transition state theory remains the main framework for understanding and computing reaction rates.
