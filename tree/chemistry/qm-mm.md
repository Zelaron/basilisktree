Arieh Warshel and Michael Levitt combined quantum mechanics for the reacting atoms with classical mechanics for the surrounding protein in their 1976 study of the enzyme lysozyme. QM/MM methods made enzyme reactions computable, and Warshel, Levitt and Martin Karplus won the 2013 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for multiscale models.
