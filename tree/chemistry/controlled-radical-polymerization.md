Atom transfer radical polymerisation (Krzysztof Matyjaszewski and Mitsuo Sawamoto, 1995) and RAFT polymerisation (Ezio Rizzardo's group at CSIRO, 1998) gave ordinary radical polymerisation a living character. Chemists can now make polymers of controlled length and architecture, including block and brush polymers, with ordinary equipment under mild conditions.
