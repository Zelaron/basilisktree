Amedeo Avogadro proposed in 1811 that equal volumes of gases at the same temperature and pressure contain equal numbers of molecules, which explained Gay-Lussac's simple volume ratios of 1808. Chemists ignored the idea for about fifty years until Cannizzaro revived it in 1860; it fixes molecular weights and underlies the mole.
