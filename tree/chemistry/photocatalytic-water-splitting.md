Akira Fujishima and Kenichi Honda showed in 1972 that a titanium dioxide electrode under ultraviolet light splits water into hydrogen and oxygen. The Honda-Fujishima effect started research into solar fuels, photoelectrochemistry and photocatalysts, including self-cleaning titanium dioxide coatings.
