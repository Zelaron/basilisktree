Find a conventional, phonon-mediated superconductor that works above 77 K, the boiling point of liquid nitrogen, at ambient pressure; the record has stood at 39 K for MgB2 since 2001. Calculations in 2024 predicted that Mg2IrH6 would superconduct near 160 K, but attempts to make it have given the insulator Mg2IrH5. A solution is a material whose zero resistance and Meissner effect above 77 K at 1 atm are confirmed by independent groups.
