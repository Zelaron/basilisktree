William Knowles used a chiral rhodium phosphine catalyst in 1968 to hydrogenate an alkene so that one mirror-image form was favoured, and Monsanto made L-DOPA for Parkinson's disease this way in 1974. Noyori's BINAP catalysts and Sharpless's asymmetric epoxidation (both 1980) followed, and the three shared the 2001 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
