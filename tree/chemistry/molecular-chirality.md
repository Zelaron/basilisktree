Louis Pasteur sorted two mirror-image crystal forms of sodium ammonium tartrate by hand in 1848 and found that their solutions rotate polarised light in opposite directions. He concluded that the molecules themselves can be left- or right-handed, the starting point of stereochemistry and of chiral drug design.
