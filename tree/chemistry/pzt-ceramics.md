After barium titanate was found to be ferroelectric in the 1940s, Gen Shirane, Etsuro Sawaguchi and Bernard Jaffe showed in 1952 to 1954 that lead zirconate titanate (PZT) is a strongly piezoelectric ferroelectric ceramic. PZT became the standard material in ultrasound probes, sonar, sensors, actuators and fuel injectors.
