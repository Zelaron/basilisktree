Charles Pedersen's accidental synthesis of crown ethers in 1967, which wrap around metal ions, was followed by Jean-Marie Lehn's cryptands and Donald Cram's host-guest molecules. The three shared the 1987 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for founding what Lehn called chemistry beyond the molecule, the study of molecular recognition and self-assembly.
