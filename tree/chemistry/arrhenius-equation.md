Building on van 't Hoff's earlier relation, Svante Arrhenius showed in 1889 that reaction rates rise exponentially with temperature, k = A exp(-Ea/RT), where Ea is an activation energy barrier. It is still the first formula of chemical kinetics, used from drug shelf-life testing to combustion modelling.
