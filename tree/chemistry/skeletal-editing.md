Skeletal editing reactions delete, insert or swap single atoms in the ring skeleton of a finished molecule instead of rebuilding it from scratch. Mark Levin's nitrogen deletion of secondary amines (Nature, 2021), and work by Richmond Sarpong, Bill Morandi and others, made this a recognised strategy for modifying drug candidates late in a synthesis.
