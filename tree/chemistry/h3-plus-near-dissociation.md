Close to its dissociation limit, H3+, the simplest polyatomic molecule, with two electrons, shows a rich infrared spectrum that Carrington, Buttenshaw and Kennedy discovered in 1982 and mapped over the following decade; it remains unassigned. A solution is a first-principles calculation, including non-adiabatic effects and states just above dissociation, that reproduces and assigns the observed lines.
