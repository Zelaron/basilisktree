Adrien Côté, Omar Yaghi and colleagues made COF-1 and COF-5 in 2005, crystalline porous networks built entirely from light elements joined by strong covalent bonds. COFs are tunable and stable, and COF-999 (2024) captured carbon dioxide from open air in tests; they are studied for gas storage, catalysis and energy storage.
