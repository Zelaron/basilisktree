Hideo Hosono's group reported in 2008 that fluorine-doped LaFeAsO superconducts at 26 K, and relatives reached 55 K within months. Iron pnictides and chalcogenides were the second family of high-temperature superconductors after the cuprates, and their magnetism and electronic structure continue to inform theories of unconventional superconductivity.
