Abraham Darby fuelled a blast furnace at Coalbrookdale with coke instead of charcoal in 1709, making iron without clearing forests. Coke iron became cheap and abundant over the following decades and supplied the iron for steam engines, bridges and machines of the Industrial Revolution.
