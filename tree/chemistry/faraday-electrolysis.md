Michael Faraday showed in 1833 and 1834 that the mass deposited at an electrode is proportional to the charge passed, and that equal charges liberate equivalent amounts of different elements. With William Whewell he coined electrode, anode, cathode, ion and electrolyte. The laws underpin electroplating and industrial electrolysis.
