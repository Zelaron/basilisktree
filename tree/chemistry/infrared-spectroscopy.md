William Coblentz recorded the infrared spectra of more than a hundred compounds in 1905 and showed that chemical groups such as OH and C=O absorb at characteristic frequencies. Infrared spectra became fingerprints for identifying compounds and functional groups, and infrared spectrometers are standard equipment in chemical and forensic laboratories.
