Paul Rothemund folded a long single strand of viral DNA into smiley faces and other shapes with about 200 short staple strands (Nature, 2006), building on Nadrian Seeman's designed DNA junctions of 1982. DNA origami makes programmable nanostructures that position molecules with nanometre precision, for nanoscale devices, diagnostics and drug delivery.
