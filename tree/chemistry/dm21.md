DeepMind's DM21, a neural-network exchange-correlation functional trained partly on fractional-charge and fractional-spin data, reduced systematic errors of earlier functionals and improved thermochemistry benchmarks (Kirkpatrick, Cohen and colleagues, Science, 2021). Microsoft's Skala (2025) beat hybrid functionals on a broad chemistry benchmark at semi-local cost.
