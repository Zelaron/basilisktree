Egyptians made the first synthetic pigment, Egyptian blue (calcium copper silicate), by firing sand, lime, a copper source and an alkali at about 900 degrees Celsius; the earliest identified example dates to about 3250 BCE and use was widespread from about 2600 BCE. Its strong near-infrared glow is now used to find traces of ancient paint.
