Archer Martin and Anthony James separated volatile compounds carried by a gas through a heated column in 1952. Connected to mass spectrometers from the late 1950s, gas chromatography finds trace contaminants, drugs and flavours in complex mixtures. Martin shared the 1952 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for partition chromatography.
