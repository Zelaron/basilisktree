While trying to make quinine from coal-tar aniline, eighteen-year-old William Perkin obtained the purple dye mauveine (1856). Synthetic alizarin (1868) and indigo (1880, industrial in 1897) followed, and dye makers grew into the modern chemical and pharmaceutical industry.
