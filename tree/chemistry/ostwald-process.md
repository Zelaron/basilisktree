Wilhelm Ostwald patented in 1902 the oxidation of ammonia to nitric oxide over a platinum gauze, with the oxide then converted to nitric acid. Combined with Haber-Bosch ammonia, it makes the nitric acid for fertilizers and explosives from air and natural gas. Ostwald won the 1909 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for his work on catalysis.
