General Electric researchers, using H. Tracy Hall's belt press, made the first reproducible synthetic diamonds from graphite at about 100,000 atmospheres and 2,000 °C in December 1954; ASEA in Sweden (Baltzar von Platen) had made diamonds in 1953 without publishing. Synthetic diamond now supplies most industrial abrasives, and vapour deposition (1980s) makes gem and electronic grades.
