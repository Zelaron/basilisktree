Protonated methane, CH5+, is so floppy that its five protons scramble almost freely, and the dense high-resolution infrared spectrum recorded by White, Tang and Oka in 1999 has never been assigned line by line. A solution is a first-principles calculation of its rotation–vibration levels accurate enough to assign most observed lines and to reproduce the ground-state combination differences measured in 2015.
