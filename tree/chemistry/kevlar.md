Stephanie Kwolek at DuPont noticed in 1965 that a cloudy, thin solution of an aromatic polyamide was liquid-crystalline, and spun it into fibres about five times stronger than steel by weight. Sold as Kevlar from 1971, the fibre is used in body armour, tyres, ropes and aircraft parts.
