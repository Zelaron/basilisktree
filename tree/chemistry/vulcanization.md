Heating natural rubber with sulfur cross-links its chains, so it stays elastic instead of turning sticky in summer and brittle in winter. Charles Goodyear found the effect in 1839 and patented it in 1844; Thomas Hancock patented a similar process in Britain in 1843. Vulcanized rubber made tyres, belts, hoses and seals practical.
