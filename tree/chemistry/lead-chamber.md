John Roebuck made sulfuric acid in 1746 by burning sulfur with saltpetre in lead-lined chambers at Birmingham, far cheaper than using glass vessels, and it could be supplied by the tonne. Sulfuric acid became the first heavy chemical of industry; the contact process (patented 1831, industrial from 1875) later replaced the lead chamber.
