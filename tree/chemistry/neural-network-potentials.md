Jörg Behler and Michele Parrinello trained neural networks in 2007 to reproduce density functional theory energies and forces, describing each atom's surroundings with symmetry functions. Such machine-learned potentials give near-first-principles accuracy at far lower cost and, with Gaussian approximation potentials (2010) and equivariant networks, drive large-scale atomistic simulation.
