John Rex Whinfield and James Tennant Dickson at the Calico Printers' Association in Manchester made polyethylene terephthalate in 1941, building on Wallace Carothers's work on polyesters. As Terylene and Dacron it became a major textile fibre, and as PET it is the plastic of drinks bottles and food packaging.
