After Döbereiner's platinum lighter (1823) and similar cases, Berzelius proposed in 1835 that some substances provoke reactions by their presence alone and named the effect catalysis. Catalysts now speed up most industrial chemical processes, from fertilizer and fuel production to exhaust cleanup.
