Harold Kroto, Robert Curl and Richard Smalley vaporised graphite with a laser in 1985 and found a stable cluster of 60 carbon atoms shaped like a football, C60 or buckminsterfullerene. Wolfgang Krätschmer and Donald Huffman made it in bulk in 1990, and Kroto, Curl and Smalley shared the 1996 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
