Mikhail Tsvet separated plant pigments into coloured bands as they moved down a chalk-filled column and named the method chromatography (1903 to 1906). Martin and Synge's partition chromatography (1941; Nobel Prize 1952) made it general, and chromatography is now the standard way to separate and purify mixtures in chemistry, biology and medicine.
