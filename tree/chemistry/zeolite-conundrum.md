Why have fewer than 300 zeolite framework types been made, when computer enumerations since 2004 have produced hundreds of thousands of hypothetical frameworks with energies close to those of real ones? The puzzle was named the zeolite conundrum in 2013. A solution is a criterion or model that predicts which hypothetical frameworks can be synthesized, confirmed when ones it ranks highly are made for the first time.
