Roberto Car and Michele Parrinello introduced in 1985 a way to move atoms under forces computed from density functional theory on the fly, by treating the electrons as fictitious dynamical variables. Ab initio molecular dynamics made first-principles simulation of liquids, chemical reactions and materials possible.
