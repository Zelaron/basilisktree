A mobile robot designed by Andrew Cooper's group at Liverpool ran 688 experiments over 8 days, choosing each next experiment by Bayesian optimisation, and found a photocatalyst mixture six times more active than its starting point (Nature, 2020). It showed that a free-roaming robot can work in an ordinary laboratory and make its own decisions.
