Mikhail Eremets and Alexander Drozdov reported in 2015 that hydrogen sulfide compressed to about 150 gigapascals superconducts at 203 K, as density functional theory had predicted. Lanthanum hydride reached about 250 K in 2019, while later claims of room-temperature superconductivity in other hydrides were retracted.
