Egyptian craftsmen shaped a paste of ground quartz, lime and alkali, coloured with copper, and fired it so that a bright blue-green glaze formed on the surface, making faience beads and amulets by about 4000 BCE. It is among the first glazed ceramics and a step toward the invention of glass.
