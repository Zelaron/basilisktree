Danfeng Li, Harold Hwang and colleagues at Stanford found in 2019 that thin films of an infinite-layer nickel oxide, Nd0.8Sr0.2NiO2, superconduct at about 10 K. Bilayer La3Ni2O7 reached about 80 K under pressure (Meng Wang and colleagues, 2023), and strained thin films superconduct near 40 K at ambient pressure (2025), making nickelates a new family of high-temperature superconductors.
