William Ramsay and Lord Rayleigh isolated argon from air in 1894, after finding that nitrogen from air is denser than nitrogen made from chemicals. Ramsay then found helium on Earth (1895) and, with Morris Travers, neon, krypton and xenon (1898), adding a whole new group to the periodic table. Rayleigh and Ramsay won Nobel Prizes in 1904.
