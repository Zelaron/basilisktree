Berni Alder and Thomas Wainwright used a computer in 1957 to follow hard spheres colliding and found that they form a solid at high density. Aneesur Rahman simulated liquid argon with realistic forces in 1964, and molecular dynamics became the standard way to simulate liquids, proteins and materials atom by atom.
