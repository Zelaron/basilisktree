Make ammonia from nitrogen, water and renewable electricity near room temperature, with efficiency and durability that rival the Haber–Bosch process, which emits about 1–2% of global CO2. Lithium-mediated electrolysis reached 98% Faradaic but only 21% energy efficiency at 100 mA/cm² in 2026, and molecular catalysts need costly reductants. A solution runs for thousands of hours at industrial current density with competitive efficiency and cost.
