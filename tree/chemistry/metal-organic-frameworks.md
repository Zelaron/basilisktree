Metal ions joined by organic linkers form crystalline frameworks with large pores. Richard Robson set out the principles in 1989, Susumu Kitagawa made gas-adsorbing frameworks in 1997 and Omar Yaghi's MOF-5 (1999) showed permanent porosity; some MOFs have over 7,000 square metres of internal surface per gram. The three shared the 2025 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
