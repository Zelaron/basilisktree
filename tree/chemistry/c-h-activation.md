Robert Bergman and William Graham independently showed in 1982 that iridium complexes break the strong carbon-hydrogen bonds of alkanes, following Alexander Shilov's platinum chemistry of 1969 to 1972. Turning C-H bonds into functional groups is now a core strategy of synthesis and an aim of fuel and chemical processing.
