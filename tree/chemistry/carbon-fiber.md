Akio Shindo in Japan (1961) and William Watt, Leslie Phillips and William Johnson at the Royal Aircraft Establishment (1963) made strong, stiff carbon fibres by heating polyacrylonitrile fibres under tension. Carbon-fibre composites are light and strong enough for airliners, racing cars, wind turbine blades and sports equipment.
