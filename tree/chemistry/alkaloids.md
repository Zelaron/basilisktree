Friedrich Sertürner isolated morphine from opium around 1805, the first pure active principle extracted from a plant. Pelletier and Caventou then isolated strychnine (1818) and quinine (1820). Purifying single compounds with known doses began the shift from herbal remedies to modern drugs.
