Meta FAIR Chemistry released Open Molecules 2025 (OMol25), more than 100 million density functional theory calculations at a hybrid-functional level covering biomolecules, electrolytes and metal complexes, together with the UMA models trained on it and related datasets. It is one of the largest open datasets of its kind and a shared base for machine-learned molecular simulation.
