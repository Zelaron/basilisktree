Cato Guldberg and Peter Waage proposed in 1864 that the rate of a reaction is proportional to the concentrations of the reacting substances, and that equilibrium is reached when forward and backward rates are equal. This gave the equilibrium constant, a central quantity of solution and industrial chemistry.
