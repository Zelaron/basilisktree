Make fuels such as hydrogen or methanol directly from sunlight, water and CO2 with efficient, durable and cheap devices, as Giacomo Ciamician urged in 1912. Devices built from costly III–V semiconductors have exceeded 10% solar-to-hydrogen efficiency since 1998, while a 100 m² photocatalyst array reached 0.76% in 2021. A solution is a scalable system that stores over 10% of sunlight as fuel for years at a cost competitive with fossil fuels.
