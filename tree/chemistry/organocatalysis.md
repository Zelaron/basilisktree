Benjamin List showed that the amino acid proline catalyses asymmetric aldol reactions, and David MacMillan developed chiral imidazolidinone catalysts for Diels-Alder reactions, both in 2000. Metal-free small-molecule catalysts proved cheap, robust and versatile, and the two shared the 2021 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
