Saw-Wai Hla and Karl-Heinz Rieder used a scanning tunnelling microscope tip in 2000 to carry out every step of an Ullmann coupling on single molecules: removing iodine from iodobenzene, bringing the fragments together and joining them into biphenyl. It showed that chemical reactions can be directed one molecule at a time.
