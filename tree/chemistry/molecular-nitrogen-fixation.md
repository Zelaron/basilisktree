Dmitry Yandulov and Richard Schrock reported in 2003 a molybdenum complex that catalytically converts nitrogen gas into ammonia at room temperature and pressure with a proton source and a reductant. It was the first well-defined molecular catalyst for the reaction and a model for nitrogenase; later catalysts from the Peters and Nishibayashi groups were far more efficient.
