Jien-Wei Yeh and Brian Cantor independently reported in 2004 that alloys of five or more metals in near-equal proportions can crystallise as one simple solid solution. High-entropy alloys opened a very large composition space, now searched with computation and machine learning for materials with high strength at extreme temperatures.
