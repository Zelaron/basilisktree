Andre Geim and Konstantin Novoselov peeled single-atom-thick layers of carbon from graphite with adhesive tape in 2004 and measured their remarkable electronic properties, work that won the 2010 Nobel Prize in Physics. Graphene started the study of two-dimensional materials, which includes MXenes (2011) and transition-metal dichalcogenide monolayers.
