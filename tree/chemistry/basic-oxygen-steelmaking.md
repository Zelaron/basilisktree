At Linz and Donawitz in Austria in 1952, a process developed by Robert Durrer and colleagues blew pure oxygen onto molten iron in a converter, refining a heat of steel in under an hour. The basic oxygen furnace replaced the open hearth, cut costs, and now produces about 70 percent of the world's steel.
