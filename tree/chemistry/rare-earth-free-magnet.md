Make a permanent magnet from abundant elements, without rare earths, that rivals neodymium–iron–boron magnets, whose best grades store over 400 kJ/m³ and which drive electric motors and wind turbines; the 2011 rare-earth price spike made this urgent. Iron nitride and tetrataenite (FeNi), cast in bulk in 2023, lead the candidates. A solution matches NdFeB's energy product and keeps its coercivity at motor temperatures.
