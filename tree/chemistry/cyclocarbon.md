Katharina Kaiser, Leo Gross and colleagues at IBM Zurich, with Harry Anderson's group at Oxford, made a ring of 18 carbon atoms, cyclo[18]carbon, on a salt surface by removing carbon monoxide groups from a precursor with the tip of a scanning probe microscope (Science, 2019). Atomic force microscopy showed alternating triple and single bonds.
