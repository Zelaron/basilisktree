Why do ions change protein solubility, surface tension and colloid stability in the consistent order Franz Hofmeister reported in 1888, when classical electrostatics treats ions of equal charge alike? Hydration, dispersion forces and ion–surface binding have all been invoked, but a 2022 perspective found no general predictive theory. A solution predicts the size and order of specific-ion effects, including reversals, for new ions and surfaces.
