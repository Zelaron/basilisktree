Akihiro Kojima, Tsutomu Miyasaka and colleagues used lead-halide perovskites as light absorbers in dye-sensitised solar cells in 2009 and obtained 3.8 percent efficiency. Work by Henry Snaith, Nam-Gyu Park and others in 2012 raised it to about 10 percent in solid-state cells, and single-junction cells now exceed 26 percent, with higher figures when stacked on silicon.
