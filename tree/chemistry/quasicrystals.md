Dan Shechtman saw ten-fold symmetry in the electron diffraction of a rapidly cooled aluminium-manganese alloy in 1982 and published it in 1984. The pattern was ordered but not periodic, which forced crystallographers to redefine what a crystal is; he won the 2011 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
