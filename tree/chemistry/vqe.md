Alberto Peruzzo, Jarrod McClean, Alán Aspuru-Guzik and colleagues used a photonic quantum chip in 2014 to estimate the ground-state energy of the HeH+ molecule, with a classical computer tuning the quantum circuit's parameters. The variational quantum eigensolver became the template for chemistry on noisy quantum computers.
