William Grove combined hydrogen and oxygen over platinum electrodes to produce an electric current in 1839, after Christian Schönbein had reported the effect in 1838. Alkaline fuel cells powered the Apollo spacecraft, and proton-exchange membrane cells now drive hydrogen vehicles and provide backup power.
