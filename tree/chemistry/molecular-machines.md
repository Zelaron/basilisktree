Jean-Pierre Sauvage made interlocked-ring catenanes (1983), Fraser Stoddart a rotaxane shuttle (1991) and Ben Feringa a light-driven molecular rotary motor (1999), giving molecules that move in a controlled way. They shared the 2016 Nobel Prize in Chemistry; applications such as drug delivery and smart materials remain at an early stage.
