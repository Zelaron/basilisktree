Chinese alchemical texts of about 850 CE warn that mixing saltpetre, sulfur and charcoal can cause fires, and the Wujing Zongyao of 1044 gives the first military formulas. Gunpowder reached the Islamic world and Europe by the 13th century, made guns and cannon possible, and remained the only widely used explosive until the 19th century.
