Johann Becher (1667) and Georg Ernst Stahl (1703) proposed that burning and rusting release a substance called phlogiston. Although wrong, the theory organised combustion, smelting and respiration into one scheme and guided experiments for a century, until Lavoisier's oxygen theory replaced it.
