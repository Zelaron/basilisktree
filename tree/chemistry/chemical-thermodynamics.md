Josiah Willard Gibbs's paper 'On the Equilibrium of Heterogeneous Substances' (1876 to 1878) introduced free energy, chemical potential and the phase rule. It showed how to calculate from thermodynamic data whether a reaction proceeds and where it stops, and became the foundation of physical chemistry and materials science.
