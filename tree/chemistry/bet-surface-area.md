Stephen Brunauer, Paul Emmett and Edward Teller extended Langmuir's adsorption model to multiple layers in 1938. The BET method, which measures how much nitrogen gas a powder adsorbs, is the standard way to determine the surface area of catalysts, pharmaceuticals, cements and porous materials such as zeolites and metal-organic frameworks.
