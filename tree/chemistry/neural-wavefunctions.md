FermiNet (David Pfau, James Spencer and colleagues at DeepMind) and PauliNet (Jan Hermann, Frank Noé and colleagues), both introduced in 2019 and published in 2020, use deep neural networks as many-electron wavefunctions optimised by variational Monte Carlo. They match or beat coupled-cluster accuracy on small molecules.
