Franz Fischer and Hans Tropsch at the Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Coal Research found in 1925 that carbon monoxide and hydrogen over iron or cobalt catalysts form liquid hydrocarbons. The process makes synthetic fuels from coal, natural gas, biomass or captured carbon dioxide; Germany made fuel this way in the Second World War, and South Africa still does.
