Ahmed Zewail used femtosecond laser pulses in 1987 to watch ICN molecules break apart in real time, making the transition state of a reaction directly observable. Femtochemistry, rewarded with the 1999 Nobel Prize in Chemistry, opened the study of chemical dynamics on the timescale of bond vibrations.
