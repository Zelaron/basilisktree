Bjørk Hammer and Jens Nørskov showed in 1995 that the energy of a metal's d-band states determines how strongly adsorbates bind to its surface, which also explains why gold is the noblest metal. With scaling relations and volcano plots, the d-band model made computational screening and design of heterogeneous catalysts possible.
