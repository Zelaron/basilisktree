Ascanio Sobrero made nitroglycerin in 1847 but found it too dangerous to handle. Alfred Nobel soaked it into porous kieselguhr and added a blasting cap, patenting dynamite in 1867. It made mining, tunnelling and canal building far faster, and its profits endowed the Nobel Prizes.
