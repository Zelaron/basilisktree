Neanderthals at Campitello Quarry in Italy glued flint flakes to handles with birch-bark tar about 200,000 years ago. Making the tar means heating bark with little oxygen, a simple dry distillation, so this is among the oldest known manufactured materials and an early use of chemistry to make an adhesive.
