More than 550 absorption bands imprinted on starlight by interstellar gas, first noted by Mary Lea Heger in 1922, still lack known carriers; only ionized buckminsterfullerene, C60+, is confirmed (2015–2019), accounting for five of them. A solution assigns further bands to specific molecules by matching laboratory or computed spectra in wavelength, width and relative strength, confirmed by independent groups.
