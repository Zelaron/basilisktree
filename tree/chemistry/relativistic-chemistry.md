Pekka Pyykkö and Jean-Paul Desclaux showed in 1979 that relativity shrinks the s orbitals of heavy atoms, which explains why gold is yellow and mercury is liquid. Relativistic effects were later shown to supply most of the voltage of a lead-acid battery (2011), and quantum chemists must include them for heavy elements.
