Paul Anastas and John Warner's 1998 book set out twelve principles of green chemistry, building on Barry Trost's concept of atom economy (1991). Preventing waste, choosing safer solvents and reagents and using catalysis became design criteria for chemical processes, and the principles are now taught and applied in industry.
