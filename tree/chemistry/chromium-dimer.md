The chromium dimer Cr2, a molecule with a formal sextuple bond, had resisted accurate calculation since the 1980s. Henrik Larsson, Huanchen Zhai, Cyrus Umrigar and Garnet Chan computed its potential energy curve close to experiment using DMRG and quantum Monte Carlo (JACS, 2022), in a paper titled 'Closing a Chapter of Quantum Chemistry'.
