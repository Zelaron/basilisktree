Pol Duwez's group at Caltech cooled a gold-silicon melt at about a million degrees per second in 1960 and froze it into a disordered, glass-like metal. Metallic glasses are strong and elastic and make good transformer cores; bulk metallic glasses from slower-cooling alloys appeared in the 1990s.
