Graph neural networks trained on Materials Project data, M3GNet (Chi Chen and Shyue Ping Ong, 2022), CHGNet (2023) and MACE-MP-0 (Ilyes Batatia, Gábor Csányi and colleagues, 2023), can simulate almost any inorganic material across the periodic table without retraining. These universal interatomic potentials made machine-learned simulation an off-the-shelf tool.
