Friedrich Reinitzer found in 1888 that cholesteryl benzoate seems to melt twice, first to a cloudy fluid and then to a clear liquid. Otto Lehmann studied it in polarised light and recognised a state between crystal and liquid, the basis of liquid-crystal displays in watches, computers, phones and televisions.
