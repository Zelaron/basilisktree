Does an extra electron in liquid water sit in a cavity it carves out, or spread over the surrounding water molecules? The hydrated electron, identified by its absorption spectrum by Hart and Boag in 1962, drives radiation chemistry, and simulations have supported both pictures. A solution is one first-principles model that reproduces its measured optical, vibrational and thermodynamic properties together and rules out the alternative.
