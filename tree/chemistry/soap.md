A Sumerian clay tablet of about 2500 BCE gives a formula for a soap-like cleanser made by heating oil with wood ash, used for washing wool. Boiling fat with alkali, saponification, has made soap ever since, and Michel Chevreul worked out its chemistry in the 1810s and 1820s.
