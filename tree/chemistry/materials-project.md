Kristin Persson, Gerbrand Ceder and colleagues launched the Materials Project in 2011, an open database of density functional theory calculations for known and hypothetical inorganic compounds, alongside the US Materials Genome Initiative. Its free data and software trained a generation of machine-learning models for materials discovery.
