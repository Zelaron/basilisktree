Henry Bessemer blew air through molten pig iron to burn out carbon and silicon, making steel in under half an hour (1856); William Kelly in the United States had found the same effect earlier. The Siemens-Martin open hearth (1865) and the Thomas-Gilchrist basic lining (1878) extended it, and cheap steel built railways, ships and skyscrapers.
