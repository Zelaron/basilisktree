Emanuel Peled proposed in 1979 that lithium and other alkali metals survive in organic electrolytes because a thin film forms on their surface, the solid electrolyte interphase, which passes ions but blocks electrons. The interphase decides the life, safety and fast charging of lithium-ion cells, and engineering it remains central to battery research.
