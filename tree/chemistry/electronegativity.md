Linus Pauling put numbers on how strongly an atom attracts the electrons it shares in a bond (1932), starting from bond energies. His electronegativity scale explains bond polarity and acidity and helps predict reactivity across the periodic table; Mulliken (1934) and Allred and Rochow (1958) proposed other scales.
