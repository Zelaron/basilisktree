Justus von Liebig argued in 1840 that plants take their nutrients from mineral salts in the soil, and John Bennet Lawes patented superphosphate, made by treating phosphate rock with sulfuric acid, in 1842. A manufactured fertilizer industry followed and raised crop yields through the rest of the 19th century.
