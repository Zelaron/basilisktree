How do hydrocarbon molecules in a flame turn into the first solid soot particles so quickly? Physical stacking of aromatic molecules and chemical routes such as resonance-stabilized radical chain reactions (2018) have been proposed, and a 2023 claim of physical nucleation was disputed in 2025. A solution is a mechanism that, in a validated kinetic model, reproduces measured inception rates and particle composition across flames.
