What is the ground-state energy of the iron–molybdenum cofactor of nitrogenase in the 76-orbital model used since 2019 to benchmark quantum computers, after Reiher and colleagues posed the problem in 2017? Solving it means an energy within chemical accuracy, about 1 kcal/mol, and a ranking of the spin states. A January 2026 preprint from Garnet Chan's group claims the energy classically but finds several spin states tied.
