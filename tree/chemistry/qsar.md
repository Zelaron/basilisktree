Corwin Hansch and Toshio Fujita showed in 1964 that the biological activity of related compounds can be fitted to their lipophilicity and to electronic substituent constants. This founded quantitative structure-activity relationships (QSAR), which predict the properties of untested molecules and are forerunners of today's machine-learning property models.
