Mesoamericans mixed latex from the rubber tree with the juice of morning glory vines to change its bounce, and made rubber balls by about 1600 BCE, as finds from El Manatí in Mexico and later experiments show. It is deliberate polymer processing three thousand years before vulcanisation.
