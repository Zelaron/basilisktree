John Fenn's electrospray ionisation (1988 to 1989) and the laser desorption methods of Koichi Tanaka (1987 to 1988) and of Michael Karas and Franz Hillenkamp (1988, later MALDI) lifted intact proteins into the gas phase for mass analysis. They made mass spectrometry a main tool of proteomics and biochemistry, and Fenn and Tanaka shared the 2002 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
