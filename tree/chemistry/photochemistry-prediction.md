Can simulations predict what a molecule does after absorbing light, including products, branching ratios and timescales, before the experiment? In a 2023–24 challenge, 15 blind predictions for cyclobutanone agreed with ultrafast electron diffraction only qualitatively and hinged on the electronic-structure method. A solution is a reproducible protocol whose blind predictions match measured lifetimes and product ratios for new molecules.
