From his time in Basel (about 1527) Paracelsus pushed alchemy toward making medicines from minerals and metals and argued that the dose makes the poison. Iatrochemistry, or medical chemistry, started chemical pharmacology and the idea that diseases can be treated with specific chemical substances.
