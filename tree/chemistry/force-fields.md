Shneior Lifson and Arieh Warshel treated molecules as balls and springs with fitted parameters in their consistent force field (1968), and Norman Allinger's MM2 (1977) refined it for organic chemistry. Force fields make it cheap enough to simulate proteins, polymers and drug binding with molecular dynamics.
