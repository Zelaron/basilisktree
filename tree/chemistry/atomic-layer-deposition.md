Tuomo Suntola developed atomic layer epitaxy in 1974, building films one atomic layer at a time from alternating self-limiting surface reactions; Soviet chemists around Valentin Aleskovskii had worked on similar molecular layering since the 1960s. Atomic layer deposition makes the hafnium oxide gate insulators in chips from 2007 and is used in displays and batteries.
