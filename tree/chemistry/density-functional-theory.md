Pierre Hohenberg and Walter Kohn proved in 1964 that the ground-state energy of a many-electron system is determined by its electron density, and Kohn and Lu Jeu Sham made this practical in 1965. Density functional theory is now the most widely used method for calculating the properties of molecules and materials, and brought Kohn the 1998 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
