A crystal structure of the 2-norbornyl cation at 40 K, reported in 2013 by Ingo Krossing, Karsten Meyer, Paul von Ragué Schleyer and co-workers, showed a delocalised three-centre, two-electron bond. It ended a dispute of about six decades, begun by Saul Winstein and challenged by Herbert Brown, over whether this ion is nonclassical.
