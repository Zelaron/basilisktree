Find a dry polymer electrolyte that conducts lithium ions at above 1 mS/cm at 25 °C and is stable against lithium metal. Poly(ethylene oxide)–salt electrolytes, proposed for batteries by Michel Armand in 1978, reach about 0.01 mS/cm at room temperature, and reports since 2022 of 1–3 mS/cm near 30 °C rely on high salt contents or plasticizers. A solution is a plasticizer-free polymer meeting the target in independent tests and long cycling.
