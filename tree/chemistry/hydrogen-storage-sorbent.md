Design a porous material that stores hydrogen near room temperature well enough for a vehicle tank to meet the US Department of Energy's system targets of 5.5 wt% and 40 g/L (2025) and ultimately 6.5 wt% and 50 g/L, between −40 and 60 °C. That needs binding of about 15–25 kJ/mol at many sites; frameworks tested since 2003 reach high capacities only near 77 K. A solution is a material whose measured usable capacity lets a system meet the targets.
