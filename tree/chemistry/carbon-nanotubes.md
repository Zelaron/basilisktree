Sumio Iijima's electron micrographs of multiwalled carbon nanotubes (1991) were followed in 1993 by single-walled tubes, reported by Iijima and by Donald Bethune's group. The tubes are very strong, conduct heat well and are metallic or semiconducting depending on their structure, and they are used in composites and in electronics research.
