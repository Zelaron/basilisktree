Find a p-type transparent conductor as good as n-type indium tin oxide, which conducts about 10⁴ S/cm while staying clear, enabling transparent p–n electronics. Since CuAlO2 (1997), doped CuI films have reached about 600 S/cm and amorphous Cu(S,I) 10³–10⁴ S/cm (2023), but with band gaps of 2.6–2.9 eV that absorb blue light. A solution is an air-stable p-type film above 1,000 S/cm with a band gap above 3.1 eV, reproduced independently.
