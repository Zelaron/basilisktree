Carl Frosch and Lincoln Derick at Bell Labs found in 1957 that a thermally grown silicon dioxide layer masks silicon against dopants, and Mohamed Atalla showed in 1957 to 1959 that it also passivates the surface. These findings made the planar process and the MOSFET possible, and with them the integrated circuit industry.
