Jan Czochralski pulled a thin metal crystal from a melt in 1916 while measuring crystallisation rates. Gordon Teal and John Little revived the method at Bell Labs in 1950 to grow single-crystal germanium and then silicon, and it now grows nearly all the silicon wafers used for chips and solar cells.
