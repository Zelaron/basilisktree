Paul Flory and Maurice Huggins independently developed in 1941 and 1942 a lattice model of polymer solutions that predicts when a polymer dissolves, swells or separates from its solvent. Flory-Huggins theory remains the basic thermodynamic model for polymer solutions and blends, and Flory received the 1974 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for his work on polymers.
