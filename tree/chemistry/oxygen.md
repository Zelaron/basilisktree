Carl Wilhelm Scheele (about 1771, published 1777) and Joseph Priestley (1774) isolated a gas that made candles burn brightly and kept mice alive. Antoine Lavoisier recognised it as the element that combines in burning and named it oxygen, overturning phlogiston theory and starting the chemical revolution.
