Copper is the only metal that electro-reduces CO2 to ethylene and ethanol in quantity, as Yoshio Hori's group found in 1985–86, yet how the key carbon–carbon bond forms is disputed: CO dimerization, CO–CHO coupling, cations and surface restructuring have all been proposed. A solution is a mechanism, tested by simulation and in situ experiment, that predicts product selectivity well enough to design better catalysts.
