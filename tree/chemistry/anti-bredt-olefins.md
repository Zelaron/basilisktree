Bredt's rule of 1924 says that a double bond cannot sit at the bridgehead of a small bridged ring system because of ring strain. Neil Garg's group at UCLA generated and trapped such anti-Bredt olefins as reactive intermediates and used them in synthesis (Science, 2024).
