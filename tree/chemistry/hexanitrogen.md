Weiyu Qian, Artur Mardyukov and Peter Schreiner made the nitrogen chain N6, hexanitrogen, by reacting chlorine or bromine with silver azide, and isolated it as a film at 77 K (Nature, 2025). It is the first neutral nitrogen allotrope other than N2 to be isolated, and it decomposes to N2 with a large release of energy.
