Design a sorbent that pulls CO2 from air at about 420 ppm, works in humid air, releases it with little heat and lasts thousands of cycles, so that direct air capture, assessed by the American Physical Society in 2011, can approach the US Department of Energy's goal of under $100 per tonne; costs in 2025 exceeded $1,000. A solution is a sorbent whose measured capacity, kinetics and stability support a credible process cost below $100 per tonne.
