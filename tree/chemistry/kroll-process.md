William Kroll showed in 1940 that titanium tetrachloride reduced with molten magnesium gives titanium metal. The Kroll process, run commercially from the late 1940s, still supplies most titanium metal for jet engines, airframes, chemical plant and medical implants, although it is slow and costly.
