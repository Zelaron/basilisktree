Axel Becke showed in 1993 that mixing a fraction of exact Hartree-Fock exchange into density functional theory greatly improves accuracy; B3LYP, which adds Lee-Yang-Parr correlation, became the most used functional for organic molecules. The non-hybrid PBE functional (1996) remains the workhorse for solids.
