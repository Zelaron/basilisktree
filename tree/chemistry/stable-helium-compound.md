Find a neutral compound of helium that is thermodynamically stable at ordinary pressure, a goal since the first noble-gas compound was made in 1962. Na2He, made in a diamond anvil cell in 2017, is stable only above 113 GPa, and a 2021 calculation put a van der Waals solid, HeN4, on the convex hull at 0 GPa, though it has not been made. A solution is a compound predicted to be stable at 1 atm and room temperature and then synthesized.
