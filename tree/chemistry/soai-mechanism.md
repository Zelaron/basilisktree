How does the Soai reaction, found in 1995, turn a tiny excess of one mirror-image form of a pyridyl or pyrimidyl alcohol into almost pure product as its zinc alkoxide catalyses its own formation? A tetrameric zinc aggregate (Denmark, 2020) and hemiacetal zinc complexes (Trapp, 2025) compete as explanations. A solution is a mechanism and kinetic model that reproduces the amplification quantitatively. Soai shared the 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
