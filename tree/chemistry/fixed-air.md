Joseph Black heated magnesia alba (magnesium carbonate) and showed by weighing in 1754 that the fixed air it lost is a distinct gas, now called carbon dioxide, that is also produced by fermentation and breathing. It was the first gas characterised by quantitative experiment and began the chemistry of airs.
