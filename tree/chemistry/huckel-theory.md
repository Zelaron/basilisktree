Erich Hückel's simple orbital model of pi electrons (1931) explained why planar rings with 4n+2 pi electrons, such as benzene, are unusually stable. Despite its crudeness it still guides chemists' thinking about aromaticity, conjugated molecules, dyes and organic electronics.
