Marie and Pierre Curie followed radioactivity through chemical separations of pitchblende and announced polonium and radium in 1898, then isolated radium salts from tonnes of ore. They founded radiochemistry; Marie Curie received the 1903 Nobel Prize in Physics (shared) and the 1911 Nobel Prize in Chemistry, and radium became a cancer treatment and a research tool.
