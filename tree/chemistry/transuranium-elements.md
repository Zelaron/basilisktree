Edwin McMillan and Philip Abelson made neptunium, element 93, in 1940, and Glenn Seaborg's group made plutonium, element 94, in 1940 to 1941 at Berkeley. Seaborg's actinide concept (1944) redrew the periodic table, and McMillan and Seaborg shared the 1951 Nobel Prize in Chemistry; elements up to 118 had been made by 2010.
