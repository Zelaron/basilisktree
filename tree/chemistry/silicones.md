Frederic Kipping studied organosilicon compounds for decades. Eugene Rochow at General Electric found in 1940, and Richard Müller in Germany independently soon after, that methyl chloride passed over heated silicon and copper gives methylchlorosilanes. This direct process makes silicone oils, rubbers, resins and sealants cheaply, for uses from medical implants to building construction.
