Electrolysing brine yields chlorine, caustic soda and hydrogen together. Hamilton Castner's mercury cell and Karl Kellner's similar one, both patented in 1892, made it a large industry. Chlorine feeds PVC, solvents, bleach and water disinfection, and caustic soda feeds paper, soap and aluminium refining.
