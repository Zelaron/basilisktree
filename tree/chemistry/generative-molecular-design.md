Rafael Gómez-Bombarelli, Alán Aspuru-Guzik and colleagues trained a variational autoencoder on SMILES strings to map molecules into a continuous space (ACS Central Science, 2018; preprint 2016). Gradient-based search in that space proposes new molecules with desired properties, launching generative chemistry for drug and materials design.
