Oxidize methane straight to methanol with oxygen or air under mild conditions, at high conversion and selectivity, as the enzyme methane monooxygenase does; industry instead goes through energy-intensive syngas. Periana's 1998 platinum catalyst needed fuming sulfuric acid, and later systems are selective only at low conversion or with stepwise schemes. A solution converts methane directly with O2 at practical conversion and over 90% selectivity.
