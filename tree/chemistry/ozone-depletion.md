Mario Molina and Sherwood Rowland showed in 1974 that chlorine released from CFCs in the stratosphere destroys ozone in a catalytic chain, after Paul Crutzen's work on nitrogen oxides (1970). The Antarctic ozone hole (1985) confirmed the threat and led to the Montreal Protocol (1987); the three shared the 1995 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
