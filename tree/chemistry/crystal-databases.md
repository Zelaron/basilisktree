Olga Kennard founded the Cambridge Structural Database in 1965 to collect all published crystal structures of organic and metal-organic compounds; it now holds more than a million. With the Inorganic Crystal Structure Database and the Protein Data Bank, such archives became the data on which modern computational chemistry and materials machine learning rely.
