The Jabirian corpus (about 850 to 950 CE) and Abu Bakr al-Razi's Secret of Secrets (about 900) classified substances and described laboratory apparatus and procedures. Alkali, alembic and elixir are Arabic loanwords, and Latin translations carried the methods to medieval Europe.
