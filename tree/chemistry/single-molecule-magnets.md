Roberta Sessoli, Dante Gatteschi and colleagues showed in 1993 that a manganese cluster, Mn12-acetate, keeps its magnetisation like a tiny bar magnet at low temperature. Dysprosocenium complexes (Layfield and Mills groups, 2017 and 2018) raised the hysteresis temperature to 60 K and then 80 K, above the boiling point of liquid nitrogen, with hopes for dense data storage and quantum bits.
