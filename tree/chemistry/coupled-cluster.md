Jiří Čížek adapted the exponential cluster expansion from nuclear physics to molecular electrons in 1966. After Rodney Bartlett and others made it practical in the 1970s and 1980s, coupled cluster with single, double and perturbative triple excitations, CCSD(T) (1989), became the gold standard for accurate small-molecule energies.
