Surface-force measurements in 2016 found that the electrostatic screening length in concentrated salt solutions and ionic liquids grows with concentration, far beyond Debye–Hückel theory, but other techniques have not consistently reproduced it. A solution is a theory or simulation that agrees with all reliable measurements, settles whether this underscreening is a bulk property, and explains its scaling with ion size and concentration.
