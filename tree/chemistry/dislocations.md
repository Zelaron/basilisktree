Three papers of 1934 by G. I. Taylor, Egon Orowan and Michael Polanyi independently proposed line defects in crystals, called dislocations, to explain why metals yield at a tiny fraction of their theoretical strength. The theory of how dislocations glide, tangle and pin one another is the basis of metal strengthening, alloy design and work hardening.
