Alexander Parkes moulded nitrocellulose into Parkesine, shown at the 1862 London exhibition. John Wesley Hyatt's celluloid, nitrocellulose plasticised with camphor (1870), replaced ivory and tortoiseshell and later became photographic and cinema film.
