In 1923 Johannes Brønsted and Thomas Lowry independently defined an acid as a proton donor and a base as a proton acceptor, while Gilbert Lewis defined acids as electron-pair acceptors and bases as electron-pair donors. The first is the working definition in water chemistry and biochemistry, and the second extends to catalysis and coordination chemistry.
