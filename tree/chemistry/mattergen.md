Microsoft Research's MatterGen, a diffusion model for crystal structures (Claudio Zeni, Tian Xie and colleagues, Nature, 2025), generates stable materials for specified chemistry, symmetry and property targets. One generated compound, TaCr2O6, was synthesised, and its measured bulk modulus came within about 20 percent of the target.
