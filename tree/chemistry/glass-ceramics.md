Don Stookey at Corning overheated a photosensitive lithium silicate glass in 1953 and found that it had crystallised into a strong, opaque ceramic instead of melting. Controlled crystallisation of glass gave Pyroceram for missile nose cones and CorningWare cookware, and glass-ceramics are now used for stovetops and telescope mirror blanks.
