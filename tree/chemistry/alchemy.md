Greco-Egyptian alchemists such as Zosimos of Panopolis (about 300 CE), and independently Chinese alchemists, tried to transmute base metals into gold and to make elixirs of life. Their apparatus, procedures and substances, including distillation, sublimation and acids, grew into chemistry, though transmutation was never achieved.
