Ludwik Leibler's group at ESPCI in Paris reported in 2011 polymer networks whose cross-links swap partners when heated without breaking the network, so a thermoset can be reshaped, repaired and recycled while staying insoluble. The material flows gradually like silica glass, hence the name vitrimer.
