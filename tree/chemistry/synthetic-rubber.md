Fritz Hofmann's team at Bayer patented the process for methyl rubber, an early synthetic rubber, in 1909. German Buna rubbers (1929) and DuPont's neoprene (1930) followed, and wartime shortages of natural rubber made synthetic rubber a very large industry in the 1940s. It now supplies about half of the world's rubber.
