Brian O'Regan and Michael Grätzel reported in 1991 a solar cell in which a dye on nanoporous titanium dioxide absorbs light and injects electrons, reaching about 7 percent efficiency with cheap materials. The Grätzel cell inspired later photovoltaics, including the perovskite solar cell.
