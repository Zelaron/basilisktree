William Buehler and colleagues at the US Naval Ordnance Laboratory found between 1959 and 1962 that an alloy of nickel and titanium, named Nitinol, returns to a remembered shape when warmed, through a reversible martensitic transformation. Nitinol is used in vascular stents, orthodontic wires and actuators.
