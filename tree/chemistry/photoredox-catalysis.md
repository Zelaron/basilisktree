In 2008 and 2009 David MacMillan, Tehshik Yoon and Corey Stephenson and their groups showed that ruthenium and iridium complexes excited by visible light can shuttle single electrons to drive organic reactions under mild conditions. Photoredox catalysis became a mainstream tool for forming bonds in drug discovery and materials synthesis.
