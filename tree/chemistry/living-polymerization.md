Michael Szwarc showed in 1956 that anionic polymerisation of styrene started with sodium naphthalene has no termination step, so the chains keep growing whenever more monomer is added. Living polymerisation gives polymers of uniform chain length and lets chemists build block copolymers; it is the model for later controlled radical methods.
