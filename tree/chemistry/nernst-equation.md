Walther Nernst derived in 1889 how an electrode's potential depends on the logarithm of the concentrations of the ions involved, linking electrochemistry to thermodynamics. It gives battery and cell voltages, sets the readings of pH and ion-selective electrodes and describes membrane potentials in nerve cells.
