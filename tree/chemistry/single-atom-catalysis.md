Botao Qiao, Tao Zhang, Jun Li and colleagues showed in 2011 that isolated platinum atoms on iron oxide catalyse the oxidation of carbon monoxide efficiently, and coined the term single-atom catalysis. Using every metal atom as an active site saves precious metals, and single-atom catalysts became a large research field linking homogeneous and heterogeneous catalysis.
