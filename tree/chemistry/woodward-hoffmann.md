Robert Woodward and Roald Hoffmann showed in 1965 that the symmetry of molecular orbitals decides whether concerted reactions such as electrocyclic ring closures and cycloadditions occur, and with what stereochemistry. With Kenichi Fukui's frontier orbital theory (1952), the rules won Hoffmann and Fukui the 1981 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
