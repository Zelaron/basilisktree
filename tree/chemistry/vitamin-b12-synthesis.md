Robert Woodward at Harvard and Albert Eschenmoser at ETH Zürich, leading about a hundred co-workers over more than a decade, completed the total synthesis of vitamin B12 in 1972, one of the most complex natural products ever made. Puzzles met along the way led Woodward and Roald Hoffmann to the orbital symmetry rules.
