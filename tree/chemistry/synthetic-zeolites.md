Richard Barrer made synthetic zeolites by hydrothermal methods from 1948, and Robert Milton at Union Carbide produced zeolites A and X from 1949 and developed them as molecular sieves for drying and separating gases. Zeolite catalysts transformed oil refining from the 1960s, and zeolites are also used in detergents and water softening.
