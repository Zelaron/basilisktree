Karl Jansky, studying static for Bell Labs in 1932-33, found a steady radio hiss that rose and fell once per sidereal day and came from the direction of the centre of the Milky Way. Grote Reber built a dish telescope in 1937 and mapped the radio sky. Radio astronomy went on to reveal pulsars, quasars and the cosmic microwave background.
