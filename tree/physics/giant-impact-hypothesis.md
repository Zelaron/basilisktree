William Hartmann and Donald Davis (1975), and independently Alastair Cameron and William Ward (1976), proposed that the Moon formed from debris thrown out when a Mars-sized body struck the young Earth. The idea explains the Moon's small iron core and the large angular momentum of the Earth-Moon system, and it is now the leading theory of lunar origin.
