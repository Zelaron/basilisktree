If a black hole evaporates into purely thermal Hawking radiation, the information about what fell in seems destroyed, contradicting quantum mechanics (Hawking, 1976). Calculations since 2019 with quantum extremal surfaces and replica wormholes reproduce the Page curve that unitarity requires, mostly in simplified models; how the information gets out of a realistic black hole, and what an infalling observer meets, remain open.
