Dust in a planet-forming disk sticks into pebbles, but bodies around a metre across should bounce, fragment or drift into the star faster than they can grow (Weidenschilling, 1977). How do kilometre-sized planetesimals form? The streaming instability followed by gravitational collapse is the leading answer and fits Kuiper-belt binaries, but whether it works under realistic disk turbulence is open.
