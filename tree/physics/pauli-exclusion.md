Wolfgang Pauli proposed in 1925 that no two electrons in an atom can occupy the same quantum state. The rule accounts for the shell structure of atoms and so for the periodic table and chemistry. The same principle keeps ordinary matter from collapsing and supports white dwarfs and neutron stars against gravity.
