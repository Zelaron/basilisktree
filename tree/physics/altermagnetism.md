Libor Šmejkal, Jairo Sinova, Tomáš Jungwirth and colleagues showed in 2022 by symmetry analysis that a third class of collinear magnets exists, with zero net magnetization like antiferromagnets but spin-split electronic bands like ferromagnets. Photoemission experiments saw the spin splitting in MnTe in 2024. Altermagnets could be used in spintronic devices.
