Gustav Kirchhoff and Robert Bunsen showed in 1859 that each element emits and absorbs light at its own set of wavelengths, so spectra identify substances. They explained the dark Fraunhofer lines in sunlight as absorption in the Sun's atmosphere and discovered caesium and rubidium this way. Spectral analysis revealed what stars are made of and later led to quantum theory.
