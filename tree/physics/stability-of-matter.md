Freeman Dyson and Andrew Lenard proved in 1967-68 that the energy of ordinary matter made of electrons and nuclei is bounded below by a multiple of the number of particles, so matter does not collapse or release unlimited energy. The proof relies on the exclusion principle, and Elliott Lieb and Walter Thirring gave a much shorter proof in 1975.
