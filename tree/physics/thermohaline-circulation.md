Henry Stommel showed in 1961 with a two-box model that the density-driven overturning of the ocean, set by temperature and salinity, can have two stable states, so ocean circulation can switch abruptly. Wallace Broecker's picture of a global conveyor belt (1987) linked deep-water formation in the North Atlantic to rapid climate shifts recorded in ice cores.
