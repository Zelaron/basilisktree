Joseph Fourier argued in 1824 that the atmosphere keeps Earth warmer than it would be without one. Eunice Foote (1856) found that carbon dioxide and water vapour heat most in sunlight, and John Tyndall (1859) measured how strongly they absorb infrared radiation while oxygen and nitrogen do not. This is the physical basis of climate science and of the warming caused by added CO2.
