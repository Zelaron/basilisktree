In 2023 groups led by Xiaodong Xu, by Kin Fai Mak and Jie Shan, and by Tingxin Li observed fractional quantum Hall states without an external magnetic field in twisted MoTe2, and in 2024 Long Ju's group saw them in rhombohedral graphene. Their fractionally charged excitations at zero field offer a possible route to non-abelian anyons and topological qubits.
