George Gamow, and independently Ronald Gurney and Edward Condon, showed in 1928 that a quantum particle has a finite chance of passing through an energy barrier that classical physics forbids. This explained alpha decay and its widely varying lifetimes. Tunnelling also lets the Sun fuse hydrogen and underlies the scanning tunnelling microscope and flash memory.
