Gary Glatzmaier and Paul Roberts ran in 1995 a three-dimensional computer simulation of convection and magnetic field generation in Earth's liquid outer core. It produced a dipole field like Earth's that reversed polarity spontaneously after tens of thousands of simulated years, showing that the geodynamo and its reversals can be studied by solving the fluid and magnetic equations.
