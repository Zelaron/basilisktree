Inge Lehmann found faint P waves from distant earthquakes arriving inside the shadow zone of the liquid core, and concluded in 1936 that the core contains a solid inner sphere that refracts waves into the shadow. It is mostly iron and nickel, about 1,200 km in radius, and it grows slowly as the Earth cools.
