Inge Lehmann found faint P waves from distant earthquakes arriving inside the shadow zone of the liquid core, and concluded in 1936 that the core has a distinct inner sphere that refracts waves into the shadow. It was later shown to be solid, is mostly iron and nickel, about 1,200 km in radius, and grows slowly as the Earth cools.
