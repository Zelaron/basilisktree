Igor Gornyi, Alexander Mirlin and Dmitry Polyakov (2005) and, independently, Denis Basko, Igor Aleiner and Boris Altshuler (2006) argued that interacting particles in a disordered system can fail to thermalize, staying localized even at finite energy density. Signatures were seen in cold-atom experiments in 2015, and whether the phase survives in large systems is debated.
