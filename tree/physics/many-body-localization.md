Denis Basko, Igor Aleiner and Boris Altshuler argued in 2006 that interacting particles in a strongly disordered system can fail to thermalize, remaining localized even at finite energy density, so that the system never reaches equilibrium. Signatures were seen in cold-atom experiments in 2015. Whether the phase survives in very large systems is still debated.
