Bohigas, Giannoni and Schmit (1984) conjectured that quantum systems whose classical motion is chaotic, such as the Sinai billiard, have energy levels with the statistics of random matrices. Numerics support it overwhelmingly and periodic-orbit theory explains it, but there is no proof for any single chaotic billiard or surface; rigorous results cover random ensembles and special many-body models.
