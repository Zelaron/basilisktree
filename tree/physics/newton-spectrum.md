Newton's prism experiments (1666, published 1672) showed that white sunlight is a mixture of colours, which a prism separates and a second prism recombines, and that each colour is refracted by its own amount. He concluded that lenses cannot bring all colours to one focus and built a reflecting telescope. His Opticks (1704) gave the full account.
