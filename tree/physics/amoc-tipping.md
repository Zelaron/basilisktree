Could the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation, which carries heat to the North Atlantic, collapse under global warming? Stommel's 1961 box model and a 1988 coupled model have two stable states (Broecker warned of the risk in 1987); statistical early-warning signals suggest a collapse this century, while most climate models weaken it without collapse before 2100. Reconciling the two would settle it.
