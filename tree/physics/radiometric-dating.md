Bertram Boltwood showed in 1907 that the ratio of lead to uranium in rocks grows with age, and his dates ran from hundreds of millions to over two billion years. Arthur Holmes built the first geological time scale from radioactive decay in 1913. Radiometric dating gives absolute ages for rocks, meteorites and fossils, and it showed that Earth is billions of years old.
