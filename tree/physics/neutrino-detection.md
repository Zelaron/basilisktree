Clyde Cowan and Frederick Reines detected antineutrinos from the Savannah River nuclear reactor in 1956 through inverse beta decay, in which an antineutrino turns a proton into a neutron and a positron. The particle Pauli had proposed in 1930 was seen 26 years later, and Reines received the 1995 Nobel Prize in Physics for it.
