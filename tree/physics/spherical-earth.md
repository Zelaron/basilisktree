Aristotle (about 350 BCE) argued that Earth is a sphere, because its shadow on the Moon in eclipses is always round and the stars visible from a place change as one travels north or south. The idea had been proposed by the Pythagoreans, and from Aristotle on it was the basis of Greek geography and astronomy.
