Ice cores show that atmospheric CO2 fell to about 190 ppm at glacial maxima, roughly 90 ppm below interglacial levels (Vostok record, 1987). The ocean must have stored the missing carbon, but models using known mechanisms struggle to reproduce the full drawdown. A quantitative carbon budget that a physically based model reproduces across a whole glacial cycle would settle it.
