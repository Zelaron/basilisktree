Hideki Yukawa predicted in 1935 that the nuclear force is carried by a particle about 200 times heavier than the electron. In 1947 Cecil Powell, César Lattes and Giuseppe Occhialini found it, the pion, in cosmic-ray tracks in photographic emulsions exposed on mountains. Yukawa's link between a force's range and the mass of the exchanged particle became a standard tool.
