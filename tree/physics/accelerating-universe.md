The Supernova Cosmology Project led by Saul Perlmutter and the High-Z Supernova Search Team led by Brian Schmidt, with Adam Riess, found in 1998-99 that distant Type Ia supernovae are fainter than expected, so the expansion of the universe is speeding up. The cause, dark energy, makes up about 68 percent of the cosmic energy budget, and the three shared the 2011 Nobel Prize.
