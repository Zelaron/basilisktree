Nobody knows how to define a chiral gauge theory, such as the electroweak part of the Standard Model, on a lattice beyond perturbation theory: the Nielsen–Ninomiya theorem (1981) forbids the naive route. Lüscher's construction covers abelian theories (1998), and boundary and mass-generation proposals are disputed; a lattice formulation of a non-abelian chiral gauge theory, tested in simulation, would settle it.
