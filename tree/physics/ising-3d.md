Onsager solved the two-dimensional Ising model exactly in 1944, but no exact solution is known in three dimensions, where the model describes uniaxial magnets and the liquid–gas critical point. The conformal bootstrap now fixes its critical exponents to about nine digits (2024); an exact solution, or a proof that the critical point is a conformal field theory with these data, would settle it.
