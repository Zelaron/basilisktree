Edmond Halley mapped the trade winds in 1686 and attributed them to the Sun heating the tropics. George Hadley (1735) argued that air flowing toward the equator is deflected westward by Earth's rotation, which gives the easterly trades and a circulation cell reaching to about 30 degrees latitude. William Ferrel added the mid-latitude cell in 1856.
