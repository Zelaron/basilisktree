In 1955 Enrico Fermi, John Pasta, Stanisław Ulam and Mary Tsingou simulated on the MANIAC computer a chain of masses joined by slightly nonlinear springs, expecting energy put into one mode to spread among all. Instead it nearly returned to the start. The result led to solitons and the study of chaos, and it is one of the first computer experiments in physics.
