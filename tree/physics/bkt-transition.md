Vadim Berezinskii (1971) and, independently, Michael Kosterlitz and David Thouless (1972-73) showed that two-dimensional systems such as superfluid films have a phase transition in which vortices and antivortices bind in pairs below a critical temperature and unbind above it. It was the first topological phase transition, and Kosterlitz and Thouless shared the 2016 Nobel Prize.
