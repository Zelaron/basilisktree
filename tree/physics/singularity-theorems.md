Roger Penrose proved in 1965 that once a star has collapsed far enough to form a trapped surface, general relativity requires a singularity, a place where spacetime ends. Stephen Hawking applied the argument to the expanding universe, and the Hawking-Penrose theorem of 1970 covered both cases. The theorems showed that singularities are generic predictions of general relativity.
