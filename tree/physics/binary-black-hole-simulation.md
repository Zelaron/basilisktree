Frans Pretorius in 2005 and, soon after, the groups of Manuela Campanelli and of John Baker achieved stable numerical simulations of two black holes orbiting, merging and settling down, solving Einstein's equations on supercomputers after decades of attempts. Such waveforms were used to identify and interpret the gravitational waves that LIGO detected in 2015.
