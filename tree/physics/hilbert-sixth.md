Hilbert's sixth problem (1900) asked for an axiomatic treatment of physics, including a rigorous passage from the mechanics of atoms to the laws of fluids through Boltzmann's kinetic theory. Deng, Hani and Ma derived the Boltzmann equation from hard-sphere dynamics for long times (2024), then the compressible Euler and incompressible Navier–Stokes–Fourier equations (2025); the second paper is not yet refereed.
