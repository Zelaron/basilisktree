Maarten Schmidt recognized in 1963 that the spectrum of the radio source 3C 273, whose position Cyril Hazard had fixed from lunar occultations, has hydrogen lines at a redshift of 0.158, which puts it about 2.4 billion light-years away. Quasars shine with the power of whole galaxies and are now understood to be fed by gas falling into supermassive black holes.
