Simulations of the canonical giant impact make the Moon mostly from the impactor Theia, yet Earth and Moon have nearly identical oxygen, titanium and tungsten isotopes, a match sharpened since 2001. Which impact, or which Theia, makes such a twin? Candidates include a fast-spinning Earth, equal-mass collisions and synestias; a model that matches both the dynamics and the isotopes would settle it.
