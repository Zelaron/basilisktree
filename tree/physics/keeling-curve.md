Charles David Keeling began measuring carbon dioxide at Mauna Loa Observatory in March 1958, with an infrared gas analyzer, supported by Roger Revelle at Scripps. The record shows a seasonal cycle from plant growth and a steady rise from about 313 parts per million in 1958 to over 420 today, the clearest evidence that fossil fuel burning is raising atmospheric CO2.
