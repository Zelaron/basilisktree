Stephen Hawking showed in 1974 that quantum effects near the event horizon make a black hole emit thermal radiation, with a temperature inversely proportional to its mass. A black hole therefore slowly evaporates, which gave physical meaning to its entropy and raised the black hole information paradox. The radiation from astrophysical black holes is far too faint to observe.
