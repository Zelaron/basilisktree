Telegraphed observations made it possible to draw weather maps within hours of measurement. Urbain Le Verrier organized a storm-warning service in France in the 1850s, and in Britain Robert FitzRoy issued storm warnings and the first public forecasts, which appeared in The Times from 1861. Forecasting became a routine government service.
