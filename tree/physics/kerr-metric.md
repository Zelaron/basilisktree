Roy Kerr found in 1963 the exact solution of Einstein's equations for the spacetime around a rotating mass. It has an event horizon, an ergosphere in which nothing can stay at rest, and a ring singularity. By the uniqueness theorems an uncharged black hole settles into a Kerr black hole, so the solution describes the black holes now observed.
