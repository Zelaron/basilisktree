Do rotating black holes settle down after a disturbance? The Kerr stability conjecture says that small perturbations of a Kerr black hole decay, leaving a nearby Kerr black hole. Slow rotation was settled by Klainerman, Szeftel and Giorgi (2021–2022); in June 2026 Peter Hintz posted a 339-page proof for every subextremal Kerr black hole, which has not yet been refereed.
