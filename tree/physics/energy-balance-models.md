Mikhail Budyko and William Sellers published in 1969 simple climate models in which the temperature at each latitude balances absorbed sunlight against emitted heat, with ice reflecting sunlight. The ice-albedo feedback allows more than one climate for the same sunlight and makes a runaway to a fully frozen Earth possible. Such models are still used for conceptual studies.
