Hannes Alfvén showed in 1942 that a magnetic field threading a conducting fluid is carried along with it and acts like elastic strings that support transverse waves, now called Alfvén waves. Magnetohydrodynamics became the theory of the Sun, the solar wind, planetary dynamos and fusion plasmas, and Alfvén received the 1970 Nobel Prize in Physics.
