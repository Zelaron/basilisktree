Censuses of stars and gas in the late 1990s found only about half of the ordinary matter that Big Bang nucleosynthesis requires. In 2020 Jean-Pierre Macquart's team used fast radio bursts to weigh the gas between galaxies and found the missing baryons there; a 2025 survey by Liam Connor, Vikram Ravi and colleagues put about three quarters of all baryons in it.
