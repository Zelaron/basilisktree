James Chadwick showed in 1932 that a neutral radiation produced when alpha particles hit beryllium consists of particles about as heavy as the proton. The neutron completed the picture of the nucleus as protons and neutrons, explained isotopes, and, because it carries no charge, became the key to inducing nuclear reactions, including fission.
