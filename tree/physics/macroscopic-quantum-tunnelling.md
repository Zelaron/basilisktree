John Clarke, Michel Devoret and John Martinis showed in 1984-85 that the phase across a superconducting Josephson junction, a variable describing billions of Cooper pairs, tunnels through an energy barrier and has quantized energy levels like a single atom. The experiments underlie superconducting quantum bits, and the three shared the 2025 Nobel Prize in Physics.
