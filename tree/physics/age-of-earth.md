Clair Patterson measured lead isotopes in the Canyon Diablo iron meteorite and in Earth samples, and in 1956 reported an age of 4.55 billion years for Earth and the meteorites, a figure that has stood since. The ultra-clean laboratory methods he developed also showed that leaded petrol had contaminated the environment with lead, which contributed to its phase-out.
