Tsung-Dao Lee and Chen-Ning Yang proposed in 1956 that the weak interaction might not respect mirror symmetry. In 1957 Chien-Shiung Wu and colleagues showed it: electrons from the beta decay of cobalt-60 nuclei, polarized in a magnetic field at low temperature, were emitted preferentially in one direction. This overturned the assumption that physics looks the same in a mirror.
