Ernst Chladni argued in 1794 that stones and iron masses found on the ground had fallen from space. Jean-Baptiste Biot's report on the thousands of stones that fell at L'Aigle in France in April 1803 convinced the French Academy of Sciences. Accepting that rocks reach Earth from space opened the study of the solar system's raw material.
