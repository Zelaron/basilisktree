Robert Dietz (1961) and Harry Hess (1962) proposed that new ocean floor forms at mid-ocean ridges and moves away on both sides, to be consumed in deep trenches, driven by mantle convection as Arthur Holmes had suggested in 1931. The idea explained why the ocean floor is young and gave continental drift a mechanism.
