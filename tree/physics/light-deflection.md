Arthur Eddington and Frank Dyson organized expeditions to Príncipe and Sobral to photograph stars near the Sun during the total eclipse of 29 May 1919. The stars appeared displaced by about the amount general relativity predicts, twice the Newtonian value, and the result brought Einstein public attention worldwide.
