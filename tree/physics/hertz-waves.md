Heinrich Hertz generated radio waves with a spark-gap oscillator and detected them across a room in 1886-88. He showed that they travel at the speed of light and can be reflected, refracted and polarized like light. This confirmed Maxwell's theory of electromagnetism and led directly to radio communication.
