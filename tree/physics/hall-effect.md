Edwin Hall found in 1879 that a magnetic field perpendicular to a current-carrying conductor produces a voltage across the conductor, at right angles to both. The sign and size of the voltage reveal whether the charge carriers are positive or negative and how many there are. Hall probes measure magnetic fields in many devices, and a quantum version of the effect was found in 1980.
