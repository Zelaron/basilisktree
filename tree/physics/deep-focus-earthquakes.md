Earthquakes occur as deep as about 700 km (Wadati, 1928), where pressure should suppress ordinary brittle faulting. Proposed mechanisms include transformational faulting in metastable olivine, dehydration embrittlement and thermal runaway, and each fails to explain some observations. A mechanism that matches laboratory experiments and the rupture properties of large deep earthquakes would settle it.
