Frederick Vine and Drummond Matthews (1963), and independently Lawrence Morley, proposed that the magnetic stripes measured across mid-ocean ridges record reversals of Earth's field in new crust as it forms and spreads symmetrically. The pattern gave direct, testable evidence for seafloor spreading and a way of dating the ocean floor.
