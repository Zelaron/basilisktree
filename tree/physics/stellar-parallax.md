In 1838 Friedrich Bessel measured the tiny yearly shift in the position of 61 Cygni against more distant stars, about 0.3 arcseconds, which puts it near 10 light-years away. Thomas Henderson (Alpha Centauri) and Friedrich Struve (Vega) obtained parallaxes at about the same time. It gave the first distance to a star and ended a search that began with Copernicus.
