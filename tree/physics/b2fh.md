Margaret Burbidge, Geoffrey Burbidge, William Fowler and Fred Hoyle showed in 1957 how nuclear reactions in stars build the elements from carbon to uranium, through helium burning and the slow (s) and rapid (r) neutron-capture processes. Alastair Cameron reached similar conclusions independently. Most heavy elements were thus made in stars and supernovae.
