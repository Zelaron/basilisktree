Josef Stefan inferred from experimental data in 1879 that the total power radiated by a hot body grows as the fourth power of its absolute temperature, and Ludwig Boltzmann derived the law for an ideal black body from thermodynamics in 1884. It sets the equilibrium temperatures of planets and stars and was an early step toward Planck's quantum theory.
