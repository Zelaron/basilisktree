Pablo Jarillo-Herrero's group at MIT reported in 2018 that two graphene sheets stacked with a twist of about 1.1 degrees, the magic angle that Rafi Bistritzer and Allan MacDonald had predicted in 2011 to give extremely flat electronic bands, behave as a correlated insulator and, when doped, as a superconductor. The discovery started the field of twistronics.
