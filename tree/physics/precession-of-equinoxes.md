Comparing his own measurements of star positions with older records, Hipparchus found about 129 BCE that the equinox points drift slowly westward along the ecliptic, by at least one degree per century. Newton explained the cause in 1687 as the pull of the Sun and Moon on Earth's equatorial bulge, which turns the axis around a cone once every 26,000 years.
