Lev Landau proposed in 1937 that a phase transition can be described by an order parameter, zero in the symmetric phase and nonzero in the ordered one, with the free energy expanded in powers of it. The theory ties transitions to broken symmetry and gives universal behaviour near the critical point, which the renormalization group later corrected where fluctuations matter.
