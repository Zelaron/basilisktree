In 2005 Daniel Eisenstein's team using the Sloan Digital Sky Survey, and Shaun Cole's team using the 2dF Galaxy Redshift Survey, found an excess of galaxy pairs about 150 megaparsecs apart, the imprint of sound waves in the early universe. This standard ruler measures cosmic distances and the expansion history, and it is a key probe of dark energy.
