Milutin Milanković calculated in 1920 how the wobble of Earth's axis, the changing tilt and the shape of its orbit alter the sunlight reaching high northern latitudes, and proposed that these cycles pace the ice ages. His Canon of Insolation appeared in 1941, and deep-sea sediments studied by Hays, Imbrie and Shackleton in 1976 showed climate cycles at the predicted periods.
