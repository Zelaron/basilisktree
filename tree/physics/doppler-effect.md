Christian Doppler argued in 1842 that the observed frequency of a wave depends on the relative motion of source and observer. Buys Ballot confirmed it for sound in 1845 using trumpeters on a moving train, and Hippolyte Fizeau pointed out the same shift for light in 1848. It underlies radar speed measurement, medical flow imaging and stellar radial velocities.
