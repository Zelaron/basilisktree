John Hubbard, Martin Gutzwiller and Junjiro Kanamori each proposed in 1963 a model of electrons hopping between sites of a lattice and repelling each other when two occupy the same site. It is the simplest model of strongly correlated electrons, describes the Mott transition and magnetism, and is a standard test case for numerical methods and quantum simulators.
