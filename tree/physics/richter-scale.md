Charles Richter, working with Beno Gutenberg at Caltech in 1935, defined a logarithmic magnitude scale for earthquakes from the amplitude of seismograph traces, so that each unit is a tenfold increase in amplitude. Gutenberg and Richter later gave the frequency law of earthquake sizes (1944), and the moment magnitude scale (1979) replaced the original for large events.
