For given masses, are there only finitely many central configurations, arrangements of n gravitating bodies whose accelerations all point to the centre of mass in proportion to distance, so the shape can spin rigidly? Asked by Chazy (1918) and Wintner (1941) and listed as Smale's sixth problem (1998), it is proved for four bodies (2006) and for five bodies in the plane outside a thin set of masses (2012).
