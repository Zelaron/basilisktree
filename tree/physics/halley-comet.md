Edmond Halley noticed in 1705 that the comets of 1531, 1607 and 1682 had similar orbits and predicted that one comet would return in 1758. After Alexis Clairaut calculated the delay caused by Jupiter and Saturn, it was sighted on Christmas Day 1758. The return confirmed that comets orbit the Sun under Newtonian gravity.
