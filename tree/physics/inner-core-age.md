If iron conducts heat as well as calculations (2012) and some experiments suggest, the core loses so much heat by conduction that the inner core must be young, under about a billion years, and the early geodynamo is hard to power: the 'new core paradox'. Conductivity experiments disagree and age estimates still vary widely; consistent values for iron alloys at core conditions, from experiment and first principles, would settle it.
