Ice cores drilled at Vostok, Antarctica, and analysed by Claude Lorius, Jean Jouzel, Jean-Marc Barnola and colleagues, showed in 1987 that carbon dioxide and temperature rose and fell together through the last 160,000 years of glacial cycles. The EPICA core extended the record to 800,000 years in 2008. Air trapped in the ice gives direct samples of past atmospheres.
