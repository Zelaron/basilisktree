Giuseppe Carleo and Matthias Troyer showed in 2017 that a neural network can represent the wavefunction of an interacting quantum system and be trained by variational Monte Carlo to find its ground state. The same year Juan Carrasquilla and Roger Melko showed that networks can recognize phases of matter from samples, helping to start machine learning in many-body physics.
