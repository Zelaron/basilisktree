Kenneth Wilson formulated gauge theory on a spacetime lattice in 1974. The lattice makes strongly coupled theories computable and gave a picture of how quarks are confined. Lattice QCD computed the masses of protons and other hadrons from first principles (Budapest-Marseille-Wuppertal, 2008) and supplies inputs for precision tests of the Standard Model.
