The expansion rate measured locally with Cepheids and supernovae, about 73 km/s/Mpc, disagrees with the 67 to 68 inferred from the cosmic microwave background under ΛCDM, a gap first flagged around 2013 that exceeded 5σ by 2022. Is it a measurement error or new physics? An independent local method reaching 1% precision, or a cosmological model that fits all the data, would decide it.
