On 17 August 2017 LIGO and Virgo detected gravitational waves from two merging neutron stars, and about two seconds later telescopes saw a gamma-ray burst from the same place, followed by a kilonova seen across the spectrum. The event showed that gravitational waves travel at the speed of light to about one part in a quadrillion and that mergers forge heavy elements such as gold.
