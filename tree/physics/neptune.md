Urbain Le Verrier and John Couch Adams independently calculated, from irregularities in the motion of Uranus, where an unseen planet should be. Johann Galle at the Berlin Observatory found Neptune on 23 September 1846, within about one degree of Le Verrier's predicted position. It was a major success of Newtonian gravity and of mathematical prediction in astronomy.
