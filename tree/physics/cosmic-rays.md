Victor Hess flew in balloons up to 5.3 km in 1912 and found that ionizing radiation increases with altitude, so it must come from space. Cosmic rays are mostly high-energy protons and nuclei, and they led to the discovery of the positron, the muon and the pion before high-energy accelerators were built.
