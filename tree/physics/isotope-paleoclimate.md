Harold Urey showed in 1947 that the ratio of oxygen isotopes in carbonate shells records the temperature of the water in which they grew. Cesare Emiliani applied it to deep-sea cores in 1955 and found many glacial cycles, and Willi Dansgaard and others read ice cores the same way, giving records of past climate that reach back hundreds of thousands of years.
