Harold Urey calculated in 1947 that the ratio of oxygen isotopes in carbonate depends on the water temperature, and his group measured shell temperatures in 1951. Cesare Emiliani applied the method to deep-sea cores in 1955 and found many glacial cycles, and Willi Dansgaard and others read ice cores the same way, extending climate records back hundreds of thousands of years.
