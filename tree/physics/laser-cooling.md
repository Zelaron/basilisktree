In 1985 Steven Chu and colleagues slowed atoms with laser beams tuned just below an atomic resonance, so that atoms moving against a beam see it Doppler-shifted into resonance, reaching a few hundred microkelvin. Magneto-optical traps (1987) and the work of William Phillips and Claude Cohen-Tannoudji extended the method, which underlies atomic clocks and Bose-Einstein condensates.
