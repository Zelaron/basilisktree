Augustin-Louis Cauchy introduced the stress tensor and the equations of elastic equilibrium in 1822, building on Claude-Louis Navier's general theory of 1821. Siméon Denis Poisson (1829) showed that elastic solids carry two kinds of waves, which seismologists later identified as P and S waves. The theory underlies structural engineering and continuum mechanics.
