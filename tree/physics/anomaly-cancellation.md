Michael Green and John Schwarz showed in 1984 that the gauge and gravitational anomalies of ten-dimensional superstring theory cancel only for the gauge groups SO(32) and E8×E8, the latter realized by the heterotic string of 1985. This made the theory consistent and led to rapid growth of research on string theory as a candidate theory of quantum gravity, which remains untested.
