Michael Green and John Schwarz showed in 1984 that the gauge and gravitational anomalies of ten-dimensional superstring theory cancel for the gauge group SO(32), and soon after for E8×E8, which makes the theory consistent. This led to rapid growth of research on string theory as a candidate theory of quantum gravity, which remains experimentally untested.
