The COBE satellite's FIRAS instrument (John Mather) showed in 1990 that the cosmic microwave background has a perfect black-body spectrum at about 2.7 K, and its DMR instrument (George Smoot) detected in 1992 temperature ripples of about one part in 100,000. These are the seeds of galaxies and clusters, and they confirmed the Big Bang picture and the growth of structure.
