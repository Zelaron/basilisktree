Kenneth Wilson showed in 1971 how to describe a system by averaging out its short-distance details step by step, so that physics at different scales is linked by flows in the space of theories. Building on Leo Kadanoff's block-spin idea, it explained why very different systems share the same critical behaviour and became central to field theory and condensed matter physics.
