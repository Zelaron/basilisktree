Osborne Reynolds saw in 1883 that pipe flow turns turbulent at high speed, but the threshold stayed unclear. In 2011 Kerstin Avila, Björn Hof and colleagues measured how turbulent puffs decay and split and found that turbulence becomes sustained at a Reynolds number of about 2,040, where splitting wins. Experiments in 2024 confirmed that the transition is of the directed-percolation type.
