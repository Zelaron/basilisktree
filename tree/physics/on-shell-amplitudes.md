Zvi Bern, Lance Dixon and David Kosower showed in 1994 that unitarity cuts let gauge-theory scattering amplitudes be built from simpler on-shell ones, and in 2005 Ruth Britto, Freddy Cachazo, Bo Feng and Edward Witten found a recursion relation that does so without Feynman diagrams. The methods reveal hidden simplicity and speed up collider predictions.
