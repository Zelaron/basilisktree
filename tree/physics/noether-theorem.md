Emmy Noether proved in 1918 that every continuous symmetry of a physical system's action corresponds to a conserved quantity: symmetry under shifts in time gives conservation of energy, under shifts in space momentum, and under rotations angular momentum. The theorem ties symmetry to conservation laws throughout modern physics.
