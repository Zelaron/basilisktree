Machine-learned models trained on decades of reanalysis data, Huawei's Pangu-Weather (Kaifeng Bi and colleagues) and Google DeepMind's GraphCast (Remi Lam and colleagues), matched or beat the best physics-based medium-range forecasts in 2023 at a small fraction of the computing cost. ECMWF began running its own AI forecasting system operationally in 2025.
