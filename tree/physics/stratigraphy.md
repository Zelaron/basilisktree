Nicolas Steno's De solido (1669) set out three rules for reading rock layers: sediments are deposited in order with the oldest at the bottom, they start out horizontal, and they extend sideways until they thin out or meet a barrier. He had also shown that tongue stones are fossil shark teeth. The rules let geologists reconstruct the sequence of past events from rocks.
