Einstein added a constant term to his field equations in 1917 so that they would allow a static universe. Willem de Sitter found a matter-free solution the same year, which was later understood to expand. Einstein dropped the term after the expansion of the universe was found, and it returned as a candidate for dark energy after 1998.
