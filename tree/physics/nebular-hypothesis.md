Immanuel Kant (1755) and Pierre-Simon Laplace (1796) proposed that the Sun and planets formed from a rotating cloud of gas that flattened into a disk as it contracted. This accounts for the planets' nearly circular, coplanar orbits and common direction of motion. Images of protoplanetary disks around young stars since the 1990s support the picture.
