Philip Anderson proposed in 1973 the resonating valence bond state, in which the spins of a frustrated magnet pair into singlets that fluctuate between arrangements, so that the system never orders even at zero temperature. He revived the idea in 1987 as a possible origin of high-temperature superconductivity. Candidate materials are still debated.
