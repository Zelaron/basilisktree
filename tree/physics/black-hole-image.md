The Event Horizon Telescope, an array of radio dishes across Earth linked by very-long-baseline interferometry, published in April 2019 the first image of a black hole, the ring and dark shadow of the 6.5-billion-solar-mass black hole in the galaxy M87. In May 2022 it released an image of Sagittarius A*, the Milky Way's black hole. Both are consistent with general relativity.
