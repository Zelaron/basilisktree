Carl Anderson and Seth Neddermeyer found in 1936, in cosmic-ray tracks in a cloud chamber, a particle with the charge of the electron and about 200 times its mass. It was first taken for Yukawa's nuclear-force particle, but it turned out to be a heavy, unstable cousin of the electron, the first sign that the particles of matter come in repeated families.
