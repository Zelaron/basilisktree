Carl Anderson and Seth Neddermeyer found in 1936, in cosmic-ray tracks in a cloud chamber, a particle with the electron's charge and a mass between those of the electron and proton, now known to be 207 electron masses. First taken for Yukawa's nuclear-force particle, it is a heavy, unstable cousin of the electron, the first sign that matter comes in repeated families.
