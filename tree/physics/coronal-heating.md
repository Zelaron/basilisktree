The Sun's corona is above a million degrees, hundreds of times hotter than the visible surface, as spectroscopy showed in 1939–1943. Is it heated by magnetic waves or by countless small reconnection events called nanoflares? Solar Orbiter, Parker Solar Probe and the Inouye telescope now see evidence for both, and an energy budget that closes for the quiet Sun and active regions would settle it.
