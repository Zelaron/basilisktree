Gilbert Walker found in the 1920s that surface pressure over the Pacific and Indian Oceans rises and falls in opposition, the Southern Oscillation. In 1969 Jacob Bjerknes explained that it is coupled to El Niño warming of the eastern Pacific by a feedback between trade winds and sea surface temperature. ENSO is the largest natural source of year-to-year climate variability.
