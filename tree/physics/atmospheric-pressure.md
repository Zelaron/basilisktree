Torricelli's mercury barometer (1643) showed that air has weight and that a vacuum can exist above a liquid column. In 1648 Pascal's brother-in-law carried a barometer up the Puy de Dôme and found that the column fell with height, and Otto von Guericke built air pumps in the 1650s. Boyle's gas law followed in 1662.
