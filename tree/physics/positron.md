Carl Anderson photographed in 1932, in a cloud chamber exposed to cosmic rays, the track of a particle with the mass of an electron and a positive charge. It was the antiparticle that Dirac's equation had demanded and the first antimatter observed. Positron emission tomography now uses positrons for medical imaging.
