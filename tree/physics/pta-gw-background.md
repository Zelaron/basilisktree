In June 2023 the NANOGrav, European (EPTA), Parkes (PPTA) and Chinese (CPTA) pulsar timing array collaborations reported correlated shifts in the arrival times of pulses from millisecond pulsars, with the angular pattern predicted by Hellings and Downs, evidence for a background of nanohertz gravitational waves. The source is probably supermassive black hole binaries.
