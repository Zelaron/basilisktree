Anne L'Huillier showed in 1987 that intense laser light in a gas produces very high harmonics. In 2001 Pierre Agostini's group made trains of pulses lasting about 250 attoseconds and Ferenc Krausz's group isolated single pulses of 650 attoseconds. Such flashes can follow the motion of electrons in atoms and molecules, and the three shared the 2023 Nobel Prize in Physics.
