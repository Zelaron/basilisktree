Laplace's Traité de mécanique céleste (five volumes, 1799-1825) used perturbation theory to show that the long-term irregularities in the motions of Jupiter, Saturn and the Moon follow from Newtonian gravity. It gathered the mathematics of the solar system into one work and established the standard methods of celestial mechanics.
