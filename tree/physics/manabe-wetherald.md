Syukuro Manabe and Richard Wetherald published in 1967 a one-dimensional model of the atmosphere that treated radiation, convection and water vapour feedback together. It found that doubling carbon dioxide would warm the surface by about 2 degrees Celsius and cool the stratosphere, as later observed. Manabe shared the 2021 Nobel Prize in Physics.
