Ejnar Hertzsprung (1911) and Henry Norris Russell (1913) plotted the brightness of stars against their colour or spectral type and found that most lie on a diagonal band, the main sequence, with a separate group of giants above it. Annie Jump Cannon's spectral classes (OBAFGKM) supplied the spectral types. The diagram became the basic tool of stellar astrophysics.
