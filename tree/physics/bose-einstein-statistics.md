In 1924 Satyendra Nath Bose derived Planck's law by counting photons as indistinguishable particles, and Einstein extended the method to atoms. The resulting Bose-Einstein statistics describes particles of integer spin and predicts that they can pile into a single lowest state at low temperature, as in the condensate observed in 1995.
