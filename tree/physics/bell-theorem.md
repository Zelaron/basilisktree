John Stewart Bell proved in 1964 that no theory with local hidden variables can reproduce all the predictions of quantum mechanics: correlations between measurements on entangled particles can exceed a bound, the Bell inequality. This turned the debate between Einstein and Bohr over hidden variables into a question that experiments can decide.
