Edwin Hubble identified Cepheid variable stars in the Andromeda nebula and showed, in a result announced on 1 January 1925, that it lies far outside the Milky Way. This settled the 1920 Shapley-Curtis debate in favour of island universes and showed that the Milky Way is one galaxy among very many, building on Harlow Shapley's 1918 measurement of its size.
