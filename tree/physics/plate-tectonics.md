Dan McKenzie and Robert Parker (1967), Jason Morgan (1968) and Xavier Le Pichon (1968) described Earth's surface as rigid plates moving over the globe and meeting at ridges, trenches and transform faults, building on Tuzo Wilson's transform faults (1965). The theory explains earthquakes, volcanoes and mountain building and organizes the earth sciences.
