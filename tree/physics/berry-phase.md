Michael Berry showed in 1984 that when a quantum system is carried slowly around a loop in parameter space, its state acquires, besides the usual dynamical phase, a geometric phase that depends only on the path. Shivaramakrishnan Pancharatnam had found an optical analogue in 1956. Berry phases appear in molecules, electron bands and topological matter.
