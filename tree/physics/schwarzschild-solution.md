Karl Schwarzschild found, within weeks of the publication of general relativity and while serving on the Russian front, the exact gravitational field outside a spherical mass, published in 1916. It was the first exact solution of Einstein's equations, and it contains the radius that later became understood as the event horizon of a black hole.
