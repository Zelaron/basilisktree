IceCube, a cubic kilometre of Antarctic ice instrumented with light sensors and led by Francis Halzen, reported in 2013 evidence for neutrinos from beyond the solar system: 28 events with energies up to about a petaelectronvolt. Neutrinos were later traced to the blazar TXS 0506+056 (2018) and to the Milky Way, opening neutrino astronomy. Halzen received the 2026 Nobel Prize.
