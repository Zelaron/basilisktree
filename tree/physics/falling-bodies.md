In Two New Sciences (1638) Galileo showed that a body falling from rest covers distances proportional to the square of the elapsed time, and that a projectile follows a parabola, the result of steady horizontal motion combined with uniform downward acceleration. His inclined-plane experiments replaced Aristotle's account of motion and prepared the ground for Newton's laws.
