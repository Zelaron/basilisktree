Duncan Lorimer and colleagues found in 2007, in archival pulsar survey data from the Parkes telescope, a radio flash lasting about five milliseconds from far outside the Galaxy. Thousands of such fast radio bursts have since been found, some of them repeating, and in 2020 a magnetar in the Milky Way produced one, tying at least some to neutron stars.
