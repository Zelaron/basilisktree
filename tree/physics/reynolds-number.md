In 1883 Osborne Reynolds injected dye into water flowing in a glass pipe and showed that the flow turns turbulent when one dimensionless number, speed times diameter over kinematic viscosity, passes a threshold, near 2,000 with disturbances and up to 13,000 in very quiet water. The number shows when flows of different sizes behave alike, which allows scale-model testing.
