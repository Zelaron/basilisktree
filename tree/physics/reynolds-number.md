In 1883 Osborne Reynolds injected dye into water flowing through a glass pipe and showed that the flow changes from smooth to turbulent when one dimensionless number, speed times diameter divided by kinematic viscosity, passes a critical value of about 2,000. The Reynolds number tells engineers when flows of different sizes behave alike, which makes scale-model testing possible.
