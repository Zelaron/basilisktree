Jacob Bekenstein proposed in 1972-73 that a black hole has an entropy proportional to the area of its event horizon. In 1973 James Bardeen, Brandon Carter and Stephen Hawking derived four laws of black hole mechanics that parallel the laws of thermodynamics. Hawking's discovery of black hole radiation in 1974 showed that the analogy is physical.
