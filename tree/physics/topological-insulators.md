Charles Kane and Eugene Mele (2005), and Andrei Bernevig, Taylor Hughes and Shoucheng Zhang (2006), predicted insulators whose bulk has an energy gap but whose edges or surfaces carry protected conducting states. Laurens Molenkamp's group observed the edge states in HgTe quantum wells in 2007, and three-dimensional examples followed in 2008-09.
