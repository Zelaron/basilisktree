Peter Franken and colleagues sent a ruby laser beam through a quartz crystal in 1961 and saw light come out at exactly twice the frequency, the first observation of second-harmonic generation. Nicolaas Bloembergen then built the theory of nonlinear interactions of light with matter. Nonlinear optics underlies laser frequency conversion, ultrafast pulses and optical communications.
