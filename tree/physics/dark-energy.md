Why is the expansion of the universe accelerating (1998)? A cosmological constant fits the data, but naive quantum estimates of vacuum energy exceed it by up to 120 orders of magnitude, and DESI data (2024–2025) hint that dark energy weakens over time. Showing whether it is constant, and explaining its value, would settle it; DESI finished its five-year survey in April 2026, with full results expected in 2027.
