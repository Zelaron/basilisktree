Philip Anderson showed in 1958 that in a sufficiently disordered medium, quantum waves such as electrons can be trapped in a small region and stop diffusing, so that a conductor can become an insulator at zero temperature. The effect applies to any wave and has been observed with light, sound and cold atoms.
