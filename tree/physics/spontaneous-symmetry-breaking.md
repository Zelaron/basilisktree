Yoichiro Nambu, with Giovanni Jona-Lasinio, and independently Jeffrey Goldstone showed in 1960-61 that the laws of a system can have a symmetry that its lowest-energy state lacks, as in a superconductor, and that breaking a continuous symmetry produces massless Goldstone bosons. The idea led to the Higgs mechanism of 1964 and to the origin of particle masses in the Standard Model.
