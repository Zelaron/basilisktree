James Cronin and Val Fitch found in 1964 that neutral kaons, which should decay in a way symmetric under combined charge conjugation and parity (CP), sometimes decay in a way that violates it, in about 2 of every 1,000 decays. Matter and antimatter therefore behave slightly differently, a necessary condition for a universe with more matter than antimatter.
