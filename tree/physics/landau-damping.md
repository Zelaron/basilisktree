Lev Landau showed in 1946 that a wave in a collisionless plasma can die away without any energy lost to collisions, because particles moving at nearly the wave's speed exchange energy with it. The effect is a basic part of kinetic plasma theory, was confirmed in laboratory experiments in 1964, and has analogues in galactic dynamics.
