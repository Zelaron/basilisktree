Penrose (1969) conjectured that singularities formed by realistic gravitational collapse are hidden inside black holes (weak censorship); a strong version says Einstein's equations are generically deterministic, with no continuation past a Cauchy horizon. Both are open beyond symmetric models, and Dafermos and Luk (2017) showed that the C0 form of strong censorship fails. It is problem 15 on Barry Simon's 1984 list.
