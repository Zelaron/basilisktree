Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar showed in 1930-31, combining special relativity with Fermi-Dirac statistics, that a white dwarf has a maximum mass because degenerate electrons cannot support more; the limit is about 1.4 solar masses for a carbon or oxygen star. Heavier stellar cores must collapse further, and white dwarfs reaching it are a leading model for Type Ia supernovae.
