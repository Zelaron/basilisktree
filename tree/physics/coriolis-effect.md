Gaspard-Gustave de Coriolis showed in 1835 that Newton's laws in a rotating frame need an extra term that makes a moving body appear to curve sideways. On Earth it steers large-scale winds and ocean currents and sets the spin of cyclones, and Foucault's pendulum displayed it directly in 1851.
