Klaus Hasselmann developed statistical fingerprint methods in 1979-93 to separate human influence on climate from natural variability, and Benjamin Santer and others applied them to observed temperature patterns. The 1995 IPCC report concluded that the balance of evidence suggests a discernible human influence on global climate. Hasselmann shared the 2021 Nobel Prize.
