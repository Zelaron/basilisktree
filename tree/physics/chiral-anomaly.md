Stephen Adler, and independently John Bell and Roman Jackiw, showed in 1969 that chiral symmetry, a symmetry of the classical theory of massless fermions, is broken by quantum effects when the particles are coupled to gauge fields. The anomaly explains the decay of the neutral pion into two photons and constrains which gauge theories are consistent.
