Bruno Pontecorvo proposed neutrino oscillations in 1957. Super-Kamiokande (Takaaki Kajita) saw atmospheric muon neutrinos vanish in 1998, and the Sudbury Neutrino Observatory (Arthur McDonald) showed in 2001-02 that solar neutrinos change flavour, solving the problem Raymond Davis and John Bahcall raised. Neutrinos therefore have mass, which the Standard Model lacked.
