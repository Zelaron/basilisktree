The Oxford Calculators of Merton College (about 1335) proved that a body accelerating uniformly covers the same distance as one moving at a constant speed equal to the average of its initial and final speeds. Nicole Oresme gave a graphical proof around 1350 by plotting speed against time, and Galileo used the result in his law of falling bodies.
