Why does a supercooled liquid become rigid without crystallizing, and is there an ideal glass transition with vanishing configurational entropy beneath the laboratory one (Kauzmann, 1948)? Philip Anderson called it the deepest unsolved problem in solid-state theory (1995). Mean-field theory is exact in infinite dimensions (2014), but simulations reaching far lower temperatures have not settled three dimensions.
