Josh Deutsch (1991) and Mark Srednicki (1994) argued that in a chaotic quantum system each individual energy eigenstate already looks thermal for local measurements, with expectation values that depend smoothly on energy. This hypothesis explains how an isolated quantum system can thermalize, and it has been tested numerically and in cold-atom experiments.
