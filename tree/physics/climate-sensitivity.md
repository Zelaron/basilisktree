How much will Earth eventually warm if atmospheric CO2 doubles? The 1979 Charney report estimated 1.5–4.5 °C; multiple lines of evidence have narrowed it to a likely 2.5–4 °C (IPCC, 2021), with cloud feedbacks the main uncertainty. Narrowing the range to about half a degree, which would sharpen every carbon budget, is the goal.
