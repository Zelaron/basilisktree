Sam Edwards and Philip Anderson proposed in 1975 a model of magnets with random interactions, the spin glass. Giorgio Parisi solved its mean-field version in 1979 with replica symmetry breaking; Francesco Guerra and Michel Talagrand proved his free-energy formula in 2003-06. The methods are used in optimization and neural networks, and Parisi shared the 2021 Nobel Prize.
