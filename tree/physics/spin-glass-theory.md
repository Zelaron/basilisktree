Sam Edwards and Philip Anderson introduced in 1975 a model of magnets with random interactions, the spin glass. Giorgio Parisi solved its mean-field version in 1979 using replica symmetry breaking, a hierarchy of states that Michel Talagrand proved correct in 2006. The methods are used in optimization and neural networks, and Parisi shared the 2021 Nobel Prize.
