William Rowan Hamilton (1834-35) recast mechanics as first-order equations for positions and momenta, with a single energy function, the Hamiltonian, generating the motion. Carl Jacobi added the Hamilton-Jacobi theory. The phase-space picture became the framework of statistical mechanics, and its Poisson brackets are the classical model for quantum commutators.
