John Clauser and Stuart Freedman made the first test of a Bell inequality in 1972, and in 1982 Alain Aspect closed an important loophole by switching the polarizer settings while the photons were in flight. The inequalities were violated, as quantum mechanics predicts. Loophole-free tests followed in 2015, and Aspect, Clauser and Anton Zeilinger shared the 2022 Nobel Prize.
