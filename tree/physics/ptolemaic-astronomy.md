Claudius Ptolemy's Almagest (about 150 CE) set out a complete geocentric model of the Sun, Moon and planets built from circles, epicycles and the equant, with tables for computing their positions. It remained the standard astronomical text of the Islamic world and Europe for about 1,400 years, until Copernicus and Kepler.
