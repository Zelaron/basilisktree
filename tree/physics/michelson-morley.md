In 1887 Albert Michelson and Edward Morley compared the travel time of light along two perpendicular arms of an interferometer and found no difference, whichever way the apparatus pointed relative to Earth's motion. The null result ruled out a detectable ether wind and set the stage for special relativity. Descendants of the interferometer detect gravitational waves.
