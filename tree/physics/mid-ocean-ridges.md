Marie Tharp turned echo-sounding profiles of the North Atlantic into maps, and in 1952 noticed a rift valley running down the crest of the Mid-Atlantic Ridge. With Bruce Heezen and Maurice Ewing she showed by 1957 that the ridge is part of a mountain system encircling the globe. The rift valley supported the idea that the ocean floor is spreading apart.
