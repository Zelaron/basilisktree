Chen-Ning Yang and Robert Mills proposed in 1954 a field theory in which a symmetry group acts differently at each point of spacetime, which requires massless vector fields that interact with themselves. Their non-abelian gauge theory generalizes electromagnetism and underlies the electroweak theory and quantum chromodynamics, so it is the framework of the Standard Model.
