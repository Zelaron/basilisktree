Walter Baade and Fritz Zwicky proposed in 1934 that supernovae are a distinct class of stellar explosion, far brighter than novae, in which an ordinary star collapses into a neutron star. They also suggested that supernovae are a source of cosmic rays. Pulsars, discovered in 1967, are such neutron stars.
