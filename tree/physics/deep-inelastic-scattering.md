Experiments at SLAC led by Jerome Friedman, Henry Kendall and Richard Taylor scattered high-energy electrons from protons in 1968-69 and found that they bounce off small, hard constituents, as James Bjorken had predicted and Richard Feynman interpreted in terms of point-like partons. The partons were identified with quarks, the first direct evidence that quarks are real.
