Bernard Brunhes found in 1906 that some volcanic rocks in France are magnetized opposite to today's field, and Motonori Matuyama showed in 1929 from basalts in Japan and Manchuria that the field had reversed in the early Pleistocene. Earth's field has reversed polarity many times at irregular intervals; the last reversal was about 780,000 years ago.
