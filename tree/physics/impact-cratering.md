Eugene Shoemaker and Edward Chao showed in 1960 that Meteor Crater in Arizona was made by an impact, using the high-pressure mineral coesite found in its rocks and the structure of its rim. This established impact as a geological process and showed that most craters on the Moon are impact scars.
