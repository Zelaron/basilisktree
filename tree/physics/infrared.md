William Herschel passed sunlight through a prism in 1800 and placed thermometers along the spectrum. The one just beyond the red end showed the largest rise in temperature, revealing an invisible form of radiant heat. It was the first discovery of radiation outside the visible range, and Johann Ritter found ultraviolet beyond the violet end in 1801.
