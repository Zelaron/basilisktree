Daniel Tsui and Horst Störmer, with crystals grown by Arthur Gossard, found in 1982 Hall plateaus at fractional fillings such as one third in very clean two-dimensional electron gases. Robert Laughlin explained them in 1983 as a new quantum liquid whose quasiparticles carry a fraction of the electron's charge. Their anyon statistics were observed directly in 2020.
