Louis Agassiz announced in 1837 that boulders, polished rock and moraines across northern Europe were left by ice sheets that once covered the land, and developed the case in Études sur les glaciers (1840). Karl Schimper coined the term Eiszeit, ice age, and Jean de Charpentier had argued for former glaciers in the Alps. It showed that Earth's climate has changed greatly.
