Arthur Eddington's The Internal Constitution of the Stars (1926) set out how gas pressure and radiation pressure balance gravity inside a star and how energy flows outward. It predicted a relation between a star's mass and luminosity that observations confirmed, and it framed the question of the stellar energy source that fusion later answered.
