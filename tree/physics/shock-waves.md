In 1887 Ernst Mach and Peter Salcher photographed the shock waves around a supersonic bullet and showed that the cone angle gives the speed relative to sound, the ratio now called the Mach number. William Rankine (1870) and Pierre-Henri Hugoniot (1887) derived the conservation laws that relate the gas on the two sides of a shock.
