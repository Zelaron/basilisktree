Pierre Perrault published in 1674 several years of rainfall and flow measurements for the upper Seine, showing that the rain falling on the catchment was about six times what the river carried away, so rain alone can feed rivers. Edme Mariotte's Seine measurements agreed, and Edmond Halley estimated evaporation from the sea (1687). Quantitative hydrology began here.
