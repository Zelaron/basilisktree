Herb Dragert, Garry Rogers and colleagues used GPS in 2001 to detect a silent slip lasting weeks on the fault beneath Cascadia, and in 2002 Kazushige Obara found accompanying tremor in Japan. Slow earthquakes show that faults slip at a wide range of speeds, and they have changed how stress buildup on subduction zones is studied.
