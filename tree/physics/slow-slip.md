Herb Dragert, Kelin Wang and Thomas James used GPS in 2001 to detect a silent slip lasting weeks on the fault beneath Cascadia, and Kazushige Obara found tremor in Japan in 2002. Garry Rogers and Dragert tied slip and tremor together in 2003. Slow earthquakes show that faults slip at a wide range of speeds, which changed how subduction zones are studied.
