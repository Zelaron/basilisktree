Augustin-Jean Fresnel's 1818 memoir on diffraction gave a wave theory that predicted the patterns of light around obstacles. Poisson objected that it implied a bright spot at the centre of a round shadow, and Arago found it. Fresnel's proposal in 1821 that light waves are transverse explained polarization, which Étienne-Louis Malus had discovered in 1808.
