In 2010 Randolf Pohl and colleagues measured the proton's charge radius with muonic hydrogen and found it about 4 percent smaller than values from hydrogen spectroscopy and electron scattering. In 2019 the PRad experiment and a hydrogen measurement by Bezginov and colleagues favoured the small value, about 0.84 femtometres, now adopted by CODATA. The puzzle is largely resolved.
