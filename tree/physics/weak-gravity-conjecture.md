In any consistent theory of quantum gravity, gravity should be the weakest force: some particle must have a charge-to-mass ratio at least that of an extremal black hole (Arkani-Hamed, Motl, Nicolis and Vafa, 2006). It holds in every known string compactification and has black-hole and unitarity arguments behind it; a general proof, starting with superstring theory, or a counterexample would settle it.
