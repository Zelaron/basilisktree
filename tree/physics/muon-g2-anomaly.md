Brookhaven (2001) and Fermilab (2021-25) measured the muon's magnetic anomaly and found it above Standard Model predictions based on electron-positron data. Lattice QCD calculations (BMW, 2020) raised the predicted value, and Fermilab's final result of June 2025, at 127 parts per billion, agrees with the 2025 theory value. The electron-positron data sets still disagree.
