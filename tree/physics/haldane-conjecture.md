Haldane (1983) predicted that antiferromagnetic Heisenberg chains of integer spin have an energy gap above a unique ground state, while half-integer chains do not. Affleck and Lieb proved a form of the half-integer side (1986), and experiments since 1986 and numerics confirm the spin-1 gap, but no proof for the spin-1 chain existed before a 2026 claim, which awaits independent review.
