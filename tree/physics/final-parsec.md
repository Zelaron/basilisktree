When galaxies merge, their central black holes sink to within about a parsec of each other, where stellar encounters seem too few and gravitational waves too weak to finish the merger (Begelman, Blandford and Rees, 1980). Triaxial galaxy shapes, gas or dark matter may close the gap; the nanohertz gravitational-wave background found in 2023 suggests binaries do get closer, but which mechanism operates is unsettled.
