Does the two-dimensional Hubbard model, proposed by Anderson (1987) as the minimal model of the copper-oxide superconductors, have a d-wave superconducting ground state? Large simulations find stripes without superconductivity when electrons hop only to nearest neighbours (2020) and superconductivity once next-nearest hopping is added (2024). Agreement across methods on the doped phase diagram would settle it.
