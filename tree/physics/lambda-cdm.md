Measurements of the cosmic microwave background by BOOMERanG (2000), WMAP (2003) and Planck (2013-18) confirmed the ΛCDM model and fixed its parameters: a spatially flat universe, 13.8 billion years old, with about 5 percent ordinary matter, 27 percent cold dark matter and 68 percent dark energy. It fits many data sets with six parameters and is the standard model of cosmology.
