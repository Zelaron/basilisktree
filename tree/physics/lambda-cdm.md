Measurements of the cosmic microwave background by BOOMERanG (2000), WMAP (2003) and Planck (2013-18) led to the ΛCDM model, in which the universe is spatially flat, about 5 percent ordinary matter, 27 percent cold dark matter and 68 percent dark energy, and 13.8 billion years old. It fits a wide range of data with six parameters and is the standard model of cosmology.
