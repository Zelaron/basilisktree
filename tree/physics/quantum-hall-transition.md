At the transition between quantum Hall plateaus the localization length diverges with an exponent ν. Simulations of the Chalker–Coddington network model (1988) give about 2.6, experiments about 2.38, and no accepted field theory explains either. Identifying the critical theory, or showing that interactions or disorder geometry account for the difference, would settle it.
