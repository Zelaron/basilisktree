Is maximal (N = 8) supergravity in four dimensions free of ultraviolet divergences at every loop order? If so, a point-particle quantum theory of gravity would exist without strings. Explicit calculations show finiteness through four loops (2009), symmetry allows the first divergence at seven loops, and a seven-loop calculation, or a symmetry argument forbidding the divergence, would settle it.
