Alan Guth proposed in 1981 that the early universe went through a brief period of exponential expansion; Alexei Starobinsky (1980), Andrei Linde, Andreas Albrecht and Paul Steinhardt (1982) developed related versions. Inflation explains the universe's uniformity and flatness and seeds galaxies with quantum fluctuations. It fits the cosmic microwave background but remains unproven.
