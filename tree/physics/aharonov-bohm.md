Yakir Aharonov and David Bohm showed in 1959 that a charged particle's quantum phase is shifted by electromagnetic potentials even where the fields vanish, so interference fringes move when magnetic flux is enclosed between the paths. Akira Tonomura's electron holography confirmed it in 1986. The effect shows that potentials are physically meaningful in quantum theory.
