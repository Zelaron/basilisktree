Senthil and co-workers (2004) proposed that a two-dimensional quantum magnet can pass directly between two differently ordered phases through a continuous transition beyond Landau's theory, with fractionalized excitations at the critical point. Simulations since 2007 show near-critical behaviour, but whether the transition is truly continuous, weakly first order, or governed by complex fixed points is unsettled.
