Andrey Kolmogorov proposed in 1941 that in fully developed turbulence energy cascades from large eddies to ever smaller ones until viscosity dissipates it, with statistically universal small scales. The theory predicts an energy spectrum falling as the wavenumber to the power of minus five thirds, which experiments confirm closely. Lewis Fry Richardson had described the cascade in 1922.
