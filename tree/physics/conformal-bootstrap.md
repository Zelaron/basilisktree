Riccardo Rattazzi, Slava Rychkov, Erik Tonni and Alessandro Vichi revived in 2008 the idea that consistency conditions on conformal field theories, such as crossing symmetry and unitarity, can by themselves bound which theories exist. Numerical bootstrap calculations (2012-16) gave the most precise exponents of the three-dimensional Ising universality class.
