Peter Sweet and Eugene Parker described in 1957-58 how oppositely directed magnetic field lines in a conducting plasma can break and rejoin in a thin current sheet, turning magnetic energy into heat and fast particles. James Dungey applied the idea to Earth's magnetosphere in 1961. Reconnection powers solar flares and geomagnetic substorms and causes disruptions in tokamaks.
