A US National Academy of Sciences panel chaired by Jule Charney reviewed in 1979 the climate models of Syukuro Manabe and James Hansen and estimated that doubling carbon dioxide would warm the world by 3 degrees Celsius, with an uncertainty of 1.5 degrees either way. The range of 1.5 to 4.5 degrees stood as the standard estimate of climate sensitivity for about four decades.
