Michael Faraday found in 1845 that a magnetic field along the path of polarized light rotates the plane of polarization as the light passes through glass. It was the first experimental evidence that light and magnetism are related, twenty years before Maxwell showed light to be an electromagnetic wave. The effect is used in optical isolators and magnetic sensing.
