Brian Josephson predicted in 1962 that a supercurrent can flow between two superconductors separated by a thin insulating barrier, and that a voltage across the junction makes it oscillate at a frequency proportional to the voltage. Anderson and Rowell observed the current in 1963. Josephson junctions underlie SQUIDs, the voltage standard and superconducting qubits.
