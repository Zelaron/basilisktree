Vera Rubin and Kent Ford measured from 1970 the speeds of stars and gas at different distances from the centres of spiral galaxies, and Albert Bosma traced hydrogen gas in outer disks with radio telescopes (1978). The rotation speeds stay flat far beyond the visible edge, so galaxies hold much more mass than their stars and gas show, a main piece of evidence for dark matter.
