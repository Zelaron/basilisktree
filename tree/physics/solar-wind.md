Eugene Parker argued in 1958 that the Sun's hot corona cannot stay in static equilibrium and must expand into space as a supersonic flow, the solar wind, which drags the Sun's magnetic field into a spiral. Ludwig Biermann had inferred a continuous outflow from comet tails in 1951. Mariner 2 measured the wind directly in 1962.
