Alfred Wegener proposed in 1912 that the continents were once joined in a single supercontinent, later called Pangaea, and have drifted apart. He cited the fit of the coastlines, matching fossils and rock formations on both sides of the Atlantic, and ancient glacial traces. Most geologists rejected it for lack of a mechanism, and plate tectonics vindicated it in the 1960s.
