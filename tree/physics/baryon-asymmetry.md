Why does the universe contain matter but almost no antimatter, about six baryons for every ten billion photons? Sakharov (1967) listed the conditions any explanation must meet, but Standard Model CP violation is far too weak and its electroweak transition is a smooth crossover. Leptogenesis and electroweak baryogenesis with new particles remain the leading candidates; none is confirmed.
