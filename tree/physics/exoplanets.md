Aleksander Wolszczan and Dale Frail found planets around the pulsar PSR B1257+12 in 1992, and in 1995 Michel Mayor and Didier Queloz found 51 Pegasi b, a giant planet orbiting a Sun-like star every four days, from the star's periodic Doppler shift. Thousands of exoplanets are now known, and planets are common and varied. Mayor and Queloz shared the 2019 Nobel Prize in Physics.
