Juan Maldacena proposed in 1997 that string theory in anti-de Sitter space is equivalent to a quantum field theory without gravity on its boundary, and in 1998 Gubser, Klebanov, Polyakov and Edward Witten made it precise. The conjectured duality has thousands of checks, is a model of quantum gravity, and gives tools for strongly coupled systems such as the quark-gluon plasma.
