Wilhelm Lenz proposed in 1920 a model of spins on a lattice that prefer to align with their neighbours, and his student Ernst Ising showed in 1925 that the one-dimensional chain has no phase transition. Onsager solved the two-dimensional case exactly in 1944, and the model became the standard test case for phase transitions and critical phenomena.
