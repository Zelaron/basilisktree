William Huggins reported in 1864 that some nebulae have bright emission lines, so they are clouds of glowing gas, and that stars contain familiar elements such as hydrogen, sodium and iron. Angelo Secchi classified stellar spectra into types, and helium was identified in the Sun's spectrum in 1868, 27 years before it was found on Earth. Astrophysics began here.
