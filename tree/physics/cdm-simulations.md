Marc Davis, George Efstathiou, Carlos Frenk and Simon White ran N-body simulations in 1985 in which cold dark matter, slow-moving particles that interact only through gravity, grows into the filaments, clusters and voids of the observed galaxy distribution. Navarro, Frenk and White found in 1996 that the resulting halos share a universal density profile.
