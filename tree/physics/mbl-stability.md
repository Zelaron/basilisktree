Is many-body localization, in which a disordered interacting quantum system never thermalizes, a true phase of matter in arbitrarily large systems? Imbrie's one-dimensional proof (2016) rests on an unproven assumption, rare thermal regions can set off avalanches (2017), and numerics and cold-atom experiments through 2025 show finite-size drifts, so the existence of the phase remains open.
