Irving Langmuir named the ionized state of gas plasma in 1928 and, with Lewi Tonks, studied its collective oscillations, now called Langmuir waves. Debye shielding (1923) shows how a plasma screens electric fields over short distances. Plasma physics is the basis of research on fusion, the Sun and stars, and the space environment.
