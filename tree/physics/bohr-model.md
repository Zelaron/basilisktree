Niels Bohr proposed in 1913 that electrons in an atom move in fixed orbits with quantized angular momentum and emit or absorb light only when they jump between orbits. The model reproduced the hydrogen spectrum, including the Balmer series known since 1885, and gave the Rydberg constant in terms of fundamental constants. It was the first quantum model of atomic structure.
