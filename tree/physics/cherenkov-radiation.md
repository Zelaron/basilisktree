Pavel Cherenkov studied in 1934 a faint blue glow from liquids exposed to gamma rays. In 1937 Ilya Frank and Igor Tamm explained that a charged particle moving faster than light does in a medium emits a cone of light, the optical counterpart of a sonic boom. Cherenkov detectors identify particles and detect neutrinos in water and ice.
