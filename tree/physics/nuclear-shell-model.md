Maria Goeppert Mayer and, independently, Hans Jensen with Otto Haxel and Hans Suess showed in 1949 that nuclei have proton and neutron shells that fill at the magic numbers 2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82 and 126 once strong spin-orbit coupling is included. The model explains the stability of magic nuclei and the spins of ground states. Mayer and Jensen shared the 1963 Nobel Prize.
