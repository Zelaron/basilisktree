Walter Grotrian (1939) and Bengt Edlén (about 1940) showed that the mysterious emission lines of the solar corona, once attributed to an element called coronium, come from iron, calcium and nickel stripped of many electrons. Such ionization needs temperatures above a million kelvin, far hotter than the Sun's surface at about 5,800 K, which poses the coronal heating problem.
