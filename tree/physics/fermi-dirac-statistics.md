Enrico Fermi and Paul Dirac independently derived in 1926 the statistics of identical particles that obey the exclusion principle. Fermi-Dirac statistics govern electrons in metals and semiconductors and explain the degeneracy pressure that holds up white dwarfs and neutron stars. Particles that follow it are called fermions.
