Andrei Sakharov listed in 1967 three conditions for a universe that starts with equal matter and antimatter to end up with more matter: baryon number must be violated, charge conjugation and CP symmetry must both be violated, and there must be a departure from thermal equilibrium. The conditions frame all theories of the origin of the matter-antimatter asymmetry.
