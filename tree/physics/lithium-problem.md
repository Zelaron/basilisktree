Big Bang nucleosynthesis, using the baryon density measured from the cosmic microwave background (2003), predicts about three times more lithium-7 than old halo stars show. Nuclear-physics fixes have been largely excluded, so either stars destroyed their lithium or new physics is needed. A stellar model that depletes lithium uniformly along the Spite plateau, confirmed by data, would settle it.
