Louis de Broglie proposed in 1924 that particles have a wavelength h/p, where h is Planck's constant and p the momentum. Clinton Davisson and Lester Germer observed electrons diffracting from a nickel crystal in 1927, and George Paget Thomson saw the same effect independently. The idea led Schrödinger to wave mechanics.
