Two teams led by Reinhard Genzel and Andrea Ghez tracked for years the orbits of stars near the centre of the Milky Way, finding by 2002 that the star S2 circles an invisible object of about four million solar masses in 16 years. Only a supermassive black hole fits, and it is the best evidence that such black holes exist. Genzel and Ghez shared the 2020 Nobel Prize in Physics.
