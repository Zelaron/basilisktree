Urbain Le Verrier found in 1859 that Mercury's orbit turns forward by about 38 arcseconds per century more than Newtonian gravity with the known planets allows (43 in modern values). In November 1915 Einstein showed that general relativity accounts for the difference exactly, his first quantitative test of the theory.
