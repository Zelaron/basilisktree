Does string theory have stable or long-lived vacua with positive vacuum energy, like our accelerating universe? KKLT (2003) argued yes, while swampland conjectures (2018) say perhaps not, which would require evolving dark energy. Explicit candidate de Sitter vacua appeared in 2024, controlled only at leading order; a construction that survives all corrections, or a no-go theorem, would settle it.
