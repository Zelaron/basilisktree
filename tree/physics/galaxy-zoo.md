Galaxy Zoo, launched in July 2007 by Chris Lintott, Kevin Schawinski and colleagues, asked online volunteers to classify the shapes of nearly a million galaxies imaged by the Sloan Digital Sky Survey. Volunteers found new kinds of objects, such as Hanny's Voorwerp, discovered by Hanny van Arkel, and the project became the model for online citizen science.
