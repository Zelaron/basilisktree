After the 1906 San Francisco earthquake, Harry Fielding Reid studied the ground displacements on either side of the San Andreas Fault and proposed in 1910 that faults slowly accumulate elastic strain until the rock slips suddenly and releases it. The theory explains why earthquakes recur and remains the basic model of the earthquake cycle.
