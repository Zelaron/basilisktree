Thomas Young showed in 1801-03 that two beams of light split from one source overlap in bands of bright and dark fringes, as water waves do, and used the fringe spacing to estimate the wavelengths of colours. The two-slit form of the experiment became the standard evidence for the wave nature of light and later for the quantum behaviour of matter.
