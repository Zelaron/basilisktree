Thomas Young showed in 1801-03 that light passing through two close slits makes bands of bright and dark fringes, as overlapping water waves do, and used the fringe spacing to estimate the wavelengths of colours. The double-slit experiment became the standard evidence for the wave nature of light and later for the quantum behaviour of matter.
