In 1925 George Uhlenbeck and Samuel Goudsmit proposed that the electron has an intrinsic angular momentum, spin, with two possible orientations, to explain the doublets seen in atomic spectra. The two beams of silver atoms that Otto Stern and Walther Gerlach saw in 1922 were later understood as its effect. Spin underlies magnetism, the periodic table, MRI and spintronics.
