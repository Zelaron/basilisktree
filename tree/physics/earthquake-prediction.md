Can the time, place and size of a large earthquake be predicted hours to days ahead? Probabilistic forecasts and early warning, which gives seconds of notice after a rupture begins, work; reliable prediction does not, and claimed precursors have not survived testing, as the Parkfield experiment (1985–2004) showed. A method validated prospectively against a public catalogue would settle it.
