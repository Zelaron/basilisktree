In 1980 Klaus von Klitzing found that in a two-dimensional electron gas in a silicon transistor at low temperature and high magnetic field, the Hall resistance sits on plateaus at exactly h/e² divided by an integer, whatever the sample's size or purity. The effect gives a reproducible resistance standard and was later understood as a topological property of electron bands.
