Since 2004, improved spectroscopic analyses have lowered the Sun's measured abundances of carbon, nitrogen and oxygen, and solar models built with them no longer match the sound speed and convection-zone depth measured by helioseismology. Are the abundances or the interior opacities wrong? Abundance determinations still disagree (2021–2022); a model fitting helioseismology and solar neutrinos together would settle it.
