Christiaan Huygens, in a treatise written in 1678 and published in 1690, proposed that light is a pulse travelling through an ether and that every point on a wavefront acts as a source of secondary wavelets. The construction explained reflection, refraction and the double refraction of Iceland spar. Young and Fresnel revived the wave theory in the early 1800s.
