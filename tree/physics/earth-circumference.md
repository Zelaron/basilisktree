Eratosthenes (about 240 BCE) found that the noon Sun was overhead at Syene but cast a shadow at Alexandria, about one fiftieth of a circle to the north. From the distance between the cities he put Earth's circumference at about 250,000 stadia, which, depending on which stadion is meant, is close to the true 40,000 km. It began geodesy.
