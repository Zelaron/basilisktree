Richard Oldham showed in 1906 from earthquake records that Earth has a distinct central core, where compressional waves slow down. Beno Gutenberg fixed its boundary at a depth of about 2,900 km (1913), and Harold Jeffreys concluded in 1926 that the outer core is liquid because shear waves do not pass through it.
