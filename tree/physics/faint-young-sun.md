Early in Earth's history the Sun was 20–30% dimmer, yet geology shows liquid water on the young Earth (Sagan and Mullen, 1972). Which mix of greenhouse gases, clouds and surface albedo kept it warm? Three-dimensional climate models show that more CO2, possibly with methane, can do it, so the open question is what the Archean atmosphere contained; proxies that pin down Archean CO2 would settle it.
