Can the statistics of turbulence, its energy cascade, intermittency and anomalous scaling exponents, be derived from the equations of fluid flow? Kolmogorov's 1941 theory predicts the basic scaling but misses intermittency, and engineering still relies on empirical closure models. A predictive theory of the exponents or a controlled closure would settle it; it is distinct from the Clay regularity problem.
