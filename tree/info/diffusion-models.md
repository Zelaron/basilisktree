Jascha Sohl-Dickstein and colleagues (2015) proposed generating data by learning to reverse a gradual noising process, a method inspired by nonequilibrium thermodynamics. Song and Ermon (2019) and Ho, Jain and Abbeel (2020) made it work at scale, and diffusion models underlie most image, video and audio generators.
