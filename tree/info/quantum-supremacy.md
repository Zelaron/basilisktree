Google's Sycamore chip, with 53 working superconducting qubits, sampled the output of a random quantum circuit in about 200 seconds (Arute et al., October 2019), a task Google estimated would take a supercomputer 10,000 years. IBM disputed the estimate, and later classical simulations reduced it sharply.
