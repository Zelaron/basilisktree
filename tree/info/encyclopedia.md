Diderot and d'Alembert's Encyclopédie (1751 to 1772) filled 28 volumes in its first edition, with named contributors, cross-references and a tree of knowledge, and it was the first general encyclopedia to describe the mechanical arts in detail. It followed Pliny's Natural History (77 CE) and Chambers' Cyclopaedia (1728).
