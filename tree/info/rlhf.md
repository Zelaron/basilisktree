Paul Christiano, Jan Leike and colleagues (2017) trained a reward model from human comparisons of pairs of behaviours and optimized an agent against it, so that people could specify goals they could not write as a reward function. Long Ouyang and colleagues applied it to language models in InstructGPT (2022), a basis of ChatGPT.
