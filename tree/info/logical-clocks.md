Leslie Lamport's 'Time, Clocks, and the Ordering of Events in a Distributed System' (1978) defined a happened-before order for events on separate machines and a counter-based clock that respects it, drawing on the ordering of events in special relativity. It became a foundation of distributed-systems theory, and Lamport received the 2013 Turing Award.
