Can indivisible goods always be divided so that no agent envies another's bundle once any single good is removed from it (EFX, introduced in 2016)? Existence is proved for two agents and for three agents with additive valuations (Chaudhury, Garg and Mehlhorn, 2020); the smallest open case is four agents with additive valuations.
