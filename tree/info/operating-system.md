GM-NAA I/O, written by Robert Patrick at General Motors Research and Owen Mock at North American Aviation for the IBM 704 (1956), ran jobs in a batch without manual setup and supplied common input and output routines. IBM's OS/360 (1966) and the time-sharing systems of the 1960s extended the idea into the operating systems that manage every computer today.
