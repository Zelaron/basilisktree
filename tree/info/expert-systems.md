DENDRAL, begun in 1965 by Edward Feigenbaum, Joshua Lederberg and Bruce Buchanan at Stanford, inferred molecular structures from mass spectra using rules supplied by chemists, and MYCIN (1972) applied the approach to diagnosing blood infections. Expert systems drove the AI boom of the 1980s, and their limits contributed to the second AI winter.
