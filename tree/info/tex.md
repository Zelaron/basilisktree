Donald Knuth began TeX in 1977 and released it in 1978, after he was disappointed by the typesetting of the second edition of volume 2 of The Art of Computer Programming. Its mathematical typesetting and optimal paragraph breaking (Knuth and Plass, 1981) set a new standard, and Leslie Lamport's LaTeX (1984) became the usual format of scientific papers.
