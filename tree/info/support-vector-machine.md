Bernhard Boser, Isabelle Guyon and Vladimir Vapnik (1992) combined maximum-margin classifiers with the kernel trick, and Corinna Cortes and Vapnik added soft margins (1995). Support vector machines were among the leading methods for classification until deep learning overtook them around 2012.
