Baker, Gill and Solovay (1975) built oracles relative to which P = NP and others relative to which P ≠ NP, so no proof technique that relativizes can settle the question. Razborov and Rudich's natural proofs (1994) and Aaronson and Wigderson's algebrization (2008) ruled out further techniques.
