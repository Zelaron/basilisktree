Kaiming He, Xiangyu Zhang, Shaoqing Ren and Jian Sun (2015) added skip connections that let each layer learn a correction to its input, making networks of 152 and more layers trainable. Residual networks won the ImageNet 2015 contest, and the paper became one of the most cited in science; transformers also use residual connections.
