Miklós Ajtai (1996) showed how to build cryptographic functions whose security depends on the worst-case difficulty of lattice problems, and Hoffstein, Pipher and Silverman's NTRU (1996) gave a practical encryption scheme. No efficient quantum attack on lattice problems is known, so lattice schemes became the basis of post-quantum standards.
