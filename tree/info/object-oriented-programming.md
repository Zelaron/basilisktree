Ole-Johan Dahl and Kristen Nygaard's Simula 67 introduced classes, objects, inheritance and virtual procedures, and Alan Kay's Smalltalk (1972) at Xerox PARC developed them and named the style 'object-oriented'. C++ (1985) and Java (1995) carried the model into mainstream software. Dahl and Nygaard received the 2001 Turing Award and Kay the 2003 award.
