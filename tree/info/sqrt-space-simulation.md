Ryan Williams (2025) proved that any computation running in t steps on a multitape Turing machine can be simulated in about the square root of t log t space, improving the t / log t space bound of Hopcroft, Paul and Valiant (1975). The proof builds on the tree evaluation algorithm of Cook and Mertz. The paper appeared at STOC 2025.
