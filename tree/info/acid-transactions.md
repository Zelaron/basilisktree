Jim Gray's 'The Transaction Concept: Virtues and Limitations' (1981) described transactions that either happen completely or not at all, using locking and logging for isolation and recovery. The properties were named atomicity, consistency, isolation and durability (ACID) by Theo Härder and Andreas Reuter in 1983, and Gray received the 1998 Turing Award.
