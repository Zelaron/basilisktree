OpenAI's GPT (Alec Radford and colleagues, June 2018) and Google's BERT (Jacob Devlin and colleagues, October 2018) pretrained transformers on large amounts of unlabelled text and then fine-tuned them for specific tasks, setting records across language benchmarks. GPT-2 (2019) generated fluent paragraphs.
