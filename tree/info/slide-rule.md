Sliding two logarithmic scales against each other multiplies numbers by adding lengths. Edmund Gunter laid out the scale in 1620, and William Oughtred combined two of them around 1622 (published 1632). The slide rule was the engineer's standard calculator until electronic pocket calculators replaced it in the 1970s.
