Compress enwik9, the first gigabyte of an English Wikipedia dump, losslessly within about 50 hours on one CPU core with under 10 GB of RAM; each 1% gain over the record earns 5,000 euros from a 500,000-euro fund. Marcus Hutter launched it in 2006, arguing that compression measures intelligence. The record is 100,424,672 bytes (July 2026), so the next award needs under 99,420,425.
