Zauner conjectured in his 1999 thesis that every dimension d has d² equiangular quantum states forming a SIC-POVM, obtainable as an orbit of the Weyl–Heisenberg group. Exact SICs are known for every d up to 53 and in 115 dimensions in all, numerical ones for every d up to 193; recent work ties the problem to Stark units and Hilbert's twelfth problem.
