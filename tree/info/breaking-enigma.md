Marian Rejewski used the theory of permutations to reconstruct the wiring of the German Enigma rotors in December 1932. The Polish bomba (1938) and, from 1940, Turing and Welchman's Bombe at Bletchley Park automated the search for daily keys. The decrypts, code-named Ultra, informed Allied operations in the Second World War.
