Neal Koblitz and Victor Miller independently proposed in 1985 the use of the group of points on an elliptic curve for discrete-logarithm cryptography. Keys of about 256 bits give security comparable to 3,072-bit RSA, and elliptic curves are used in TLS, Signal, Bitcoin and most mobile devices.
