In August 2025 OpenAI reported that an ensemble of general-purpose reasoning models scored at gold-medal level in the online AI track of the International Olympiad in Informatics, ahead of all but five of 330 human contestants. In September 2025 OpenAI reported 12 of 12 problems at the ICPC World Finals in Baku and Google DeepMind's Gemini 2.5 Deep Think 10 of 12, while the best human team solved 11.
