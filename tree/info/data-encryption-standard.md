IBM's Lucifer cipher, designed by Horst Feistel's group and modified in consultation with the NSA, became the Data Encryption Standard in 1977, the first publicly specified standard cipher. Its 56-bit key was broken by brute force by the EFF's Deep Crack machine in 1998, and AES replaced it in 2001.
