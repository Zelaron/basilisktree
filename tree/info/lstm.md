Sepp Hochreiter and Jürgen Schmidhuber (1997) added gated memory cells that let recurrent networks learn dependencies across hundreds of time steps, overcoming the vanishing gradient problem. LSTMs powered speech recognition and machine translation until transformers replaced them around 2018.
