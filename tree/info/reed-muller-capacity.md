Emmanuel Abbe and Colin Sandon (FOCS 2023) proved that Reed–Muller codes, introduced in 1954, achieve Shannon capacity on symmetric binary channels, and Galen Reeves and Henry Pfister (2024) proved vanishing bit error below capacity. Kudekar and coauthors had shown the result for the erasure channel in 2017.
