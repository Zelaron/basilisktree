Scott Aaronson and Alex Arkhipov (2011) showed that sampling the output of photons passing through a linear optical network would be hard for classical computers unless the polynomial hierarchy collapses, a route to quantum advantage with far simpler hardware than a universal quantum computer. The Jiuzhang photonic experiments (2020) followed.
