The information in a string can be measured by the length of the shortest program that outputs it. Ray Solomonoff (1960 and 1964), Andrey Kolmogorov (1965) and Gregory Chaitin (1966) arrived at the idea independently. The measure is uncomputable, and it gives a precise form of Occam's razor.
