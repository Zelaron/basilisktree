Google Quantum AI's Quantum Echoes experiment (Nature, 22 October 2025) measured an out-of-time-order correlator on the 105-qubit Willow chip, which Google estimates would take the Frontier supercomputer about 13,000 times longer to simulate. The result is a number that other quantum computers can reproduce, and like earlier advantage claims it may be challenged by better classical algorithms.
