Tommy Flowers built Colossus at the Post Office Research Station. The first machine, with about 1,600 valves, worked in December 1943 and at Bletchley Park in early 1944 to break the German Lorenz teleprinter cipher. It is regarded as the first programmable electronic digital computer, and its existence stayed secret until the mid-1970s.
