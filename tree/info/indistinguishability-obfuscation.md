Aayush Jain, Huijia Lin and Amit Sahai (2020) constructed indistinguishability obfuscation, which makes two programs with the same behaviour look identical, from assumptions that cryptographers had studied for other purposes. One of them is not secure against quantum computers. Obfuscation of this kind implies most other cryptographic primitives.
