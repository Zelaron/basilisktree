A signal with no frequencies above B hertz is fixed by samples taken 2B times per second. Nyquist (1928) showed that bandwidth B carries 2B independent pulses per second, Kotelnikov (1933) proved the sampling result and Shannon (1949) gave the form found in textbooks. It underlies digital audio, images and communications.
