John Hopfield (1982) described a network of binary neurons that stores patterns as minima of an energy function and recalls them from partial cues by descending that landscape, borrowing ideas from the physics of spin glasses. Hopfield and Geoffrey Hinton shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics for foundational work on artificial neural networks.
