On 9 December 1968 Douglas Engelbart and his team at SRI showed the oN-Line System to about a thousand computer professionals in San Francisco, with a mouse (built in 1964 with Bill English), windows, hypertext links, shared-screen editing and video conferencing. The event was later called the Mother of All Demos.
