Aram Harrow, Avinatan Hassidim and Seth Lloyd (2009) gave a quantum algorithm that estimates properties of the solution of a linear system in time polylogarithmic in its size, under strong assumptions about how the input is loaded and the output is read. It launched quantum machine learning, and later work by Ewin Tang showed that some related speedups disappear.
