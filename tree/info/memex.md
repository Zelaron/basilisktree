In 'As We May Think' (The Atlantic, July 1945) Vannevar Bush described the memex, a desk-sized device storing a person's books and records on microfilm, where the user could join items by associative trails as the mind moves between ideas. It was never built, but it inspired the pioneers of hypertext and the Web.
