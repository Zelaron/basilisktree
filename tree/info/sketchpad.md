Ivan Sutherland's 1963 MIT thesis program let a person draw on the screen of the TX-2 computer with a light pen, apply geometric constraints and reuse a master drawing as many linked instances. It founded interactive computer graphics and computer-aided design, and won him the 1988 Turing Award.
