Edsger Dijkstra conceived (1956) and published (1959) a method that finds shortest paths from one source in a graph with nonnegative edge weights by repeatedly settling the nearest unvisited node. With Fibonacci heaps (1984) it runs in O(m + n log n) time, and variants route traffic in networks and maps.
