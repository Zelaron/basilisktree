László Babai announced in November 2015 an algorithm that decides graph isomorphism in quasipolynomial time, 2^O((log n)^c). Harald Helfgott found a flaw in January 2017, and Babai restored the bound within days with a correction that Helfgott confirmed. A full journal version had not appeared as of 2025.
