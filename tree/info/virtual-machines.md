IBM's CP-40 and CP-67 (1964 to 1967), developed at the Cambridge Scientific Center, gave each user a complete virtual copy of an IBM 360 computer. Popek and Goldberg formalized the requirements for virtualization in 1974, and VMware (1999) and cloud computing later built on the idea.
