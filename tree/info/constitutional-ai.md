Yuntao Bai and colleagues at Anthropic (December 2022) trained an assistant to critique and revise its own responses against a written list of principles, then trained a preference model from AI-generated comparisons, replacing most human labels of harmfulness. The method is also called reinforcement learning from AI feedback.
