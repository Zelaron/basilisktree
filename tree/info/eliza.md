Joseph Weizenbaum's ELIZA (1966) at MIT matched keywords in typed input and turned them into questions, in its best-known script imitating a Rogerian psychotherapist. Users confided in it although it understood nothing, which troubled Weizenbaum and gave the name 'ELIZA effect' to the habit of crediting programs with understanding.
