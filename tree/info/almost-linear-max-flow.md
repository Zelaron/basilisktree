Li Chen, Rasmus Kyng, Yang P. Liu, Richard Peng, Maximilian Probst Gutenberg and Sushant Sachdeva (2022) gave an algorithm for exact maximum flow and minimum-cost flow in m^(1+o(1)) time on graphs with m edges, using interior-point steps supported by dynamic data structures. It was a best paper at FOCS 2022.
