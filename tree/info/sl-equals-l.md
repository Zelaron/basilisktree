Omer Reingold (2005) gave a deterministic algorithm that decides whether two vertices of an undirected graph are connected using only logarithmic memory, which showed that SL = L. It answered a question of Lewis and Papadimitriou from 1982 and used the zig-zag product of expander graphs.
