Can an AI agent win NetHack, a 1987 roguelike with random dungeons, permadeath and hundreds of interacting items and monsters, by retrieving the Amulet of Yendor and ascending? The NetHack Learning Environment (2020) counts it solved when an agent ascends in ten consecutive games on unseen seeds with random characters; no verified AI ascension in the current game has been reported.
