OpenAI's DALL·E 2 (April 2022), Google's Imagen (May 2022) and the open-weight Stable Diffusion (August 2022, from Stability AI with Robin Rombach and colleagues) generated detailed images from text prompts using diffusion models guided by language encoders. The first DALL·E (Aditya Ramesh and colleagues, 2021) had used a transformer.
