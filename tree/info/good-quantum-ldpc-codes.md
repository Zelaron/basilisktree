Pavel Panteleev and Gleb Kalachev (2021) constructed quantum error-correcting codes with constant rate, linear distance and sparse parity checks, resolving a long-standing open problem. Such codes could sharply cut the number of physical qubits needed per logical qubit, and IBM's fault-tolerance roadmap uses related qLDPC codes.
