Arora, Lund, Motwani, Sudan and Szegedy, and Arora and Safra (1992), proved that every NP proof can be rewritten so that a randomized verifier reads only a constant number of its bits. A consequence is that many optimization problems are NP-hard even to approximate. The result won the 2001 Gödel Prize.
