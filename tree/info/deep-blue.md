IBM's Deep Blue, a chess computer with custom search chips built by Feng-hsiung Hsu, Murray Campbell and A. Joseph Hoane, beat world champion Garry Kasparov 3.5 to 2.5 in a six-game match in May 1997. It searched about 200 million positions per second with a hand-tuned evaluation function.
