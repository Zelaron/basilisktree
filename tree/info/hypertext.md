Ted Nelson coined 'hypertext' in 1965 for non-sequential writing joined by links, following Bush's memex. Early systems included the Hypertext Editing System (1967) at Brown University, NLS (1968) and HyperCard (1987), before Tim Berners-Lee built the Web.
