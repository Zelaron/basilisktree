Kunihiko Fukushima's neocognitron (1980) and Yann LeCun's convolutional networks trained by backpropagation (1989) used shared local filters, an idea taken from studies of the visual cortex. LeCun's LeNet read handwritten ZIP codes and bank cheques in the 1990s, and convolutional networks won the ImageNet contest in 2012.
