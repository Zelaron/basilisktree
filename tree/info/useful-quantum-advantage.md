A quantum computer solving a problem people actually need, such as a chemistry or materials calculation, faster or more accurately than any classical method. Feynman proposed quantum simulation in 1982; beyond-classical demonstrations since 2019 have solved no practical problem, and several were matched by improved classical simulation.
