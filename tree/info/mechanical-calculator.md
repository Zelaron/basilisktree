Wilhelm Schickard's calculating clock (1623) and Blaise Pascal's Pascaline (1642) added numbers with geared wheels and automatic carrying, and Leibniz's stepped drum (1673) extended the idea to multiplication. Mechanical calculators served offices and laboratories until electronic machines replaced them in the 1970s.
