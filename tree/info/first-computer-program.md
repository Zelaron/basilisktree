Ada Lovelace's notes (1843) on Luigi Menabrea's article about the Analytical Engine include, in Note G, a table of operations for computing Bernoulli numbers, often called the first published computer program. She also observed that such an engine could operate on any symbols that follow rules, such as musical notes.
