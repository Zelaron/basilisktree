A hand-cranked bronze gear train built in the 2nd century BCE and recovered from a shipwreck off the Greek island of Antikythera in 1901. Its dials modelled the motions of the Sun and Moon and predicted eclipses, and it is the oldest known analogue computer. Geared mechanisms of similar complexity do not reappear in the record until medieval astronomical clocks.
