Yoshua Bengio, Réjean Ducharme, Pascal Vincent and Christian Jauvin's 'A Neural Probabilistic Language Model' (2003) predicted the next word from learned vector representations of the preceding words, outperforming n-gram models. It is an ancestor of the language models used today.
