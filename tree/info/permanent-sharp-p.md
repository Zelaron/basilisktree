Leslie Valiant (1979) defined the class #P of counting problems and proved that computing the permanent of a 0-1 matrix, which counts perfect matchings, is #P-complete, although finding a single matching is easy. Toda's theorem (1991) showed how hard #P is, and the hardness of the permanent underlies the argument for boson sampling.
