Arjen Lenstra, Hendrik Lenstra and László Lovász (1982) gave a polynomial-time algorithm that finds a reasonably short basis of a lattice. It was first used to factor polynomials with rational coefficients, and it breaks knapsack-based public-key systems and is the standard attack tool against lattice cryptography.
