Meta's LLaMA (February 2023) went to researchers and was soon leaked, and Llama 2 (July 2023), Mistral 7B (September 2023), Alibaba's Qwen and DeepSeek's models were released with downloadable weights. DeepSeek-R1 (January 2025) reported reasoning performance comparable to OpenAI's o1 on several benchmarks.
