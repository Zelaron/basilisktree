Jacquard's loom (patented 1804) used chains of punched cards to select which warp threads to lift, so the woven pattern changed when the cards changed. It built on the work of Basile Bouchon (1725) and Jacques de Vaucanson, and Babbage adopted its punched cards to program his Analytical Engine.
