Semitic speakers in Egypt and Sinai wrote consonants with a few dozen signs adapted from hieroglyphs (the Proto-Sinaitic script, dated between about 1850 and 1550 BCE). The Phoenician alphabet (about 1050 BCE) spread the idea and the Greeks added vowels (about 800 BCE). Latin, Cyrillic, Hebrew and Arabic scripts descend from it.
