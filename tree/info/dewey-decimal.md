Melvil Dewey's classification (1876) gave every subject a decimal number, so a book could be shelved by topic and new books added without rearranging the collection. Callimachus' Pinakes (about 245 BCE), a subject catalogue of the Library of Alexandria, is regarded as the first library catalogue. Dewey's scheme is used in libraries in many countries.
