Egyptians pressed strips of the papyrus plant into sheets that took ink. It was in use by the First Dynasty (about 3000 BCE), and the oldest inscribed papyri found, the Diary of Merer, date from about 2560 BCE. Papyrus was the main writing material of the Mediterranean world for several thousand years, until parchment and paper displaced it.
