Manindra Agrawal, Neeraj Kayal and Nitin Saxena (2002) gave the first deterministic polynomial-time algorithm for testing whether a number is prime, without unproven assumptions. Kayal and Saxena were then students at IIT Kanpur. The paper won the 2006 Gödel Prize.
