Larry Roberts's 1963 MIT thesis recovered the three-dimensional shape of polyhedra from photographs, using edges and perspective geometry. A 1966 MIT summer project meant to build a visual system in a few months showed how hard the task was, and computer vision grew into a research field of its own.
