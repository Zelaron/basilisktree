Libratus (Noam Brown and Tuomas Sandholm, January 2017) beat four top professionals at two-player no-limit Texas hold'em over 120,000 hands, and Pluribus (2019) beat elite professionals at six-player poker. Both rely on counterfactual regret minimization, which copes with hidden information.
