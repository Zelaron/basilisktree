Yasunobu Nakamura, Yuri Pashkin and Jaw-Shen Tsai (1999) made the first superconducting qubit from a Cooper-pair box, showing coherent control of a quantum state in an electrical circuit. The transmon (2007) became the basis of the quantum computers of Google and IBM, and the 2025 Nobel Prize in Physics went to Clarke, Devoret and Martinis for the underlying circuit physics.
