Noam Nisan and Avi Wigderson (1994) showed that functions that are hard for small circuits can be turned into pseudorandom generators that fool efficient algorithms. Impagliazzo and Wigderson (1997) proved that if some problem solvable in exponential time needs exponential-size circuits then P = BPP, and Wigderson received the 2023 Turing Award.
