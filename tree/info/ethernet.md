Robert Metcalfe and David Boggs at Xerox PARC built a shared-cable network (memo of May 1973) in which stations listen before sending, detect collisions and retry after random delays. Ethernet was standardized in 1983 as IEEE 802.3 and is the dominant wired local network. Metcalfe received the 2022 Turing Award.
