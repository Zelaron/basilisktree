Paul Baran at RAND (1964) and Donald Davies at the National Physical Laboratory (1965) independently proposed dividing messages into small blocks routed separately through a network; Davies named them packets. Packet switching shares links efficiently and survives the loss of single nodes, and it became the basis of ARPANET (1969) and the internet.
