NIST published FIPS 203 (ML-KEM, from CRYSTALS-Kyber), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA, from CRYSTALS-Dilithium) and FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA, from SPHINCS+) on 13 August 2024, the first standards designed to resist attack by quantum computers, after an open competition begun in 2016. In March 2025 NIST selected the code-based HQC as a backup key-encapsulation method.
