Robert Gallager's 1960 thesis (published 1962) described codes defined by sparse parity checks and decoded by passing messages iteratively. They were largely forgotten until David MacKay and Radford Neal revived them in 1996, and they now protect Wi-Fi, 5G data channels and digital television.
