Nicos Christofides (1976), and independently Anatoliy Serdyukov, combined a minimum spanning tree with a minimum matching of its odd-degree nodes to give a tour at most 3/2 times the optimum for the metric travelling salesman problem. No better guarantee was known until Karlin, Klein and Oveis Gharan found a slightly better ratio in 2020.
