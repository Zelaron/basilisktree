Oded Regev (2005) showed, with a quantum reduction, that solving noisy linear equations modulo a number is as hard as worst-case lattice problems. Learning with errors is the basis of ML-KEM, the post-quantum key-exchange standard, and of fully homomorphic encryption. Regev received the 2018 Gödel Prize.
