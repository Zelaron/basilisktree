Jonathan Schaeffer's team at the University of Alberta used the Chinook program, which won the world checkers title in 1994, to prove in 2007 that perfect play from the starting position leads to a draw. The computation ran for 18 years and weakly solved a game with about 5 × 10^20 positions.
