Bennett, Brassard, Crépeau, Jozsa, Peres and Wootters (1993) showed that an unknown quantum state can be transferred to a distant qubit using a shared entangled pair and two classical bits. It was demonstrated in 1997, and in 2017 from the ground to the Micius satellite, and it is a basic primitive of quantum networks.
