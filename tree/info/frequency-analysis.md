Al-Kindi's treatise on deciphering cryptographic messages (about 850) showed that a substitution cipher can be broken by counting how often each symbol occurs and matching the counts to the letter frequencies of the language. It is the earliest known work on cryptanalysis and ended the safety of simple substitution.
