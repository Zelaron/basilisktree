The term 'open source' was proposed by Christine Peterson in February 1998, after Netscape released its browser code, and Bruce Perens and Eric Raymond founded the Open Source Initiative to promote it. Raymond's essay 'The Cathedral and the Bazaar' (1997) had argued for open development.
