Irving Reed and Gustave Solomon (1960) built codes from polynomial values over finite fields that correct bursts of errors, and Berlekamp and Massey supplied an efficient decoder in the late 1960s. Reed–Solomon codes protect compact discs, QR codes, RAID storage and deep-space links.
