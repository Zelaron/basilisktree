Kleene (1956) showed that networks of McCulloch–Pitts neurons recognize exactly the regular events, the languages described by regular expressions. Rabin and Scott's 1959 paper on finite automata introduced nondeterminism and won them the 1976 Turing Award. Finite automata and regular expressions are used in compilers, text search and network protocols.
