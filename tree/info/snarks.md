Gennaro, Gentry, Parno and Raykova (2013) and the Pinocchio system of Parno, Howell, Gentry and Raykova (2013) made succinct non-interactive arguments practical: short proofs that a computation was done correctly, checkable in milliseconds. Groth's 2016 scheme is widely used, and SNARKs power private payments in Zcash (2016) and blockchain rollups.
