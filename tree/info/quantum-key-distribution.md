Charles Bennett and Gilles Brassard's BB84 protocol (1984) sends secret keys as single polarized photons, and any eavesdropper disturbs the states and leaves detectable errors. Artur Ekert's entanglement-based protocol followed in 1991. Bennett and Brassard received the 2025 Turing Award.
