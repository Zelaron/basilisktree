Cameron Jones and Benjamin Bergen (2025) ran a pre-registered three-party Turing test in which interrogators chatted for five minutes with a person and a model at once. GPT-4.5, given a humanlike persona, was judged to be the human in 73% of conversations, more often than the real participants, while ELIZA and GPT-4o scored well below chance. Critics say the result does not show general intelligence.
