João Marques-Silva and Karem Sakallah's GRASP (1996) added conflict-driven clause learning to the DPLL procedure, and Chaff (Moskewicz, Madigan, Zhao, Zhang and Malik, 2001) made it fast with watched literals. Modern SAT solvers handle formulas with millions of variables, verify chips and have settled problems in combinatorics.
