Georgy Adelson-Velsky and Evgenii Landis (1962) described AVL trees, binary search trees that rotate after each update to keep their height proportional to log n, so search, insertion and deletion take logarithmic time. B-trees (Bayer and McCreight, 1970) and red-black trees (1970s) followed and underlie ordered maps and database indexes.
