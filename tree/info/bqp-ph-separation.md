Ran Raz and Avishay Tal (2018) constructed an oracle relative to which BQP is not contained in the polynomial hierarchy, using the Forrelation problem of Aaronson and a new analysis of constant-depth circuits. This answered a question that Bernstein and Vazirani raised in 1993, and it was published at STOC 2019.
