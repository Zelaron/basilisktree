Furst, Saxe and Sipser (1981) and Ajtai (1983) proved that circuits of constant depth and polynomial size, built from unbounded fan-in AND, OR and NOT gates, cannot compute the parity of n bits. Håstad's switching lemma (1986) made the bound exponential, an early unconditional lower bound for circuits.
