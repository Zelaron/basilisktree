Ewin Tang, then an 18-year-old undergraduate advised by Scott Aaronson, gave a classical algorithm (2018) for the recommendation-system problem that matches the exponential speedup of the quantum algorithm of Kerenidis and Prakash up to polynomial factors. Her dequantization technique was extended to other proposals in quantum machine learning.
