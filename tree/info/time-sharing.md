MIT's Compatible Time-Sharing System, demonstrated in November 1961 under Fernando Corbató, let many users work interactively on one computer at the same time, following John McCarthy's proposal of 1959. Time-sharing led to Multics and Unix, to electronic mail, and to the idea of computing as a shared utility.
