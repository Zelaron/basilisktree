Anatoly Karatsuba, then a student, found in 1960 that two n-digit numbers can be multiplied with three half-size products instead of four, using about n^1.585 steps. This refuted Kolmogorov's conjecture that about n² operations are needed and began the search for faster multiplication that reached n log n in 2019.
