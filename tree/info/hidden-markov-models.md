Leonard Baum and Ted Petrie (1966) developed statistics for Markov chains whose states are seen only through noisy outputs, and Baum and Lloyd Welch gave an expectation-maximization algorithm to learn them. Hidden Markov models drove speech recognition at IBM and Dragon from the 1970s and were later used to find genes in DNA.
