Ethan Bernstein and Umesh Vazirani's 'Quantum complexity theory' (1993) defined BQP, the problems a quantum computer solves efficiently with bounded error, constructed a universal quantum Turing machine, and gave an oracle problem separating BQP from classical randomized polynomial time. It founded quantum complexity theory.
