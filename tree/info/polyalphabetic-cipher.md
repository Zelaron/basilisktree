Alberti's cipher disk (De Cifris, 1467) changed the substitution alphabet during a message so that letter frequencies no longer showed through. Bellaso (1553) gave the keyword form later credited to Blaise de Vigenère, and it resisted attack until Friedrich Kasiski published a general method in 1863.
