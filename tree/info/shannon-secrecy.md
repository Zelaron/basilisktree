Claude Shannon's 'Communication Theory of Secrecy Systems' (1949) applied information theory to ciphers. It proved that the one-time pad is perfectly secret, that any perfectly secret cipher needs a key at least as long as the message, and introduced confusion and diffusion as design principles for block ciphers.
