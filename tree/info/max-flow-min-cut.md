The largest flow that can be sent through a network equals the capacity of its smallest cut (1956, shown independently by Ford and Fulkerson, and by Elias, Feinstein and Shannon). The Ford–Fulkerson augmenting-path method began the study of network-flow algorithms, used in matching, scheduling and image segmentation.
