Daniel Sleator and Robert Tarjan (1985) analysed list update and paging by comparing an online algorithm, which sees requests one at a time, with the best offline algorithm that knows them all in advance. Competitive analysis became the standard way to study caching, scheduling and other decisions made under uncertainty.
