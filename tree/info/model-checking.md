Edmund Clarke and E. Allen Emerson (1981) and, independently, Jean-Pierre Queille and Joseph Sifakis (1982) gave algorithms that check automatically whether a finite-state system satisfies a temporal-logic specification. Model checking is used to verify chips and protocols, and Clarke, Emerson and Sifakis received the 2007 Turing Award.
