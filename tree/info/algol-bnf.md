The ALGOL 60 report (1960), edited by Peter Naur for an international committee, introduced block structure, recursion and lexical scope and defined the language's syntax in Backus–Naur form, a notation John Backus had proposed for ALGOL 58. It started precise language definition, and C, Pascal and most later languages descend from ALGOL.
