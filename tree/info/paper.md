Cai Lun presented an improved method of making paper from bark, rags and hemp to the Han court in 105 CE, though paper fragments from the 2nd century BCE are known. Papermaking reached Baghdad in the 8th century and Spain and Italy in the 12th and 13th, supplying a cheap writing surface for books, records and printing.
