Juris Hartmanis and Richard Stearns's 'On the Computational Complexity of Algorithms' (1965) defined time-bounded complexity classes and proved that, given more time, Turing machines can solve strictly more problems. It founded computational complexity theory and won them the 1993 Turing Award.
