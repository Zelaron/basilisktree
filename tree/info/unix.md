Ken Thompson and Dennis Ritchie at Bell Labs began Unix in 1969 on a PDP-7, with a hierarchical file system, small tools combined by pipes and, from 1973, a kernel written in C. Its descendants and imitators include BSD, Linux, macOS, Android and iOS, which run most servers and phones.
