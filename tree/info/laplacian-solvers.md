Daniel Spielman and Shang-Hua Teng (2004) solved linear systems built from graph Laplacians in nearly linear time, using spectral sparsification. Electrical-flow methods based on such solvers led to faster algorithms for maximum flow and other graph problems, and the work won the 2015 Gödel Prize.
