Graydon Hoare began Rust as a personal project in 2006, Mozilla sponsored it from 2009, and version 1.0 appeared in May 2015. Its ownership and borrowing rules, checked at compile time, give memory safety without garbage collection, and Rust was accepted into the Linux kernel in 2022.
