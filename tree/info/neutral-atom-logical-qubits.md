Dolev Bluvstein, Mikhail Lukin and colleagues at Harvard, MIT and QuEra (Nature, December 2023) ran error-detecting and error-correcting circuits on up to 48 logical qubits encoded in reconfigurable arrays of neutral atoms moved by optical tweezers. Arrays with thousands of atoms followed in 2025.
