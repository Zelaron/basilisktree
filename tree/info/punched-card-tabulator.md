Herman Hollerith's tabulating machine (1890) read holes in cards with electrical contacts and counted them on dial counters. It tabulated the 1890 US census in a fraction of the eight years the hand-tallied 1880 census had taken. His company was a forerunner of IBM, so named in 1924.
