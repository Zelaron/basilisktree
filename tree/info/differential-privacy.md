Cynthia Dwork, Frank McSherry, Kobbi Nissim and Adam Smith (2006) defined differential privacy: adding calibrated random noise so that the output of an analysis barely depends on any one person's data. It was used for the 2020 US Census and in products by Apple and Google, and the paper won the 2017 Gödel Prize.
