Claude Shannon's 'Programming a Computer for Playing Chess' (1950) set out minimax search with an evaluation function. Alan Turing and David Champernowne's paper program Turochamp, begun in 1948, was executed by hand in 1952. Chess was the main testbed of game-playing AI until Deep Blue beat Kasparov in 1997.
