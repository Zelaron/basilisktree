Anurag Anshu, Nikolas Breuckmann and Chinmay Nirkhe (2022) proved the NLTS conjecture of Freedman and Hastings: there are local Hamiltonians all of whose low-energy states need circuits of more than constant depth to prepare. The construction uses good quantum LDPC codes, and NLTS is a weaker consequence of the quantum PCP conjecture, which remains open.
