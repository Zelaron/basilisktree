Chris Olah and colleagues began reverse-engineering the circuits inside vision networks in 2020. In 2023 and 2024 Anthropic and other groups used sparse autoencoders to extract interpretable features from language models, and in 2025 Anthropic published attribution graphs that trace how a model reaches an answer.
