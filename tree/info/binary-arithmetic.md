Leibniz's 'Explication de l'arithmétique binaire' (1703) showed how to write every number with only 0 and 1 and how to add, subtract, multiply and divide in that notation. Thomas Harriot had tried base two privately a century earlier. Binary notation became the basis of digital computers from the 1930s.
