Clay-tablet archives at Ebla (about 2500 BCE) and Ashurbanipal's library at Nineveh (7th century BCE) gathered texts in one place, and the Library of Alexandria (3rd century BCE) tried to collect all known writing. Libraries let written knowledge be stored, catalogued and copied beyond a single generation.
