Counting boards and bead frames do positional arithmetic by moving tokens. Greek sources show counting boards by the 5th century BCE, the Salamis tablet dates from about 300 BCE, and the Chinese suanpan is documented by the 2nd century BCE. Abaci stayed in everyday use across Asia into the 20th century.
