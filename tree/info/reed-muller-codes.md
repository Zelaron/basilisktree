David Muller (1954) defined binary codes from evaluations of low-degree polynomials in several variables, and Irving Reed gave a majority-logic decoder the same year. A first-order Reed–Muller code carried Mariner 9's pictures of Mars (1971), and the family later underpinned work on locally testable codes and polar codes.
