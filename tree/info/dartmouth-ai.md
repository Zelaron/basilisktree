John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, Nathaniel Rochester and Claude Shannon proposed in 1955 a two-month study of machine intelligence at Dartmouth College in the summer of 1956, and the proposal introduced the term 'artificial intelligence'. At the workshop Allen Newell, Cliff Shaw and Herbert Simon showed the Logic Theorist, a program that proved theorems of Principia Mathematica.
