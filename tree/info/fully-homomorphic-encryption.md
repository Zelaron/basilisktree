Craig Gentry (2009) showed how to compute any function on encrypted data without decrypting it, constructing the first fully homomorphic encryption scheme from ideal lattices, with a bootstrapping step that refreshes noisy ciphertexts. Rivest, Adleman and Dertouzos had posed the question in 1978.
