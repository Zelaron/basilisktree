Ian Goodfellow and colleagues (2014) trained a generator network to fool a discriminator network in a two-player game, each improving against the other. Generative adversarial networks produced the first photorealistic synthetic faces (StyleGAN, 2018) before diffusion models largely replaced them for image generation.
