Alec Reeves, working for ITT in Paris, conceived in 1937 the sending of speech as a stream of numbers, each the quantized level of a sampled waveform, which can be regenerated without accumulating noise. The SIGSALY secure telephone used PCM in 1943, and digital telephony, compact discs and audio files rely on it.
