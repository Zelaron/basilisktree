At Göbekli Tepe in southeastern Turkey, people raised carved T-shaped limestone pillars up to about 5.5 metres tall around 9500 BCE, with no clear evidence of farming at the site. It shows that monumental building could come before agriculture, and megalithic building continued for thousands of years, from Malta to Stonehenge.
