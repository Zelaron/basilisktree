Rudolf Kálmán published a recursive method in 1960 for estimating the state of a noisy dynamic system from a stream of measurements, updating the estimate and its uncertainty at each step. Stanley Schmidt adapted it for Apollo navigation, and Kalman filters now run in GPS receivers, aircraft, robots, phones and weather forecasting.
