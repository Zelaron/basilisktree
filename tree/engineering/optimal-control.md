Lev Pontryagin and colleagues stated the maximum principle in 1956, giving conditions that the best control of a dynamical system must satisfy, and Richard Bellman's dynamic programming (1953 to 1957) solved the same problems by working backward through time. Optimal control theory plans rocket trajectories, robot motions and industrial processes.
