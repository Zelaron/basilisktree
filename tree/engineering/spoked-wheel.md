The earliest known spoked wheels are those of the Sintashta culture in the southern Urals, dated to about 2000 BCE. Replacing the solid disc with spokes cut weight sharply and made fast wheeled vehicles possible, and chariots built on them soon spread to the Near East, Egypt, Greece and China.
