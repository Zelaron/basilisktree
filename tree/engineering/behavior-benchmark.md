Make a robot policy complete long everyday household activities, from cooking to cleaning, in the BEHAVIOR-1K simulator (Stanford, 2022). The 2026 BEHAVIOR Challenge at CoRL scores 100 full-length tasks in 7 scenes by average success with partial credit; in the first round (NeurIPS 2025, 50 tasks) the winning team fully completed 12.4% of held-out test episodes. Solving it means near-complete success on the full benchmark.
