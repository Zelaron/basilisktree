John Parsons began applying punched-card coordinate control to the cutting of helicopter rotor blades in the late 1940s, and in 1952 the MIT Servomechanisms Laboratory demonstrated a three-axis milling machine run by punched tape. Numerical control, later driven by computers as CNC, cuts complex shapes from digital instructions and shapes nearly every machined part today.
