Karl Drais showed his pedal-less running machine in 1817, and in 1885 John Kemp Starley's Rover, with two wheels of equal size and a chain drive, set the form of the modern bicycle. Bicycles gave ordinary people cheap, fast personal transport, and bicycle makers supplied skills and parts for early cars and the Wright brothers' aeroplane.
