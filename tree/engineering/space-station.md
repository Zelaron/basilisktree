The Soviet Union launched Salyut 1, the first space station, on 19 April 1971, and later stations such as Skylab, Mir and Tiangong followed. The International Space Station has been continuously crewed since November 2000, and its laboratories have given long-term data on human health in weightlessness, on materials and on the Earth.
