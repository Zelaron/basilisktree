The IEEE approved the 802.11 wireless local network standard in 1997 under the chairmanship of Vic Hayes. The faster 802.11a and 802.11b versions followed in 1999, and OFDM radio, covered by patents from CSIRO's John O'Sullivan and colleagues, later made Wi-Fi fast. It is now the usual way to connect devices to the Internet indoors.
