The V-2 first flew successfully on 3 October 1942 at Peenemünde under Wernher von Braun and Walter Dornberger, and a test launch in June 1944 was the first human-made object to reach space. This liquid-fuel ballistic missile with gyroscopic guidance was the ancestor of postwar rockets and missiles, and its production killed thousands of forced labourers.
