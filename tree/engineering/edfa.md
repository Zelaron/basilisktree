David Payne's group at the University of Southampton and Emmanuel Desurvire at Bell Labs showed in 1987 that fibre doped with erbium ions can amplify light directly when pumped by a laser, with no conversion to electricity. Erbium amplifiers replaced electronic repeaters on long fibre links, made wavelength-division multiplexing practical and let undersea cables carry far more data.
