Convex reading lenses mounted in frames appeared in Italy around 1286, and a sermon by the friar Giordano da Pisa in 1306 says they had been invented within the previous twenty years. Spectacles extended the working lives of scribes and craftsmen, and the lens makers who spread them later built the first telescopes and microscopes.
