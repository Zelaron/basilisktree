Godfrey Hounsfield built the first computed tomography scanner at EMI, and on 1 October 1971 it imaged a patient's brain at Atkinson Morley's Hospital in London, with a computer combining X-ray measurements taken from many angles. Allan Cormack had worked out the mathematics, and the two shared the 1979 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine. CT is now routine in diagnosis.
