Michael Faraday showed continuous electromagnetic rotation in 1821, and in 1834 Moritz von Jacobi built a motor that did useful work. Electric motors turn electrical energy into motion with little loss, and they now use close to half of the world's electricity, driving pumps, fans, tools, trains and vehicles.
