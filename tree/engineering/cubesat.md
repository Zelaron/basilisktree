Jordi Puig-Suari of Cal Poly and Bob Twiggs of Stanford proposed the CubeSat standard in 1999, a 10 centimetre cube that fits a standard launch dispenser, and the first were launched in 2003. Standard parts and shared rides made satellites cheap enough for universities, companies and developing countries, and well over a thousand CubeSats have flown.
