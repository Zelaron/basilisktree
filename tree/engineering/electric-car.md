The Tesla Roadster, developed by a team that included Martin Eberhard, Marc Tarpenning and JB Straubel, began deliveries in 2008 as the first production car to use lithium-ion cells and to travel more than 300 kilometres on a charge, and the mass-market Nissan Leaf followed in 2010. Electric cars passed a fifth of new car sales worldwide in 2024.
