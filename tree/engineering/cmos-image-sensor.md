Eric Fossum and his team at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory developed the CMOS active-pixel sensor in 1993, a camera on a chip in which each pixel has its own amplifier and the circuits are made with standard CMOS processes. It cost less and used less power than CCDs, put a camera in every phone, and now dominates digital imaging.
