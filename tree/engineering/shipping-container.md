Malcom McLean's converted tanker Ideal X carried 58 steel containers from Newark to Houston on 26 April 1956, and engineer Keith Tantlinger's corner fittings and twist-locks made the boxes stackable and standard. Containers cut the cost and time of loading cargo and made global supply chains practical, and nearly all non-bulk cargo now travels in them.
