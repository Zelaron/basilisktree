Benoît Fourneyron built a water turbine in 1827 that was more efficient and more compact than any water wheel, and developed it over the following years. James B. Francis's inward-flow turbine of 1849 and Lester Pelton's impulse wheel of 1879 are still the main designs in hydroelectric plants.
