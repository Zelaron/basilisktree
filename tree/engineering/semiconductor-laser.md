Robert Hall at General Electric and three other American groups made the first semiconductor lasers in 1962, gallium arsenide diodes that worked only at very low temperatures. The heterostructures of Zhores Alferov and Herbert Kroemer led to continuous operation at room temperature in 1970. Diode lasers drive fibre-optic links, optical discs, barcode scanners, laser printers and lidar.
