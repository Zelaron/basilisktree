The Groupe Spécial Mobile, formed by European telecom administrations in 1982 and later part of ETSI, specified a digital mobile standard, and the first GSM call was made in Helsinki in July 1991. SIM cards, text messages, encryption and roaming between countries made it the world's dominant mobile system, used by billions.
