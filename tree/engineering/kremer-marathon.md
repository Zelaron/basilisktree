Fly a human-powered aircraft around a 26-mile (42 km) marathon course in under an hour, for the Royal Aeronautical Society's £50,000 Kremer Marathon Prize, still unclaimed alongside a £100,000 prize for a manoeuvrable sporting aircraft. The first Kremer prizes, offered from 1959, went to the Gossamer Condor (1977) and Gossamer Albatross (1979). Solving it means a winning flight ratified by the Society.
