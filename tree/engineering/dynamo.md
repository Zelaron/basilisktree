In 1866 and 1867 Werner von Siemens, Charles Wheatstone and others built self-excited dynamos, in which the machine's own current feeds its magnet, and Zénobe Gramme's ring dynamo of 1871 gave smooth direct current. Generators turn mechanical power from steam or water into electricity, which became cheap enough for industry, lighting and tramways.
