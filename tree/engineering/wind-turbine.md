James Blyth in Scotland in 1887 and Charles Brush in Ohio in 1888 each built a windmill that generated electricity, and Poul la Cour in Denmark developed generating turbines in the 1890s. Johannes Juul's Gedser turbine of 1957 set the three-blade, upwind layout still used today, and wind is now among the cheapest sources of new electricity.
