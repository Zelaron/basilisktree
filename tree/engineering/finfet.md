Chenming Hu's group at Berkeley, including Tsu-Jae King Liu and Jeffrey Bokor, and Hitachi's Digh Hisamoto developed the FinFET by 1999, a transistor whose channel is a thin vertical fin wrapped by the gate on several sides. The gate controls leakage better as transistors shrink; Intel began production in 2011, and gate-all-around transistors, which succeed FinFETs, entered production in 2022.
