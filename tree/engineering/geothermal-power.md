On 4 July 1904 Prince Piero Ginori Conti lit five light bulbs with a dynamo driven by natural steam at Larderello in Italy, and a commercial geothermal power station began there in 1911. Geothermal plants use the Earth's heat for steady power in volcanic regions, and the Larderello field still produces electricity.
