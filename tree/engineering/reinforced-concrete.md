Joseph Monier patented iron-reinforced concrete garden tubs in 1867, and François Hennebique's patented system of 1892 used reinforced beams, columns and slabs to frame whole buildings. Concrete resists compression and steel resists tension, so together they span floors and bridges cheaply, and reinforced concrete is now the most widely used structural material in the world.
