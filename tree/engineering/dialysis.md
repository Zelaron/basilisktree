Willem Kolff built a rotating-drum artificial kidney from cellophane tubing, a wooden drum and a tank of fluid in occupied Netherlands in 1943, and in 1945 it saved a patient in acute kidney failure. Dialysis replaces kidney function by filtering the blood outside the body, and it now keeps millions of people with kidney failure alive.
