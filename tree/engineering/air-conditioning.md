Willis Carrier designed a system for a Brooklyn printing plant in 1902 that passed air over cold coils to control both temperature and humidity, and patented the apparatus in 1906. Air conditioning made factories, hospitals and data centres workable in heat, and it let cities and industry grow in hot regions.
