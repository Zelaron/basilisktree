Send a working probe to another star and receive its data within a human lifetime; at today's probe speeds, reaching Alpha Centauri (4.4 light years) would take tens of thousands of years. Voyager 1 (launched 1977) reached interstellar space in 2012, and Breakthrough Starshot (2016) proposed laser-driven gram-scale sails at 15–20% of light speed before pausing in 2025. Solving it means a probe that reaches another star system and reports back.
