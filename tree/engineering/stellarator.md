Lyman Spitzer proposed the stellarator in 1951, a fusion device that confines plasma in a twisted magnetic field produced by external coils alone, with no current driven through the plasma. Early machines leaked, but the computer-optimized coils of Germany's Wendelstein 7-X, first operated in 2015, showed that the design can confine plasma well.
