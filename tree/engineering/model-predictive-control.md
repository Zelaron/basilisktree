Jacques Richalet's group in France in 1978 and Charles Cutler at Shell in 1979 developed controllers that predict a process's future behaviour with a model and repeatedly solve an optimization over a moving time horizon, applying only the first step. Model predictive control became standard in refineries and chemical plants and is now used in rockets, cars and power systems.
