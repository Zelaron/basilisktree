Harvey Nathanson and Robert Wickstrom at Westinghouse built the resonant-gate transistor in 1965, a tiny vibrating metal beam on a silicon chip, one of the first MEMS devices made by semiconductor methods. Micromachining later produced the accelerometers that trigger airbags and orient phones, as well as gyroscopes, pressure sensors and microphones.
