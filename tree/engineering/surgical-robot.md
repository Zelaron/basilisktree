Intuitive Surgical's da Vinci system, which lets a surgeon operate small instruments through tiny cuts while seated at a console, received US FDA clearance for general laparoscopic surgery in 2000. Robot-assisted operations in urology, gynaecology and general surgery now number millions a year.
