SpaceX launched the first 60 Starlink satellites in May 2019, the start of a broadband constellation that now has thousands of low-orbit satellites, many linked by lasers. Starlink serves millions of customers, including places with no cable or fibre, and it has prompted rival constellations and concern among astronomers about satellite trails.
