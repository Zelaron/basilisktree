When is a generic framework of bars and joints in space rigid? In the plane the answer depends only on counting edges in subgraphs (Pollaczek-Geiringer 1927, Laman 1970), but in 3D Maxwell's 1864 count is not enough and no combinatorial characterization is known; rigidity can only be tested by a matrix rank computation at random coordinates. Solving it means a combinatorial characterization of generic 3D rigidity.
