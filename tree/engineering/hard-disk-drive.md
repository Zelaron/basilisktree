IBM's 350 disk storage unit, part of the RAMAC computer announced in 1956 and built by a team led by Reynold Johnson, stored five million characters on fifty 24-inch platters and gave random access to the data. Hard disks grew in capacity by many orders of magnitude and became the main bulk storage of computers and data centres.
