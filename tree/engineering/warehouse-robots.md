Kiva Systems, founded in 2003 by Mick Mountz with Raffaello D'Andrea and Peter Wurman, built mobile robots that slide under shelves and carry them to human pickers, so that pickers stay at a station. Amazon bought Kiva in 2012 and had more than 200,000 such robots by 2020, and warehouse automation is now standard in large e-commerce.
