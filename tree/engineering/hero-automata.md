Hero of Alexandria, active around 60 CE, described a cart driven by a falling weight and cords that could be set to follow a programmed route, a coin-operated dispenser of holy water, and the aeolipile, a sphere spun by steam jets. They are among the earliest automatic machines on record, though none led to practical engines in his time.
