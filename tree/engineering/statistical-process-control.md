Walter Shewhart at Bell Telephone Laboratories drew the first control chart in 1924, separating the random variation inherent in a process from variation with an assignable cause. His method tells a manufacturer when to adjust a process and when to leave it alone, and W. Edwards Deming took the ideas to Japan after 1950, where they shaped modern quality management.
