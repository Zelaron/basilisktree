Three-masted carracks with sternpost rudders and mixed square and lateen sails were big enough to carry cargo and crew across oceans. Carracks and their smaller cousins the caravels took Columbus across the Atlantic in 1492 and Vasco da Gama to India in 1498, and Magellan's expedition sailed around the world from 1519.
