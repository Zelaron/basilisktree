MiniMed's Continuous Glucose Monitoring System gained US FDA approval in 1999, using a small sensor under the skin to record glucose every few minutes for later review. Real-time monitors followed in the 2000s, replacing finger-prick tests for many people with diabetes and supplying the readings that automated insulin delivery systems use.
