Build a machine that makes a complete working copy of itself from simple raw stock, motors and electronics included: the kinematic replicator von Neumann described in lectures in 1948 and 1949. Self-copying machines so far rely on bought parts; RepRap's Darwin printed its own plastic parts in 2008, about half the total, but not its motors, chips or rods. Solving it means a replicator whose copies, built only from stock, replicate in turn.
