Frank Whittle's turbojet first ran on a test bench in Rugby, England, in April 1937, and Hans von Ohain's engine powered the Heinkel He 178, which flew on 27 August 1939 as the first jet aircraft. Jet engines allow high speeds and altitudes with far less vibration than propellers, and they made long-haul air travel and supersonic flight possible.
