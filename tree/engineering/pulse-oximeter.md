Takuo Aoyagi and Michio Kishi at Nihon Kohden developed pulse oximetry in 1972, finding that the pulsing of arterial blood changes how red and infrared light pass through the skin, and the company filed a patent in 1974. Pulse oximeters read blood oxygen without drawing blood and became standard in anaesthesia, intensive care and home monitoring.
