Design a sail of about a gram and a few metres across that a ground laser array of up to 100 GW can push to 15–20% of light speed without melting, tearing or sliding off the beam, as Breakthrough Starshot proposed in 2016. It is a materials, photonics and stability problem that can be posed and checked in simulation; solving it means a buildable design that meets the mass, reflectivity, cooling and beam-riding targets together.
