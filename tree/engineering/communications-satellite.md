Telstar 1, built by Bell Labs under John Pierce's direction, relayed live television across the Atlantic in July 1962, and Harold Rosen's Syncom 3 of 1964 reached geostationary orbit, as Arthur C. Clarke had proposed in 1945. Satellites carried broadcasts, telephone calls and later data between continents, and they still serve ships, aircraft and remote regions.
