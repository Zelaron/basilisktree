Axel Krieger's group at Johns Hopkins reported in 2022 that its Smart Tissue Autonomous Robot sewed together two ends of pig intestine inside live animals with no human guiding the needle, using 3D imaging and planning software. In 2025 its SRT-H system, trained on surgeons' demonstrations, autonomously performed key steps of gallbladder removal on pig tissue outside the body.
