General Motors asked in 1968 for a rugged, reprogrammable replacement for relay panels, and Dick Morley's Bedford Associates answered with the Modicon 084 in 1969. A PLC runs control logic in software that technicians can change without rewiring, and PLCs still operate factory machinery, utilities and other automated systems.
