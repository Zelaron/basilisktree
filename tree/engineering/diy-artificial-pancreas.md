Dana Lewis and Scott Leibrand built a closed-loop insulin delivery system from an insulin pump, a glucose monitor and open software in 2014 and 2015, and shared it online as OpenAPS under the slogan 'we are not waiting'. The first commercial hybrid closed-loop system, Medtronic's MiniMed 670G, was approved in 2016, and many people with diabetes still build their own.
