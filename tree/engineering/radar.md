Robert Watson-Watt and Arnold Wilkins showed in the Daventry experiment of February 1935 that a radio beam reflected from an aircraft could be detected, and Britain built the Chain Home warning network. John Randall and Harry Boot's cavity magnetron of 1940 made compact microwave radar possible. Radar now guides air traffic, ships and weather forecasting.
