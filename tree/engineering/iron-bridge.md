The Iron Bridge over the River Severn at Coalbrookdale, designed by Thomas Farnolls Pritchard and cast by Abraham Darby III, was completed in 1779. Its cast-iron arch spans about 30 metres, and it was the first major bridge built of iron, showing that iron could replace stone and timber in large structures.
