Arogyaswami Paulraj and Thomas Kailath at Stanford filed the foundational patent on spatial multiplexing with multiple antennas in 1992, and Gerard Foschini at Bell Labs proposed the layered space-time architecture BLAST in 1996. Using several antennas at both ends lets a radio link carry several data streams on one frequency, and MIMO is now at the core of Wi-Fi, 4G and 5G.
