Columbia made the first flight of NASA's Space Shuttle, the first reusable crewed orbiter, on 12 April 1981. The shuttles flew 135 missions until 2011, launched the Hubble Space Telescope and helped build the International Space Station, but the Challenger accident of 1986 and the Columbia accident of 2003 killed fourteen astronauts.
