Tesla and Neoen's Hornsdale Power Reserve in South Australia, a 100 megawatt lithium-ion battery completed in late 2017, showed that batteries can steady a grid by responding to faults in fractions of a second. Grid-scale batteries have since multiplied around the world and help solar and wind supply evening demand.
