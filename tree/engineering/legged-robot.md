Marc Raibert's Leg Laboratory at Carnegie Mellon University built one-legged hopping machines in the early 1980s that balanced by bouncing, showing that dynamic balance can come from simple control rules. His company Boston Dynamics later built the quadruped BigDog (2005) and the humanoid Atlas, and legged robots now walk, run and flip outside the laboratory.
