Keep glucose in range for people with type 1 diabetes with no meal announcements or carbohydrate counting, meeting the 2019 consensus targets of more than 70% of time in range (70–180 mg/dL) and under 4% below it. Today's systems, starting with the hybrid MiniMed 670G (2016), still need users to bolus for or announce meals. Algorithms can be tested first in the UVA/Padova simulator; solving it means meeting the targets in clinical use.
