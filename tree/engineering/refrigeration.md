Jacob Perkins patented a closed-cycle vapour-compression refrigerating machine in 1834, in which a compressor, condenser and evaporator move heat from a cold space to a warm one. Carl von Linde's ammonia machines of the 1870s industrialized cold storage and brewing, and refrigerators, air conditioners and heat pumps still run the same cycle.
