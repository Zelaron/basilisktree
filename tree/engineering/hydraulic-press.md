Joseph Bramah patented a hydraulic press in 1795 that used Pascal's principle, a small pump pushing water against a large piston, to multiply force. Henry Maudslay, who worked for Bramah, solved the sealing problem with a leather cup washer. Hydraulic presses later forged and pressed metal, and hydraulic power still moves cranes and excavators.
