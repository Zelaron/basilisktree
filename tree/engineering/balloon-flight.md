On 21 November 1783 Pilâtre de Rozier and the Marquis d'Arlandes made the first free flight by people, over Paris in a hot-air balloon built by the Montgolfier brothers, and on 1 December Jacques Charles and Nicolas-Louis Robert flew a hydrogen balloon. Balloons gave the first views of the land from the air and led to airships.
