Google deployed its first Tensor Processing Unit in 2015 and described it publicly in 2016, an application-specific chip built around a large matrix-multiply unit for neural network inference, designed by a team led by Norman Jouppi. It ran those workloads many times more efficiently than contemporary CPUs and GPUs, and it started a wave of custom AI accelerator chips.
