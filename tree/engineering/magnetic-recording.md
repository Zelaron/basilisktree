Valdemar Poulsen recorded speech on steel wire with his Telegraphone in 1898, and in 1928 Fritz Pfleumer patented magnetic tape coated with iron oxide. Magnetic recording led to audio and video tape, computer tape and the hard disk, and magnetic media still hold much of the world's stored data.
