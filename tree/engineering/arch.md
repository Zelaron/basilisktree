Builders in Mesopotamia roofed brick drains with arches, as at Nippur before 3800 BCE, and the Romans later made arches and vaults the basis of their bridges, aqueducts and domes. An arch turns the weight above it into compression along its curve, so stone and brick can span openings without beams.
