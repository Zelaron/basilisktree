James Clerk Maxwell's paper On Governors (1868) analysed a steam engine's speed governor with differential equations and showed that stability depends on the roots of its characteristic equation. Edward Routh (1877) and Adolf Hurwitz (1895) found tests that decide stability from the equation's coefficients, which began the mathematical theory of feedback control.
