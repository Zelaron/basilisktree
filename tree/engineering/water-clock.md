An Egyptian official named Amenemhet is recorded as the inventor of a water clock in the 16th century BCE, and a surviving outflow clock from Karnak dates to about 1400 BCE. Greek engineers such as Ctesibius later added float regulators to keep the flow steady, an early use of feedback. Water clocks told the hours at night and indoors for courts, armies and astronomers.
