John Harington, a courtier of Elizabeth I, described a valve-operated flushing water closet in 1596, and Alexander Cumming patented the S-shaped trap in 1775, which holds water in its bend to block sewer gas. Connected to sewers, flush toilets carried waste out of houses and became standard in cities during the nineteenth century.
