Philo of Byzantium described a water-driven compartmented wheel for irrigation around 250 BCE, and by then wheels were also turning millstones in the Near East. The water wheel gave mills a source of power other than muscle, and for about two thousand years it ground grain and later sawed timber, forged iron and drove textile mills.
