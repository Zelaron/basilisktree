Edward Ardern and William Lockett at the Davyhulme works in Manchester found in 1914 that bubbling air through sewage containing a suspended mass of microbes cleared it of organic matter in hours. Activated sludge is still the main biological process for treating city wastewater, and it keeps sewage from polluting rivers and coasts.
