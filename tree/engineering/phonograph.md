Thomas Edison recorded and played back sound on a tinfoil-covered cylinder in 1877, and Emile Berliner's flat-disc gramophone of 1887 led to the mass-produced record. Recorded sound let music, speech and news be stored and copied, and the recording industry grew from these machines.
