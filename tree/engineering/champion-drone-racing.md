Swift, an autonomous racing drone built by Elia Kaufmann, Davide Scaramuzza and colleagues at the University of Zurich, won races against three world-champion human pilots and recorded the fastest lap, as reported in Nature in August 2023. It flew from its own onboard camera images with a control policy trained by reinforcement learning in simulation.
