Fervo Energy's Project Red in Nevada, tested in 2023, generated 3.5 megawatts from hot rock with no natural flow, using horizontal wells drilled with oil-field techniques and fractures created between them. Enhanced geothermal systems could extend geothermal power beyond volcanic regions.
