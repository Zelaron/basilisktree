Excavations from the Ubaid period in Mesopotamia, around 5000 BCE, give the earliest direct evidence of sailing boats, and Egyptian pottery shows sails by 3200 BCE. A sail turns wind into thrust, which freed boats from paddling and made long voyages and sea trade practical.
