NASA's unpiloted X-43A reached Mach 9.6 on 16 November 2004 under scramjet power, a speed record for an air-breathing aircraft, after the HyShot flights of 2002 had shown supersonic combustion in flight. A scramjet burns fuel in a supersonic airflow and so needs no turbine, and the concept is studied for hypersonic missiles and reusable launch vehicles.
