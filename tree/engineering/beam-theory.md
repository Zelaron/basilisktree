Leonhard Euler and Daniel Bernoulli worked out around 1750 how a beam bends under load, with its curvature proportional to the bending moment, building on Galileo's 1638 study of the strength of materials. Euler also found the load at which a slender column buckles. The theory let engineers calculate bridges, floors and machine parts instead of relying on rules of thumb.
