William Gascoigne, an English astronomer, built a screw micrometer for telescopes around 1640 to measure small angles between stars. Hand-held micrometers for machinists followed in the 19th century, and by turning a fine screw thread into a length reading they made measurement to a thousandth of an inch routine in workshops.
