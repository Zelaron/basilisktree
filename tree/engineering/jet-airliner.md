The de Havilland Comet entered service with BOAC in May 1952 as the first jet airliner. Metal fatigue around its square windows caused two fatal crashes in 1954, which taught the industry to test airframes for fatigue and design for crack growth. The Boeing 707 of 1958 made jets the standard for long-distance travel.
