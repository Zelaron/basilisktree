NASA's Sojourner, a small six-wheeled rover carried by the Mars Pathfinder lander, drove on Mars in July 1997. The larger rovers Spirit and Opportunity (2004) and Curiosity (2012) followed, and Opportunity covered 45 kilometres over fourteen years. Perseverance (2021) has been collecting rock samples for return to Earth.
