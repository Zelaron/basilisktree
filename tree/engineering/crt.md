Karl Ferdinand Braun built a cathode-ray tube with a fluorescent screen in 1897, in which a beam of electrons steered by magnetic fields draws a trace. The Braun tube became the oscilloscope display, the radar screen, the television picture tube and the monitor of early computers, until flat panels replaced it in the 2000s.
