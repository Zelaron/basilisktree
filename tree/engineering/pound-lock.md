Qiao Weiyue, a Song dynasty transport official, built a pound lock on China's Grand Canal in 984: a chamber between two gates that lifts or lowers boats between stretches of canal at different levels. Mitre gates, in which two angled leaves meet in a V, came in 15th-century Italy and let canals climb hills with little loss of water.
