Paul MacCready's Gossamer Condor, pedalled by Bryan Allen, flew a figure-eight course on 23 August 1977 to win the first Kremer prize, and the Gossamer Albatross crossed the English Channel on 12 June 1979. Their ultralight structures led to solar-powered and high-altitude aircraft, and AeroVelo's Atlas won the Sikorsky human-powered helicopter prize in 2013.
