Paul Lauterbur showed in 1973 that magnetic field gradients could encode the position of nuclear spin signals and so form images, and Peter Mansfield developed fast imaging methods. Raymond Damadian's team scanned the first human body in 1977. MRI shows soft tissue in fine detail without ionizing radiation, and Lauterbur and Mansfield shared the 2003 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.
