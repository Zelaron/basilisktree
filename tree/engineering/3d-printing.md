Chuck Hull filed the patent for stereolithography in 1984, building solid objects layer by layer from liquid resin hardened by ultraviolet light, after Hideo Kodama's 1981 paper on the same idea. Carl Deckard's selective laser sintering (1986) and Scott Crump's fused deposition modelling (1989) followed. Additive manufacturing is now used for prototypes, tools, implants and aerospace parts.
