Joseph Swan in Britain and Thomas Edison in the United States made durable carbon-filament lamps in an evacuated glass bulb in 1878 to 1880, and Edison's bamboo-filament lamp lasted more than a thousand hours. Cheap, safe electric light created the demand for power stations and grids and replaced gas and oil lamps in homes and streets.
