Karl Benz patented his three-wheeled Patent-Motorwagen, powered by a one-cylinder petrol engine, in January 1886, and Bertha Benz drove one 106 kilometres in 1888 to show it was practical. Gottlieb Daimler and Wilhelm Maybach built a four-wheeled motor carriage in 1886 as well. Motor vehicles have reshaped cities and trade, and over a billion are now on the road.
