Harold Black conceived the negative-feedback amplifier on the Lackawanna Ferry in New York on 2 August 1927 and developed it at Bell Labs. Feeding part of the output back with opposite sign gives up some gain in exchange for stable, low-distortion amplification, and it made long-distance telephone repeaters work.
