NASA's Mariner 2, built at the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, flew past Venus on 14 December 1962, the first successful mission to another planet. Space probes have since visited every planet, and Voyager 1 entered interstellar space in 2012. Probes have supplied most of what is known about the Solar System's planets and moons.
