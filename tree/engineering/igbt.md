Jayant Baliga at General Electric demonstrated the insulated-gate bipolar transistor in 1982, a switch that combines the easy voltage control of a MOSFET with the high current capacity of a bipolar transistor. IGBTs switch the power in electric cars, trains, wind turbines, solar inverters and air conditioners.
