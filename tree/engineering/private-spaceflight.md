Burt Rutan's Scaled Composites, funded by Paul Allen, flew SpaceShipOne above 100 kilometres in June 2004, the first crewed spaceflight by a privately funded vehicle, and won the 10 million dollar Ansari X Prize, announced in 1996, with two flights in five days ending on 4 October 2004. It started the commercial suborbital flight industry.
