George Zames posed the problem of designing feedback that performs well for a whole family of plant models in 1981, using the H-infinity norm to measure sensitivity. John Doyle, Keith Glover and colleagues gave an efficient state-space solution in 1988 and 1989. Robust control gives guarantees against model error and is used in aircraft, spacecraft and industrial systems.
