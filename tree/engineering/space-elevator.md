Build a cable from the equator to beyond geostationary orbit, so that climbers can reach orbit on electric power without rockets (Artsutanov, 1960, after Tsiolkovsky's 1895 tower). An Earth elevator needs a tether with a breaking length of about 3,100 to 8,200 km, far beyond any material made at length; carbon nanotubes come close only in short pieces. Solving it means such a tether at scale and a working elevator.
