Randall Smith and Peter Cheeseman showed in 1986 how a robot could estimate the positions of map features and its own position together, treating their errors as correlated, and Hugh Durrant-Whyte and colleagues made the idea into practical algorithms. SLAM lets robots, drones and phones build a map while moving through unknown places.
