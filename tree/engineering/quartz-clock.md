Warren Marrison and Joseph Horton at Bell Laboratories built the first quartz clock in 1927, using the steady vibration of a quartz crystal as its timekeeper. Quartz clocks beat the best pendulum clocks, and quartz watches, first sold in 1969, later put that accuracy on nearly every wrist.
