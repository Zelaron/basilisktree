Soviet physicists Igor Tamm and Andrei Sakharov proposed the tokamak, a doughnut-shaped magnetic bottle with a current driven through the plasma, around 1950, and Lev Artsimovich's group ran the first machine, T-1, in 1958. T-3 reached a plasma temperature of about 10 million degrees in 1968, and the tokamak became the leading fusion design, from JET to ITER.
