Light microscopes were long thought limited by diffraction to about 200 nanometres, until Stefan Hell's STED microscopy (2000) and the single-molecule methods PALM, from Eric Betzig's group, and STORM, from Xiaowei Zhuang's (both 2006), produced sharper images. Hell, Betzig and William Moerner won the 2014 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
