A car that drives itself anywhere and in any conditions a competent human driver could, with no human fallback: Level 5 in SAE's J3016 taxonomy (2014). As of 2026 no such system exists; Waymo's driverless Level 4 robotaxis, limited to mapped areas of 10 US metros, gave 500,000 paid rides a week in June 2026. Solving it means unrestricted driverless operation with safety at least matching human drivers.
