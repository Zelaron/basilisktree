Can every resistor–capacitor two-port with a common terminal be realized as a series-parallel network? Sidney Darlington conjectured in 1955 that it can; no proof or counterexample is known, and the question belongs to the wider unsolved problem of efficient passive network synthesis. Solving it means a proof, or an RC two-port that provably needs a bridge network.
