James Hargreaves's spinning jenny (1764), Richard Arkwright's water frame (1769) and Samuel Crompton's mule (1779) multiplied the thread one worker could spin. Arkwright's water-powered Cromford Mill (1771) was an early factory, and mechanized cotton spinning was the leading industry of the early Industrial Revolution.
