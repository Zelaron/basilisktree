William Le Baron Jenney's ten-storey Home Insurance Building in Chicago, completed in 1885, is often cited as the first skyscraper, with a metal skeleton supporting its floors and walls. Together with the safety elevator, steel frames let buildings rise far higher, and the Burj Khalifa reached 828 metres in 2010.
