Which convex body of given length and width meets the least resistance in Newton's model, where particles of a rarefied medium bounce off its front surface, a limit relevant to hypersonic flight? Newton solved it in 1687 among bodies of revolution, but the optimum among all convex bodies is not rotationally symmetric (1996) and is still unknown. Solving it means the optimal shape with a proof.
