George Devol patented a programmable transfer arm in 1954, and with Joseph Engelberger he built the Unimate, which began lifting hot die-cast parts at a General Motors plant in New Jersey in 1961. Robot arms spread through car factories and electronics assembly, and millions are now in use worldwide.
