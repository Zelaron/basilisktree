Sun-dried bricks of mud mixed with straw were in use by about 9000 BCE, for example in the earliest houses of Jericho. From around 5000 to 4000 BCE builders began firing bricks to make them stronger and more durable. Brick let people build walls, houses and cities where stone was scarce.
