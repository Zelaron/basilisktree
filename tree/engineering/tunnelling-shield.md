Marc Brunel patented a tunnelling shield in 1818, an iron frame that supported the ground at the working face while miners dug, and used it from 1825 on the Thames Tunnel, the first tunnel under a navigable river. James Henry Greathead's circular shield of 1869 led to the tunnel boring machines that now dig subway, sewer and road tunnels in soft ground.
