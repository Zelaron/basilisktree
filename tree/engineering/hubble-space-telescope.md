Lyman Spitzer argued in 1946 for a telescope in space, and NASA and ESA launched the Hubble Space Telescope on 24 April 1990. Astronauts corrected a flaw in its mirror in 1993, and it went on to make deep images and measure the age and expansion of the universe. The James Webb Space Telescope, with a folding 6.5 metre mirror, followed in 2021.
