A robot that can enter an unfamiliar home and do the chores, from laundry to cooking, safely and without supervision; Steve Wozniak's coffee test asks it to find the machine, the coffee and a mug and brew a cup. Robots now make coffee in demonstrations and clean kitchens in homes they have never seen (π0.5, 2025), but none does general household work reliably. Solving it means robust unsupervised performance across ordinary homes.
