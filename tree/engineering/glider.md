George Cayley built the first glider to carry a person around 1853, and Otto Lilienthal made some two thousand controlled glides in Germany between 1891 and 1896, dying after a crash in 1896. Lilienthal's measurements of curved wings and his photographs of flights showed that heavier-than-air flight was possible, and they inspired the Wright brothers.
