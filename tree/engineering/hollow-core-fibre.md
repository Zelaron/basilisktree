In September 2025 researchers at the University of Southampton and Microsoft Azure Fiber reported in Nature Photonics a hollow-core fibre, which guides light through air, with a loss of 0.091 decibels per kilometre. That is below the roughly 0.14 dB/km of the best solid glass fibre, and light travels faster in air than in glass, which cuts delay.
