Hidetoshi Katori proposed in 2001 trapping atoms in an optical lattice at a magic wavelength, where the trapping light does not shift the clock transition, and strontium lattice clocks from his group followed in 2003 and 2005. Optical clocks now keep time far better than the caesium clocks that define the second, and are expected to lead to a new definition of it.
