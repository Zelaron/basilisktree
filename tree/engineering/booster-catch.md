On 13 October 2024, on the fifth flight test of Starship, SpaceX caught the 70 metre Super Heavy booster in mid-air with two arms on its launch tower in Texas. Catching the booster is meant to remove the need for landing legs and shorten turnaround, a step towards rapid reuse of both stages.
