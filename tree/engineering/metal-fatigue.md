August Wöhler tested railway axles under repeated loading in the 1850s and 1860s and showed that steel can break at stresses far below its static strength if the load is cycled, and that below a certain stress it can last indefinitely. His stress-against-life curve, now called the S-N curve, is the basis of fatigue design for axles, aircraft and bridges.
