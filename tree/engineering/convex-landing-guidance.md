Behçet Açıkmeşe and Scott Ploen showed in 2007 that the fuel-optimal powered-descent problem for a lander can be turned into a convex optimization problem without loss of optimality, which flight computers can solve quickly. JPL flew the method on Masten Space Systems' Xombie rocket in 2012 and 2013, and similar guidance is used to land reusable rocket boosters.
