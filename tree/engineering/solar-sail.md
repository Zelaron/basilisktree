JAXA's IKAROS, launched in May 2010, was the first spacecraft to demonstrate solar sailing in interplanetary space, using a 14 metre square membrane pushed by sunlight on its way past Venus. The Planetary Society's LightSail 2 raised its orbit around Earth by light pressure alone in 2019.
