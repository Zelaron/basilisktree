Uno Lamm of ASEA led the building of the first commercial HVDC link, a 98 km undersea cable from the Swedish mainland to Gotland that opened in 1954 with mercury-arc valves. Direct current loses less over very long distances and can join grids that are not synchronized, and thyristor and IGBT converters now let HVDC lines carry gigawatts for thousands of kilometres.
