Jason Lohn, Gregory Hornby and Derek Linden at NASA Ames used a genetic algorithm to design antennas for the three Space Technology 5 satellites, launched in March 2006. Each satellite carried an evolved antenna of unusual bent-wire shape beside a conventional helix antenna, and it was reported as the first computer-evolved hardware to fly in space.
