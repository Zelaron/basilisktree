IBM's Simon of 1994 combined a phone with a touchscreen, e-mail and apps, and Apple's iPhone, announced in January 2007, joined a multi-touch screen, a camera, a web browser and mobile data in one device. Smartphones now put a networked computer, camera and GPS receiver in billions of pockets and changed how people communicate, shop, navigate and work.
