Denmark built the first offshore wind farm at Vindeby in 1991, eleven 450 kilowatt turbines in shallow water off Lolland. Offshore turbines can be larger and meet steadier wind than onshore ones, and floating designs such as Hywind Scotland (2017) opened deep water to wind power.
