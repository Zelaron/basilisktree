John Philip Holland's submarine, launched in 1897 and bought by the US Navy in 1900 as USS Holland, was one of the first practical ones: it ran on a petrol engine at the surface and on batteries underwater, and could dive and be steered while submerged. Submarines changed naval warfare in the First World War and later carried nuclear missiles.
