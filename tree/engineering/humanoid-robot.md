Ichiro Kato's group at Waseda University completed WABOT-1 around 1972, the first full-scale humanoid robot, which walked on two legs, gripped objects with its hands and spoke Japanese. Honda's ASIMO (2000) and later machines such as Boston Dynamics' Atlas walk, run and carry loads, and humanoid robots are now being developed as general-purpose workers.
