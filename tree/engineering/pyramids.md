Imhotep is credited with the Step Pyramid of Djoser at Saqqara, built around 2670 BCE as one of the first large buildings of cut stone. The Great Pyramid of Giza, finished about 2560 BCE, stayed the tallest structure on Earth for roughly 3,800 years, and the pyramids show what organized labour and surveying could achieve in the third millennium BCE.
