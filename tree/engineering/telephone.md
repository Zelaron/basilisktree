Alexander Graham Bell was granted the first US telephone patent in March 1876, after filing on the same day as Elisha Gray's caveat for a similar device, and soon spoke to his assistant Thomas Watson over a wire. Telephone networks became the first worldwide switched communication system, and Almon Strowger's automatic exchange of 1891 removed the operator.
