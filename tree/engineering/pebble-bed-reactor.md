China's HTR-PM at Shidaowan in Shandong, built by Tsinghua University, China Huaneng and others, began commercial operation in December 2023 and is described as the first Generation IV reactor in commercial service. Its fuel is tennis-ball-sized graphite pebbles holding thousands of ceramic-coated uranium particles, and its design lets decay heat leave passively after a loss of cooling.
