Jonas Degrave, Federico Felici and colleagues at Google DeepMind and EPFL's Swiss Plasma Center trained a deep reinforcement learning controller in simulation that shaped the plasma in the TCV tokamak, reported in Nature in February 2022. In 2024 a reinforcement learning controller predicted and avoided tearing instabilities in plasmas at the DIII-D tokamak.
