Lawrence Sperry demonstrated a gyroscopic stabilizer built by his father Elmer Sperry in 1912, and in 1914 flew a biplane past judges near Paris with his hands raised while the device held it level. Gyroscopic autopilots later flew aircraft over long distances, and computer-driven systems now fly and land airliners.
