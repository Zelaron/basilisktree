Jay Lathrop and James Nall at the US Army's Diamond Ordnance Fuze Laboratories used photographic methods in 1957 to print transistor patterns, and Jean Hoerni's planar process at Fairchild (1959) formed transistors under a protective oxide layer. Photolithography prints the circuits on every chip with light, and its steady improvement is what shrank transistors for sixty years.
