Harry Nyquist published his stability criterion in 1932 while working on feedback amplifiers at Bell Labs, a test that decides the stability of a feedback loop from a plot of its frequency response. Hendrik Bode's gain and phase methods of the late 1930s and 1940s made it a design tool, and frequency-domain methods became the standard way to design feedback systems.
