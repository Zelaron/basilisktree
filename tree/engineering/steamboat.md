Robert Fulton's North River Steamboat, later known as Clermont, began regular passenger service between New York and Albany in 1807, the first commercially successful steamboat service. Earlier steamboats, such as Jouffroy's Pyroscaphe on the Saône in 1783, had worked but did not pay. Steamboats opened American rivers to upstream traffic and shortened journeys.
