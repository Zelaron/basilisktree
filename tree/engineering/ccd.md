Willard Boyle and George Smith sketched the charge-coupled device at Bell Labs in 1969, a chip that moves packets of electric charge along rows of tiny capacitors and so can read out an image. CCD sensors became the eyes of digital cameras, camcorders and space telescopes, and the pair shared the 2009 Nobel Prize in Physics.
