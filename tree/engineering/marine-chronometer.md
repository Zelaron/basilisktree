John Harrison's fourth sea clock, H4, lost only about five seconds during the 81-day voyage to Jamaica in 1761 and 1762, well inside the accuracy that the Longitude Act of 1714 demanded. A clock that keeps accurate home time lets a navigator find longitude at sea, and the chronometer made ocean navigation much safer.
