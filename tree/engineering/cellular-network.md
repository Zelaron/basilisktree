Bell Labs engineers, notably Douglas Ring in 1947, proposed cellular networks, in which a region is divided into cells that reuse frequencies. Martin Cooper of Motorola made the first handheld mobile phone call in 1973, and NTT opened the first commercial cellular network in Tokyo in 1979. There are now more mobile subscriptions than people.
