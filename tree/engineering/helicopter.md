Henrich Focke's Fw 61 of 1936 was the first helicopter that could fly under full control, and Igor Sikorsky's VS-300 of 1939 set the single main rotor and tail rotor layout that most helicopters have used since. Helicopters can hover and land almost anywhere, which made them vital for rescue, medical evacuation, offshore work and military transport.
