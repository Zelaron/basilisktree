Nicolas Minorsky analysed the automatic steering of ships in 1922 and showed that a controller acting on the error, on its accumulated sum and on its rate of change could hold a course. Proportional, integral and derivative terms are simple to tune, and PID loops still run the large majority of industrial control systems, from thermostats to chemical plants.
