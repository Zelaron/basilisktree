On 6 May 1953 John Gibbon used his heart-lung machine in Philadelphia to take over the work of an eighteen-year-old woman's heart and lungs for 26 minutes while he closed a hole in her heart. It made open-heart surgery possible, and bypass machines are now used in hundreds of thousands of operations a year.
