Otto von Guericke, mayor of Magdeburg, built an air pump in 1650 and in 1654 showed that teams of horses could not pull apart two evacuated copper hemispheres pressed together by the air. Vacuum pumps opened experiments on air pressure and the vacuum, and later made light bulbs, electron tubes and chip fabrication workable.
