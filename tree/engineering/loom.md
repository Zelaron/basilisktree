A horizontal ground loom is painted on a Badarian dish from Egypt of about 4400 BCE, though woven cloth is thousands of years older. The loom holds warp threads under tension so the weft can be passed through them, and weaving became one of the most widespread crafts and, in the 18th century, the first major industry to be mechanized.
