John Logie Baird demonstrated mechanically scanned television with moving images in London in 1926, and Philo Farnsworth transmitted the first all-electronic picture in 1927, while Vladimir Zworykin developed an electronic camera tube. Regular broadcasting began in the 1930s, and by the late twentieth century television was a leading source of news and entertainment.
