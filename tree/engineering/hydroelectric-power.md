The Vulcan Street Plant at Appleton, Wisconsin, began generating electricity from falling water in September 1882. Hydroelectric stations spread quickly, with Niagara Falls sending alternating current to Buffalo in 1896 and pumped storage plants appearing in the 1890s. Hydropower remains the largest renewable source of electricity.
