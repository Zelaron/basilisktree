Galileo Ferraris in Italy and Nikola Tesla in the United States independently described rotating magnetic fields and the induction motor in 1888. Alternating current in fixed coils produces a field that drags a simple rotor around, so the motor needs no brushes. Induction motors are rugged and cheap, and they power most of the world's industrial machinery.
