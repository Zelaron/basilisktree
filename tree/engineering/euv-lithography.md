Hiroo Kinoshita proposed extreme ultraviolet projection lithography in 1986, and after three decades of work by ASML and its partners, EUV scanners that use 13.5 nanometre light from a tin plasma entered volume chip production in 2019. The machines, which cost more than 150 million dollars each, print the finest features on the most advanced processors.
