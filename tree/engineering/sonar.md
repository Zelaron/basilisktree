Paul Langevin and Constantin Chilowsky used a quartz crystal transducer to send and receive ultrasonic pulses under water, and between 1915 and 1918 detected objects such as submarines from their echoes. Sonar became essential for naval warfare, fishing and mapping the sea floor, and the same pulse-echo idea led to ultrasonic testing and medical ultrasound.
