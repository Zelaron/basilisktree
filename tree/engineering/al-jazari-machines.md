Ismail al-Jazari's Book of Knowledge of Ingenious Mechanical Devices (1206) described about fifty machines, among them water-raising pumps driven by cranks, camshafts, segmental gears and programmable automata. It is an illustrated manual that records the state of medieval mechanical engineering.
