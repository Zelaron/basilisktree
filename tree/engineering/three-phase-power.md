Mikhail Dolivo-Dobrovolsky developed practical three-phase generators and motors at AEG in 1888 and 1889, and in 1891 a three-phase line sent power about 175 km from Lauffen to Frankfurt, in a demonstration organized by Oskar von Miller. Three-phase current gives smooth, constant power to motors and needs less wire than single-phase, and today's grids all use it.
