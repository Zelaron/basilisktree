The Pesse canoe, a hollowed pine log found in the Netherlands, was made around 8000 BCE and is the oldest surviving boat. People had crossed open sea to reach Australia more than 50,000 years earlier, so boats are far older than any remains. Watercraft opened coasts, rivers and islands to settlement and trade.
