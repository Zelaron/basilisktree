Find the lightest fully stressed truss that carries three balanced forces applied anywhere in the plane, the basic unsolved case of Michell's 1904 theory of optimal frame structures. Exact solutions are known only for special configurations, such as two of the forces being perpendicular (Sokół and Lewiński, 2016). Solving it means the optimal layout and weight for every position of the three forces.
