Wilhelm Röntgen announced X-rays in December 1895, and within weeks doctors were using them to find fractures, bullets and swallowed objects. For the first time a living body could be seen inside without surgery. Radiography became routine in hospitals and led to fluoroscopy, mammography and CT.
