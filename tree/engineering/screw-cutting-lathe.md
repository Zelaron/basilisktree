Henry Maudslay built an all-metal lathe around 1800 with a slide rest and a lead screw that cut screw threads accurately and repeatably. Accurate screws allowed precise measuring instruments and parts that fit, and Maudslay's workshop trained the engineers who built the first machine tools.
