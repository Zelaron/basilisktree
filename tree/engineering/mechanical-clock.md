Weight-driven clocks regulated by a verge and foliot escapement appeared in European church and town towers around 1300. Su Song's water-driven astronomical clock tower of the 1090s in China had used an earlier escapement. Mechanical clocks made time a measured quantity in towns and began a tradition of precision instrument making.
