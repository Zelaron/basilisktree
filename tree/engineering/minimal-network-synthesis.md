What is the smallest network of resistors, inductors and capacitors that realizes a given impedance without transformers? Bott and Duffin showed in 1949 that every positive-real impedance can be built this way, but their networks are large and minimal ones are unknown even for most biquadratic impedances. Solving it means a method that finds and proves minimal realizations, which since the inerter (2002) also guides suspension design.
