Grad argued in 1967 that smooth plasma equilibria in a torus, with nested pressure surfaces and non-constant pressure, must have a symmetry such as axisymmetry; stellarators, whose fields have none, rely on such equilibria. In September 2026 two independent preprints posted counterexamples, one with a Lean 4 certification and one with explicit formulas found using an AI model. Neither is peer reviewed yet.
