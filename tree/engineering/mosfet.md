Mohamed Atalla and Dawon Kahng at Bell Labs made the first working MOSFET in 1959, a transistor in which a voltage on an insulated gate controls a channel of current. It could be shrunk far more easily than earlier transistors and is the building block of microprocessors and memory chips; more than 10^22 have been made, more than any other manufactured object.
