Robert William Thomson patented an air-filled tyre in 1845, and John Boyd Dunlop reinvented it and patented it for bicycles in 1888. The air cushion absorbs bumps and lowers rolling resistance, and pneumatic tyres made bicycles and later road vehicles faster and more comfortable.
