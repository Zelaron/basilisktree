NASA's DART spacecraft struck the asteroid Dimorphos at about 6 kilometres a second on 26 September 2022 and shortened its orbit around Didymos by more than half an hour, the first measured change to the motion of a celestial body by a human-made object. The test showed that a kinetic impactor can deflect an asteroid, a method of planetary defence.
