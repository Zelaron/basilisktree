The Obninsk plant near Moscow, a 5 megawatt reactor, fed electricity into a grid in June 1954, and Hyman Rickover's navy programme produced the pressurized-water reactors of USS Nautilus (1955) and the Shippingport plant (1957). Nuclear plants now supply roughly a tenth of the world's electricity with very low carbon emissions.
