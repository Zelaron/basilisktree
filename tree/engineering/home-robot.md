iRobot, founded by Rodney Brooks, Colin Angle and Helen Greiner, launched the Roomba robot vacuum cleaner in September 2002. It was the first home robot to sell in large numbers, and iRobot has sold tens of millions of them, which brought autonomous navigation into ordinary homes.
