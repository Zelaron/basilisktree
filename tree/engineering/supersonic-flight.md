Chuck Yeager flew the rocket-powered Bell X-1 faster than sound at Mach 1.06 over California on 14 October 1947, showing that aircraft could pass through the sharp rise in drag near the speed of sound. Supersonic flight became routine for military aircraft, and data from the X-1 programme fed the design of later fast aircraft.
