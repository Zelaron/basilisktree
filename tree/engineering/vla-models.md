Google DeepMind's RT-2, announced in July 2023, fine-tuned a vision-language model to output robot actions as text tokens, so a robot could follow instructions involving objects and concepts from web data. Physical Intelligence's π0 (2024) and Google DeepMind's Gemini Robotics (2025) extended the approach to policies that work across many tasks and robot bodies.
