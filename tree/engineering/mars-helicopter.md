NASA's Ingenuity, a 1.8 kilogram helicopter carried to Mars by the Perseverance rover and led at JPL by MiMi Aung, made the first powered, controlled flight on another planet on 19 April 2021, in air about one percent as dense as Earth's. It flew 72 times before it was damaged in January 2024, and showed that aircraft can scout Mars.
