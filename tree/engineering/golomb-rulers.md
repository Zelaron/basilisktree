Place n marks on a ruler so that no two pairs of marks are the same distance apart, using the shortest possible ruler; such rulers pick interference-free radio channels and sparse antenna arrays (Babcock, 1953). Optimal rulers are proven only up to 28 marks, the last (length 585) by distributed.net volunteers in November 2022. Solving the next case means proving which 29-mark ruler is shortest.
