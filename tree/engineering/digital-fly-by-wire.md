NASA's modified F-8 Crusader made the first flight of an aircraft with a fully digital fly-by-wire system on 25 May 1972, using an Apollo guidance computer and no mechanical backup. The Airbus A320 of 1987 brought digital fly-by-wire to airliners, where computers now stabilize the aircraft and keep it within safe limits.
