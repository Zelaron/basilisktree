Alim-Louis Benabid and Pierre Pollak in Grenoble reported in 1987 that stimulating the thalamus with high-frequency pulses from an implanted electrode stopped tremor, and the method spread to Parkinson's disease and dystonia. In 2025 the US approved a version that senses the patient's brain signals and adapts its stimulation.
