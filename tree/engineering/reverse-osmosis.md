Sidney Loeb and Srinivasa Sourirajan at UCLA made a cellulose acetate membrane in 1960 with a thin dense skin on a porous layer, which passed water at practical rates when salt water was pushed against it under pressure. Reverse osmosis became the main desalination method and now produces most of the world's desalinated water.
