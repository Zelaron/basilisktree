The Tōkaidō Shinkansen between Tokyo and Osaka opened on 1 October 1964, running at 210 km/h on dedicated standard-gauge track under chief engineer Hideo Shima of Japanese National Railways. High-speed rail competes with air travel on routes of a few hundred kilometres and has spread to China, Europe and elsewhere, with networks now running at 300 km/h or more.
