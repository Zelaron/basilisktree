Isambard Kingdom Brunel's SS Great Britain, launched in 1843, was the first large ocean-going ship to combine an iron hull with a screw propeller, and in 1845 the first iron steamer to cross the Atlantic. The propeller had been patented in 1836 by Francis Pettit Smith and John Ericsson, and iron hulls and propellers replaced wooden paddle steamers.
