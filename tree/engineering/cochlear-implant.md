André Djourno and Charles Eyriès in Paris stimulated a deaf patient's auditory nerve with an implanted coil in 1957. Graeme Clark's multichannel implant, first used in Melbourne in 1978, and a parallel design by Ingeborg Hochmair in Austria let patients understand speech, and implants have since restored hearing to hundreds of thousands of people.
