Stanford's Stanley, led by Sebastian Thrun, drove 212 kilometres of desert track without a driver to win the DARPA Grand Challenge and its 2 million dollar prize on 8 October 2005; in the 2004 race no vehicle got past 12 kilometres. The contest brought researchers together, and many team members went on to lead self-driving car projects.
