Horace Babcock proposed in 1953 correcting atmospheric blur by measuring the distorted wavefront and reshaping a mirror in real time. The first astronomical system, COME-ON, was tested in France in 1989 and moved to ESO's 3.6 metre telescope in Chile. Adaptive optics now lets large ground telescopes rival space telescopes in sharpness at some wavelengths.
