Werner von Siemens showed a small electric railway at the Berlin trade fair in 1879, and Frank Sprague's streetcar system in Richmond, Virginia, which opened in 1888, was the first large and successful electric tramway. Electric traction led to streetcars and then subways, which were cleaner and quicker than horses or steam in cities.
