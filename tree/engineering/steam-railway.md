The Stockton and Darlington Railway (1825) was the first public line to use steam locomotives, and the Liverpool and Manchester Railway (1830), whose engine Rocket had won the Rainhill Trials in 1829, ran all its trains by steam between two cities. Designs by George and Robert Stephenson set the pattern, and railways cut journey times and reshaped trade and cities.
