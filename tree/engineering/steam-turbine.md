Charles Parsons patented a multistage steam turbine in 1884 and coupled it to a dynamo, and his turbine-driven Turbinia dashed past the Royal Navy fleet at about 34 knots in 1897. Steam turbines drive most of the world's electric generators and many ships, because they run smoothly at high speed with far better efficiency than piston engines.
