Thomas Telford's Menai Suspension Bridge (1826) hung a 176 metre span from wrought-iron chains, the longest in the world when it opened. The Brooklyn Bridge (1883), designed by John A. Roebling, used spun steel-wire cables and set the modern form, and suspension bridges now hold the span records, above two kilometres.
