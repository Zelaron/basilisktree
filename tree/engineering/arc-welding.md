Nikolay Benardos and Stanisław Olszewski demonstrated carbon-arc welding in 1881, using an electric arc between a carbon rod and the work to melt and join metal, and patented it in the 1880s. Arc welding with metal electrodes soon followed, and welded steel later built ships, pipelines and skyscrapers.
