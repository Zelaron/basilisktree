Robert Jarvik's Jarvik-7, implanted in the dentist Barney Clark by William DeVries in Utah in December 1982, was meant as a permanent artificial heart, and Clark lived 112 days. Continuous-flow pumps now support thousands of patients with heart failure, and a magnetically levitated total artificial heart, BiVACOR, was first implanted in 2024.
