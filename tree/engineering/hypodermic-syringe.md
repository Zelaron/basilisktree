In 1853 Alexander Wood in Edinburgh and Charles Pravaz in Lyon each devised a syringe with a hollow needle for injecting liquids under the skin, and Wood used his to give morphine. Hypodermic injection gave a fast, dosed route for drugs and later underpinned vaccination, anaesthesia and insulin treatment.
