Massimo Banzi, David Cuartielles, Tom Igoe, Gianluca Martino and David Mellis made the first Arduino board at the Interaction Design Institute Ivrea in 2005 as a cheap, open-source microcontroller for design students. Its simple programming environment and open hardware let artists, students and hobbyists build working electronics, and it became a standard tool of the maker movement.
