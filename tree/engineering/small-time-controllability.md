Find a test, in terms of the Lie brackets of its vector fields at an equilibrium, for when a nonlinear control system can reach every nearby state in arbitrarily short time. Exact criteria exist for linear and driftless systems, but with drift Sussmann's conditions (1983, 1987) are only sufficient and the full list of good and bad brackets is unknown. Solving it means a necessary and sufficient criterion, even just for scalar-input systems.
