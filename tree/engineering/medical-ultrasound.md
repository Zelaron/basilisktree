Ian Donald, John MacVicar and the engineer Tom Brown published pulse-echo ultrasound scans of abdominal masses and of the fetus in The Lancet in June 1958, using a machine adapted from an industrial flaw detector. Ultrasound images soft tissue without ionizing radiation, and it is routine in prenatal care.
