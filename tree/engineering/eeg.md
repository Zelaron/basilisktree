Hans Berger, a German psychiatrist, recorded the first electrical activity from a human brain through the scalp in 1924 and published it in 1929, describing the alpha rhythm. EEG gives a millisecond record of brain activity without surgery, and it is used to diagnose epilepsy and sleep disorders and underlies non-invasive brain-computer interfaces.
