Do smooth magnetic equilibria exist that are exactly quasisymmetric throughout a volume, confining particles as well as a tokamak without being axisymmetric? Garren and Boozer's 1991 near-axis expansion suggests the equations are overdetermined away from the magnetic axis, yet optimized fields reach quasisymmetry to remarkable precision (Landreman and Paul, 2022). Solving it means an exact construction or a proof of impossibility.
