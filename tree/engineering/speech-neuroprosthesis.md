Two papers in Nature in August 2023 showed implants decoding attempted speech from paralysed people at 62 words a minute, by Francis Willett, Jaimie Henderson and colleagues at Stanford, and 78 words a minute with a digital avatar, by Edward Chang's team at UCSF. In 2024 a UC Davis team including Sergey Stavisky and David Brandman reached 97.5 percent accuracy with a man with ALS.
