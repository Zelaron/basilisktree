Rune Elmqvist built, and Åke Senning implanted, the first pacemaker in Arne Larsson at the Karolinska hospital in Stockholm on 8 October 1958. Larsson outlived both men and received 26 devices. Pacemakers, shrunk by transistors and long-life batteries such as Wilson Greatbatch's of 1960, now keep millions of hearts in rhythm.
