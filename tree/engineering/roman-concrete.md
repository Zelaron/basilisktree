From about 200 BCE Roman builders mixed lime mortar with volcanic ash and rubble into a concrete that sets even under water. It made harbours, bridges and large vaults possible, and the Pantheon's dome, completed around 125 CE and 43 metres across, is still the largest unreinforced concrete dome in the world.
