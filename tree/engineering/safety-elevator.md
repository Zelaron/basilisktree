Elisha Otis invented a safety brake for hoists in 1852 and demonstrated it at the New York Crystal Palace in 1854, cutting the rope of a platform he stood on and letting a ratchet catch the fall. With the fear of falling removed, passenger elevators spread from 1857, and they made tall buildings practical to use.
