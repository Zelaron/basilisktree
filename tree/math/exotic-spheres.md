Milnor (1956) found a smooth 7-manifold that is homeomorphic to the 7-sphere yet has a different smooth structure. Kervaire and Milnor later showed that the 7-sphere has exactly 28 smooth structures (1963). The discovery showed that topology and smooth structure can differ.
