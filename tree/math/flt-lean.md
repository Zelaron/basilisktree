On 4 September 2026 Anthropic announced a complete Lean 4 proof of Fermat's Last Theorem, written in about eleven days by Claude agents directed by Tianyi Peng and reviewed by Kevin Buzzard. At about 13 million lines, it follows Wiles's proof as simplified by Darmon, Diamond and Taylor and adapts parts of Buzzard's Imperial College FLT project.
