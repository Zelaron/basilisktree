Cardano's Ars Magna (1545) published the solution of the cubic by radicals, found by del Ferro (c. 1515) and rediscovered by Tartaglia (1535), together with Ferrari's solution of the quartic. The general quintic then resisted until Abel and Galois showed in the 1820s and 1830s that no such formula exists.
