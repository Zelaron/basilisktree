A convex body has Rupert's property if a copy of it can pass through a straight hole cut in the body itself. Steininger and Yurkevich (2025) constructed the Noperthedron, a polyhedron with 90 vertices that lacks the property, disproving a conjecture from 2017. The proof is computer-assisted.
