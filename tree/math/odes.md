Equations relating a function to its derivatives. Newton classified first-order equations in 1671, Leibniz separated variables (1691), and Jacob Bernoulli posed the equation that bears his name (1695), solved by Leibniz and Johann Bernoulli. Newton's laws, circuits and population models are all written in them.
