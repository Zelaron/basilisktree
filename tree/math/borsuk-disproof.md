Borsuk conjectured in 1933 that every bounded set in n-dimensional space can be split into n + 1 pieces of smaller diameter. Kahn and Kalai (1993) showed that this fails in high dimensions, and Bondarenko (2013) showed that it fails for every n ≥ 65.
