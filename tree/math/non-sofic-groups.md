Sofic groups, introduced by Gromov in 1999, are groups approximable by finite permutation groups, and it was unknown whether every group is sofic. On 1 August 2026 OpenAI announced a construction of a non-sofic group, produced by an internal model with a Lean formalization. It has not yet been independently reviewed.
