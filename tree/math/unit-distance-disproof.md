Erdős conjectured in 1946 that n points in the plane determine at most n^{1+ε} unit distances for every ε > 0. In May 2026 an OpenAI model constructed point sets with n^{1+c} unit distances for an absolute constant c > 0, using class field towers; Will Sawin's follow-up gave an explicit exponent above 1.014.
