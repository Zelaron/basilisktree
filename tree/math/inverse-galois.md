Is every finite group the Galois group of some finite extension of the rational numbers? Hilbert (1892) and Noether (1918) laid the groundwork, and all symmetric, alternating and solvable groups and, since 2026, all 26 sporadic simple groups are known to occur. The general problem is open, and explicit polynomials for particular groups are checkable targets.
