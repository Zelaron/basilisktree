A set in three-dimensional space containing a unit line segment in every direction has Hausdorff and Minkowski dimension 3 (Hong Wang and Joshua Zahl, February 2025). It settles the Kakeya set conjecture in dimension 3; Wang received the 2026 Fields Medal, in large part for this work.
