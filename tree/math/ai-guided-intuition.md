A DeepMind team with the mathematicians Juhász and Lackenby (knot theory) and Williamson (representation theory) used neural networks to find patterns in data. They found a new relation between two families of knot invariants and a candidate approach to the combinatorial invariance conjecture for Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials (Nature, December 2021).
