Stack two copies of a graph and join them by vertical edges. Kasteleyn's 1985 bunkbed conjecture said that after random edge deletion a vertex is at least as likely to stay connected to another vertex on its level as to that vertex's copy on the other level. Gladkov, Pak and Zimin disproved it in 2024 with a 7,222-vertex counterexample based on Hollom's work.
