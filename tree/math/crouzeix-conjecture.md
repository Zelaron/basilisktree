Crouzeix conjectured in 2004 that ‖p(A)‖ ≤ 2 max|p| over the numerical range of A, for every square matrix A and polynomial p; the best general constant had been 1 + √2. Shanmu Jin posted a proof, obtained with GPT-5.6 Sol, on 27 July 2026. Crouzeix, Greenbaum and Townsend reviewed it, and Lorist and Schwenninger gave a second proof on 4 August.
