For coprime positive integers with a + b = c, the conjecture (Oesterlé and Masser, 1985) says that for every ε > 0 only finitely many triples have c > rad(abc)^(1+ε), where rad(abc) is the product of the distinct primes dividing abc. It implies many other Diophantine results, such as Roth's theorem and Faltings's theorem. Mochizuki's claimed proof (2012) is not accepted by most experts.
