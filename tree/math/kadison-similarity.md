Richard Kadison asked in 1955 whether every bounded homomorphism from a C*-algebra into the operators on a Hilbert space is similar to a *-homomorphism. It is a central problem of operator algebras, solved for cyclic representations by Uffe Haagerup in 1983 and shown equivalent to the derivation problem. OpenAI’s October 2026 release claims a proof, formalized in Lean but not yet independently reviewed.
