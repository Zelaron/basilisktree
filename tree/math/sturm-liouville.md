Boundary-value problems for second-order equations have a discrete sequence of eigenvalues, and their orthogonal eigenfunctions expand arbitrary functions (Sturm and Liouville, 1836 to 1837). The theory generalizes Fourier series and is the template for the discrete spectra of quantum mechanics.
