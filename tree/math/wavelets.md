Wavelets are localized oscillating functions that analyze signals at many scales. Morlet and Grossmann introduced them in the early 1980s, Meyer and Mallat (multiresolution analysis, 1989) built the theory, and Daubechies's compactly supported wavelets (1988) made them practical. They power JPEG 2000 and many signal-processing methods.
