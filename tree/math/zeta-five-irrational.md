Is ζ(5) = 1 + 1/2⁵ + 1/3⁵ + … irrational? Apéry proved ζ(3) irrational in 1978, and later work showed that infinitely many odd zeta values are irrational, including at least one of ζ(5), ζ(7), ζ(9), ζ(11) (Zudilin, 2001). A September 2026 preprint by Aabir Fauzan claims a proof for ζ(5), with a Lean formalization reported complete.
