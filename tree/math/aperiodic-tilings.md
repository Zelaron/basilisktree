Wang asked in 1961 whether tilings by square tiles can be decided algorithmically. Berger (1966) proved they cannot, using a set of 20,426 tiles that tile the plane only aperiodically. Penrose found aperiodic sets of just two shapes in the 1970s, and quasicrystals were discovered in 1982.
