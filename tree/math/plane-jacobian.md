If a polynomial map of the plane to itself has constant nonzero Jacobian determinant, must it have a polynomial inverse? Ludwig Kraus stated the question in 1884 and Keller posed it in n variables in 1939. Since a counterexample in three variables appeared in July 2026 ([[jacobian-3d]]), the plane is the only open case; it is verified for polynomials of degree up to 104.
