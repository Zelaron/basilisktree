Types are read as spaces and equalities as paths. Voevodsky's univalence axiom (2009) and the collaborative HoTT book (2013) proposed a foundation for mathematics suited to computer proof assistants. The book came out of a special year at the Institute for Advanced Study (2012 to 2013).
