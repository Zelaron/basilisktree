Hirsch conjectured in 1957 that a polytope with n facets in d dimensions has diameter at most n − d. Santos (2010) constructed a counterexample in dimension 43 with 86 facets. Whether the diameter is polynomial in n and d, the polynomial Hirsch conjecture, remains open.
