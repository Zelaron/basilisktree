Yau proved in 1977 to 1978 Calabi's 1954 conjecture: every compact Kähler manifold with vanishing first Chern class carries a Ricci-flat metric. Calabi–Yau manifolds provide the extra dimensions of string theory, and Yau received the Fields Medal in 1982.
