The unit conjecture predicts that the group ring of a torsion-free group over a field has only trivial units. Gardam (2021) found a nontrivial unit in the group ring over the field with two elements of a crystallographic group, with the help of a SAT solver, refuting a conjecture attributed to Higman (1940) and Kaplansky.
