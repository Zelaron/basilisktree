Smale proved the Poincaré conjecture for spheres of dimension 5 and higher (1961) through the h-cobordism theorem, and Stallings and Zeeman gave other proofs. The higher-dimensional case was thus settled decades before the 4-dimensional (Freedman, 1982) and 3-dimensional (Perelman, 2003) cases.
