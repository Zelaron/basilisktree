Cantor conjectured in 1878 that no set is larger than the integers and smaller than the real numbers. Gödel showed in 1940 that the continuum hypothesis cannot be disproved from the ZFC axioms, and Cohen showed in 1963, with his method of forcing, that it cannot be proved. It was the first of Hilbert's problems of 1900.
