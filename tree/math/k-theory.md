Grothendieck's K-group of vector bundles (1957), Bott's periodicity theorem (1957 to 1959) and Atiyah and Hirzebruch's topological K-theory (1959) turned vector bundles into algebra. K-theory became a tool in topology, operator algebras and number theory.
