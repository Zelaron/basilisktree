Napier's Mirifici Logarithmorum Canonis Descriptio (1614) introduced logarithms, which turn multiplication into addition. Briggs's base-10 tables (1617) and the slide rule (Oughtred, 1622) were the main tools of calculation in science and engineering for about 350 years.
