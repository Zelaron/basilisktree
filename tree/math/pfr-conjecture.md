If a set A in F₂ⁿ has |A + A| ≤ K|A|, then A is covered by polynomially many (in K) cosets of a subgroup of size at most |A|. This polynomial Freiman–Ruzsa conjecture (Marton) was proved by Gowers, Green, Manners and Tao in November 2023 with entropy methods, and formalized in Lean within three weeks.
