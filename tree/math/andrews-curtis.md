Andrews and Curtis conjectured in 1965 that every balanced presentation of the trivial group, with as many relators as generators, can be reduced to the trivial presentation by Nielsen moves and conjugations. The conjecture is tied to questions in 4-dimensional topology and is widely believed to be false. The shortest potential counterexample is the Akbulut–Kirby presentation AK(3), which no computer search has trivialized.
