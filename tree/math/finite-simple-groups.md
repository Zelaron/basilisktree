Every finite simple group is cyclic, alternating, of Lie type, or one of 26 sporadic groups. The proof, by hundreds of mathematicians, runs to tens of thousands of pages; it was declared complete in 1983, and a gap in the quasithin case was closed by Aschbacher and Smith in 2004.
