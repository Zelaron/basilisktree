Kadison and Singer asked in 1959 whether every pure state on the diagonal matrices extends uniquely to all bounded operators on ℓ². The question is equivalent to problems in signal processing and discrepancy theory. Marcus, Spielman and Srivastava solved it in 2013 using interlacing polynomials.
