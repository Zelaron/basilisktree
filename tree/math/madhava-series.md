Madhava (c. 1340 to 1425) and the Kerala school found infinite series for sine, cosine and arctangent, including π/4 = 1 − 1/3 + 1/5 − …, centuries before Gregory (1671), Newton and Leibniz. Their results survive in later works such as Nilakantha's Tantrasamgraha (1501).
