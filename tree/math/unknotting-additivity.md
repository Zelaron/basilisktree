The unknotting number is the minimum number of crossing changes that turn a knot into the unknot. Brittenham and Hermiller (2025) found knots whose connected sum has a smaller unknotting number than the sum of the two, answering a question implicit since Wendt (1937). The knot 7₁ joined to its mirror image needs at most 5 changes, although each summand needs 3.
