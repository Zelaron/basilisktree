In a right triangle the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares on the other two sides. Babylonian tablets of about 1800 BCE already use the relation; the Greek tradition credits Pythagoras (c. 530 BCE) with a proof, and Euclid's Elements I.47 gives the classic one.
