In March 2023 the amateur David Smith, with Craig Kaplan, Joseph Samuel Myers and Chaim Goodman-Strauss, showed that a 13-sided tile called the hat tiles the plane only aperiodically (reflections allowed). Their 'spectre' tile (May 2023) does so without reflections, answering the einstein problem of a single aperiodic tile.
