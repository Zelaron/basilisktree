Diophantus's Arithmetica (c. 250 CE) poses problems asking for rational or integer solutions of polynomial equations and uses symbolic shorthand for unknowns. Fermat's famous marginal note of the 1630s was written in a copy of Bachet's 1621 edition of the book.
