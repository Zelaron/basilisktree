Lie developed the theory of continuous groups of symmetries, first for differential equations in the 1870s and fully in his Theorie der Transformationsgruppen (1888 to 1893). Lie groups and their Lie algebras are the language of rotations, special relativity and the gauge symmetries of particle physics.
