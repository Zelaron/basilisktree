MIP* = RE (Ji, Natarajan, Vidick, Wright and Yuen, 2020) showed that interactive proofs with entangled provers can verify the halting problem. Together with earlier work it refutes Connes's 1976 embedding conjecture on von Neumann algebras and Tsirelson's problem in quantum information.
