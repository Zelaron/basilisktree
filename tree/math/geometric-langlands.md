In May 2024 Dennis Gaitsgory, Sam Raskin and seven coauthors announced a proof of the unramified categorical geometric Langlands conjecture over the complex numbers, in five papers of more than 1,000 pages. It is a geometric counterpart of the Langlands correspondence, for sheaves on curves.
