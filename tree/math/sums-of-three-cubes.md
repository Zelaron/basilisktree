Which integers are sums of three cubes of integers, positive or negative? Numbers congruent to ±4 mod 9 never are, and Heath-Brown conjectured (1992) that all others are, in infinitely many ways. After 33 and 42 fell to large computer searches in 2019, the unsolved cases below 1000 are 114, 390, 627, 633, 732, 921 and 975.
