Is every smooth 4-manifold homotopy equivalent to the 4-sphere diffeomorphic to it? Freedman (1982) proved the topological version, and in dimension 4 the smooth and piecewise-linear versions are equivalent. Candidate exotic 4-spheres, such as the Cappell–Shaneson spheres, have repeatedly turned out to be standard.
