Divide a domain into small elements and approximate the solution of a PDE by simple functions on each. Courant described the idea in 1943, Turner, Clough and colleagues developed it for aircraft structures in 1956, and Zienkiewicz's textbook (1967) spread it. It is the standard tool of structural and fluid simulation.
