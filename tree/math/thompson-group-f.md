Is Thompson's group F, the group of piecewise-linear homeomorphisms of the unit interval with dyadic breakpoints and slopes that are powers of 2, amenable? Geoghegan popularized the question in 1979, and F has no free subgroups (Brin and Squier, 1985), the usual obstruction to amenability. Earlier claimed proofs have failed; OpenAI's October 2026 claim of nonamenability, formalized in Lean, awaits review.
