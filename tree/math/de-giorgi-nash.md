Solutions of elliptic and parabolic equations with merely bounded measurable coefficients are Hölder continuous (De Giorgi, 1957; Nash, 1958; Moser's Harnack inequality, 1960). The theorem solved Hilbert's nineteenth problem on the regularity of solutions of variational problems.
