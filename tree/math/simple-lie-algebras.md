Killing (1888 to 1890) and Cartan (thesis, 1894) classified the simple Lie algebras: four infinite families and five exceptional ones, including the 248-dimensional E₈. The classification is the basis of the root systems and Dynkin diagrams used throughout geometry and particle physics.
