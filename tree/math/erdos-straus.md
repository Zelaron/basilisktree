Can 4/n be written as 1/x + 1/y + 1/z with positive integers x, y, z for every n ≥ 2? Erdős and Straus conjectured so in 1948. It suffices to treat prime n, the possible exceptions have density zero (Vaughan, 1970), and computers have checked every n up to 10¹⁸ (2025).
