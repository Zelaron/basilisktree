For a one-to-one analytic function f on the unit disk with f(0) = 0 and f′(0) = 1, the n-th Taylor coefficient satisfies |aₙ| ≤ n. Bieberbach conjectured it in 1916, and de Branges proved it in 1984 to 1985 (Acta Mathematica 1985) with inequalities for special functions.
