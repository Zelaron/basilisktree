Does the 6-sphere admit a complex structure? It carries an almost complex structure from the octonions, and among spheres only S² and S⁶ admit almost complex structures (Borel and Serre, 1953), but whether S⁶ has an integrable one, a question named after Heinz Hopf, has resisted attempts since the 1940s. An August 2026 construction by Levent Alpöge, made with an internal Claude model, is under review.
