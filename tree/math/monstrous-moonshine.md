McKay noticed in 1978 that 196884 = 196883 + 1, linking a coefficient of the j-function to the smallest representation of the Monster group. Conway and Norton (1979) conjectured a systematic connection, and Borcherds proved it in 1992 using vertex algebras, work for which he received the Fields Medal in 1998.
