A nowhere-zero k-flow orients every edge of a graph and gives it a value from 1 to k − 1 so that inflow equals outflow at each vertex. Tutte conjectured in 1954 that every bridgeless graph has a nowhere-zero 5-flow; for planar graphs this is the dual of the five colour theorem. The Petersen graph shows that 5 would be best possible, and Seymour proved that 6 suffices (1981).
