The Turing degrees order sets of natural numbers by relative computability. Hartley Rogers asked in 1967 whether this order has any symmetry other than the identity, and Theodore Slaman and Hugh Woodin showed that rigidity is equivalent to their bi-interpretability conjecture. OpenAI’s October 2026 release claims every automorphism is the identity, formalized in Lean but not yet independently reviewed.
