Kakeya asked in 1917 for the smallest area in which a needle can be turned through a full circle. Besicovitch showed in 1928 that there is no minimum: regions of arbitrarily small area work. His sets containing a segment in every direction (1920) led to the Kakeya conjecture on their dimension.
