Highly symmetric complex functions on the upper half-plane. Klein and Poincaré studied them in the 1880s, Ramanujan's τ-function (1916) revealed their arithmetic, and Hecke's operators (1937) gave the theory its structure. They link partitions, elliptic curves and sphere packing, and were central to the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem.
