Find the function that makes an integral as small or as large as possible. It began with Johann Bernoulli's brachistochrone problem (1696) and acquired its general equation from Lagrange's 1755 letter to Euler, now the Euler–Lagrange equation. It is the basis of Lagrangian mechanics and least-action principles.
