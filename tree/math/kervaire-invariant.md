Framed manifolds with Kervaire invariant one can exist only in dimensions 2^j − 2, and do exist in dimensions 2, 6, 14, 30 and 62. Hill, Hopkins and Ravenel (2009) ruled out all dimensions from 254 up, and Lin, Wang and Xu (preprint, 2024) constructed one in dimension 126, the last open case.
