Karmarkar (1984) gave a polynomial-time algorithm for linear programming that is also fast in practice, moving through the interior of the feasible region; Khachiyan's ellipsoid method (1979) had first shown polynomial time. Nesterov and Nemirovski (1994) extended the approach to convex optimization.
