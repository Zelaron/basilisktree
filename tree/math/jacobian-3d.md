Keller's 1939 Jacobian conjecture says that a polynomial map from ℂⁿ to itself with constant nonzero Jacobian determinant is invertible. On 19 July 2026 Levent Alpöge posted a degree-7 counterexample in three variables, found with Claude Fable 5; its Jacobian is −2 and it maps three points to one. The case n = 2 remains open.
