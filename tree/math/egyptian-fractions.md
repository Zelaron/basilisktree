The Rhind papyrus (c. 1550 BCE, copied by the scribe Ahmes from an older text) writes fractions as sums of distinct unit fractions and tabulates 2/n for odd n up to 101. Such decompositions are still studied, for example in the Erdős–Straus conjecture of 1948.
