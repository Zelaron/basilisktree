No positive integers satisfy aⁿ + bⁿ = cⁿ for n > 2. Wiles proved it in 1995, with Taylor's help on a gap, through the modularity of semistable elliptic curves, 358 years after Fermat's note. A Lean formalization led by Kevin Buzzard is under way.
