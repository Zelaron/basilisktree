Menaechmus found the curves cut from a cone by a plane (c. 350 BCE), and Apollonius's Conics (c. 200 BCE) developed their geometry and named the ellipse, parabola and hyperbola. Kepler's elliptical orbits (1609) and Newton's gravitation rest on it.
