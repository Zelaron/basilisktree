Aristotle's Prior Analytics (c. 350 BCE) gave the first formal system of valid inference, the syllogism: from 'all A are B' and 'all B are C' conclude 'all A are C'. It was the standard logic of Europe and the Islamic world for about two thousand years, until Boole and Frege.
