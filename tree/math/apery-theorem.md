ζ(3) = 1 + 1/8 + 1/27 + … is irrational. Apéry announced the proof in 1978 with an unmotivated recurrence for rational approximations, and van der Poorten explained it in 'A proof that Euler missed' (1979). Rivoal (2000) showed that infinitely many odd zeta values are irrational.
