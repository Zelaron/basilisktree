The integers 1 to 7824 can be coloured red and blue with no monochromatic Pythagorean triple a² + b² = c², but 1 to 7825 cannot (Heule, Kullmann and Marek, 2016). The SAT-solver proof is 200 terabytes long, the largest mathematical proof at the time.
