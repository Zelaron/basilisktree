Math, Inc.'s Gauss agent completed a Lean formalization of the strong prime number theorem, a challenge set by Terence Tao and Alex Kontorovich in January 2024, in about three weeks (September 2025). In 2026 it helped complete Lean formalizations of Viazovska's sphere-packing proofs in dimensions 8 and 24.
