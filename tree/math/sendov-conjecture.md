If all roots of a polynomial of degree n ≥ 2 lie in the closed unit disk, each root has a critical point within distance 1 (Sendov, 1959). Tao proved it for large degree in 2020. Lech Mazur posted a Lean-verified proof for all degrees, developed with GPT-5.6 Pro, on 5 August 2026, and Tao published a digest with a shorter Lean formalization on 12 August.
