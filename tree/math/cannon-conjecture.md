James Cannon conjectured in the early 1990s that every hyperbolic group whose boundary at infinity is a 2-sphere acts geometrically on hyperbolic 3-space. A proof would make each such torsion-free group the fundamental group of a closed hyperbolic 3-manifold, a central goal of geometric group theory. OpenAI’s October 2026 release claims a proof, formalized in Lean but not yet independently reviewed.
