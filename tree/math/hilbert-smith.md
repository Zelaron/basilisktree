The Hilbert–Smith conjecture says that a locally compact group acting faithfully on a connected manifold must be a Lie group. It grew out of Hilbert's fifth problem (1900) and Paul Smith's 1941 work, and was known only up to dimension three (John Pardon, 2013). OpenAI’s October 2026 release claims a proof in every dimension; it is not formalized and not yet independently reviewed.
