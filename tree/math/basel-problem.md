Euler showed that 1 + 1/4 + 1/9 + … = π²/6 (1734), solving a problem posed by Mengoli in 1650 that had defeated Jacob Bernoulli and others. His comparison of the sine function with its infinite product led on to the zeta function and its values at even integers.
