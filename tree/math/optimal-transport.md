Monge posed the problem of moving a pile of earth to fill a hole at least cost (1781). Kantorovich's relaxation and duality (1942) made it tractable, and he shared the 1975 Nobel Prize in economics for related work. Wasserstein distances are now used in PDE, statistics and machine learning.
