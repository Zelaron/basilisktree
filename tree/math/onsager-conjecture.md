Onsager predicted in 1949 that Euler flows with Hölder exponent above 1/3 conserve energy and that rougher ones can dissipate it. Isett (2018) constructed energy-dissipating solutions at every exponent below 1/3 by convex integration, and Buckmaster, De Lellis, Székelyhidi and Vicol (2019) refined the result.
