Golod and Shafarevich (1964) proved an inequality that yields infinite class field towers, and, with Golod's construction, infinite finitely generated torsion groups, answering the general Burnside problem. The same criterion supplied the number fields in the 2026 disproof of Erdős's unit distance conjecture.
