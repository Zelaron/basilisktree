A system of PDEs with analytic coefficients and analytic initial data has a unique analytic solution near the data surface. Cauchy proved special cases (1842), and Kovalevskaya proved the general theorem in her 1874 thesis, published in 1875. It is the first general existence theorem for PDEs.
