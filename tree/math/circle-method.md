Hardy and Ramanujan found an asymptotic formula for the number of partitions of an integer (1918). Hardy and Littlewood turned the idea into a general method for Waring's problem and Goldbach-type questions, and Vinogradov used it to show that every large odd number is a sum of three primes (1937).
