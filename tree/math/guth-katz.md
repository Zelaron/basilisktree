n points in the plane determine at least about n / log n distinct distances (Guth and Katz, preprint 2010), nearly matching the n / √(log n) bound that grid sets achieve and settling Erdős's 1946 question up to a root of a logarithm. The proof combines the polynomial method with the geometry of lines in space.
