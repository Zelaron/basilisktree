Is the Mandelbrot set locally connected? Douady and Hubbard showed in the 1980s that a yes would give a complete combinatorial model of the set and would imply that hyperbolic maps are dense in the quadratic family. Yoccoz proved local connectivity at every parameter that is not infinitely renormalizable; the infinitely renormalizable parameters are the remaining obstacle.
