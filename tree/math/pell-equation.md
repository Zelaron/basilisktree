The equation x² − Ny² = 1 has infinitely many integer solutions for every non-square N. Brahmagupta's composition rule (628) and the chakravala method, completed by Bhāskara II (1150), solved it long before Fermat's challenge of 1657; Lagrange proved that solutions always exist (1768).
