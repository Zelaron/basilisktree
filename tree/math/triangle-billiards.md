Does every triangular billiard table have a periodic path? Acute triangles have one (Fagnano's orbit), as do right triangles, triangles whose angles are rational multiples of π, and, by computer search, obtuse triangles with largest angle up to 112.4°. In June 2026 Giovanni Forni posted a claimed proof that every polygon has a periodic billiard orbit.
