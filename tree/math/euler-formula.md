The identity e^{ix} = cos x + i sin x, presented in Euler's Introductio in analysin infinitorum (1748), links the exponential function to trigonometry; at x = π it gives e^{iπ} + 1 = 0. It underlies complex analysis, Fourier analysis and the phasor methods of electrical engineering.
