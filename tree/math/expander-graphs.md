Sparse graphs that are nonetheless highly connected. Pinsker (1973) proved by counting that they exist, Margulis (1973) gave the first explicit construction, and Lubotzky–Phillips–Sarnak and Margulis built optimal 'Ramanujan graphs' (1988). They are used in error-correcting codes, derandomization and network design.
