Köthe asked in 1930 whether a ring with no nonzero nil ideal also has no nonzero nil one-sided ideal. In September 2026 a pre-release OpenAI model, GPT-6 Astra, run by Tom Adamczewski of Epoch AI, found a counterexample with a Lean 4 proof. Böhmler and Marczinzik wrote it up, and Greenfeld, King and Vendramin gave a second construction.
