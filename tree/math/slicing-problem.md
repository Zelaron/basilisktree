Bourgain asked in 1986 whether every convex body of volume 1 in n dimensions has a hyperplane section of (n − 1)-dimensional volume bounded below by a universal constant. Klartag and Lehec answered yes in December 2024, using a bound of Qingyang Guan and the method of stochastic localization.
