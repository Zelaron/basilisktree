A graph on n vertices with no complete subgraph on r + 1 vertices has at most (1 − 1/r)n²/2 edges, and the extremal graphs are complete r-partite (Turán, 1941; Mantel had proved the case r = 2 in 1907). It founded extremal graph theory.
