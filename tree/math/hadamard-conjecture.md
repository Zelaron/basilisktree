A Hadamard matrix is a square matrix of entries ±1 whose rows are mutually orthogonal; its order must be 1, 2 or a multiple of 4. The conjecture, usually credited to Paley (1933), says every multiple of 4 is the order of one. In August 2026 constructions were announced for all twelve orders below 2000 that were still open, so the smallest open order now lies above 2000.
