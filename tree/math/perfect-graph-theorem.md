A graph is perfect, meaning that in every induced subgraph the chromatic number equals the clique number, exactly when it has no odd hole and no odd antihole. Berge conjectured this in 1961; Chudnovsky, Robertson, Seymour and Thomas announced a proof in 2002 (published 2006).
