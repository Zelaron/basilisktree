A lattice in 24 dimensions in which each sphere touches 196,560 others, found by Leech in 1965 (published 1967). Its symmetry group led Conway in 1968 to three new sporadic simple groups, and the lattice gives the densest sphere packing in 24 dimensions (proved in 2016).
