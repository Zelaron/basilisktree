For a compact Riemann surface the theorem computes the dimension of the space of meromorphic functions with prescribed poles from the genus and the degree of the pole divisor. Riemann proved an inequality (1857) and Roch completed it (1865); Hirzebruch and Grothendieck generalized it in the 1950s.
