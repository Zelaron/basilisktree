Sums of many small independent effects are approximately bell-shaped. De Moivre found the normal approximation to the binomial (1733), Laplace stated the general theorem (1810), and Lyapunov (1901) and Lindeberg (1922) gave rigorous conditions. It is the reason the normal distribution appears throughout statistics.
