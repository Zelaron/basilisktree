The triangle of binomial coefficients appears in Indian, Persian and Chinese works: al-Karaji (c. 1000), Jia Xian (c. 1050), Yang Hui (1261). Pascal's treatise (written 1654, published 1665) proved its properties by induction, and Newton extended the binomial theorem to arbitrary exponents (c. 1665).
