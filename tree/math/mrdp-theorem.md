No algorithm can decide whether a polynomial equation with integer coefficients has an integer solution, so Hilbert's tenth problem (1900) has a negative answer. Davis, Putnam and Julia Robinson reduced it in 1961, and Matiyasevich completed the proof in 1970 using Fibonacci numbers.
