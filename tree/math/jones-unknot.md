Does the Jones polynomial (1984) detect the unknot, that is, does every nontrivial knot have a Jones polynomial different from that of the unknot? Computer checks cover all prime knots with up to 24 crossings, nontrivial links with trivial Jones polynomial exist, and its refinement Khovanov homology does detect the unknot (Kronheimer and Mrowka, 2011).
