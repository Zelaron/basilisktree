Runners with distinct constant speeds circle a track of length 1. The conjecture, posed by Jörg Wills in 1967 and independently by Thomas Cusick, says that with n runners each one is at some moment at distance at least 1/n from all the others. Computer-assisted proofs in 2025–2026 extended the known cases from 7 runners to 13.
