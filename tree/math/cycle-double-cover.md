Every bridgeless graph has a family of cycles that covers each edge exactly twice (Szekeres, 1973; Seymour, 1979). OpenAI announced a three-page proof by GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra on 10 July 2026 and released a Lean formalization a week later; Sang-il Oum wrote an exposition of the argument.
