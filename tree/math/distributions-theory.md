Generalized functions such as the Dirac delta can be differentiated any number of times. Sobolev introduced weak derivatives and the spaces that bear his name in the 1930s, and Schwartz's theory of distributions (1945 to 1950) made the idea systematic and won him the 1950 Fields Medal. It is the standard framework for linear PDEs.
