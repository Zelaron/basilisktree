Kirkman (1847) studied arrangements of points in triples so that every pair lies in exactly one triple, later called Steiner triple systems, and posed the schoolgirl problem (1850): walk fifteen girls in rows of three for seven days with no pair repeated. Designs are used in experiment planning, coding theory and finite geometry.
