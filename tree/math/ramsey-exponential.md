The diagonal Ramsey number satisfies R(k, k) ≤ (4 − ε)^k for a fixed ε > 0 (Campos, Griffiths, Morris and Sahasrabudhe, 2023), the first exponential improvement on the 4^k bound of Erdős and Szekeres (1935). Gupta, Ndiaye, Norin and Wei lowered the base to 3.8.
