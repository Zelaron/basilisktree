Khovanov (1999) categorified the Jones polynomial: his homology theory of links has the polynomial as its Euler characteristic. It detects the unknot (Kronheimer and Mrowka, 2011) and gave Rasmussen a combinatorial proof of the Milnor conjecture on slice genus (2004).
