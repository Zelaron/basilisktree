Two symmetric convex sets are positively correlated under any centered Gaussian measure. Conjectured between the 1950s and the 1970s, it was proved in 2014 by the retired statistician Thomas Royen, in the Far East Journal of Theoretical Statistics; an exposition by Latała and Matlak (2017) brought it wide attention.
