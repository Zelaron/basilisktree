Elgindi (2019) proved that solutions of the 3D Euler equations with C^{1,α} velocity can blow up in finite time. Chen and Hou (2022 to 2023) proved finite-time blow-up from smooth data for the axisymmetric Euler equations in a cylinder, with a computer-assisted proof.
